# Task: Skyrim SE grass on exterior land
Design doc section: `docs/design/skyrim-se-implementation-plan.md`, milestone M-S2 (exteriors: terrain and vegetation); decision 0016 (neutral world model)
Goal: the grass that Skyrim SE's ground textures name grows on the neutral exterior land, placed by the game pack and drawn through the engine's groundcover path, behind `[Game] neutral grass` (off by default).
Inherited code touched (at `4029a06b87`): `apps/openmw/mwrender/groundcover.cpp` (`buildChunk` split into `buildGroundcoverChunk`, the constructor's override into `makeGroundcoverContext`, both moved unchanged), `groundcoverinstances.{hpp,cpp}` (one overload of `groundcoverCellsInRange` with a cell size), `apps/openmw/mwrender/skyrimmodel.{hpp,cpp}` (`BuildContext::mOverride`, applied in `buildShape`), `rendermanager.{hpp,cpp}` (`useNeutralTerrain`, `updateSceneChildren`, the update loop).
Inputs / outputs: new SDK header `sdk/include/openeden/game/grass_source.hpp` (DRAFT 0.1, found by `dynamic_cast` on the registered `WorldCellSource`, like `CellWaterSource`); new pack files `game_packs/skyrimse/{include/game_skyrimse,src}/grass.*`; new engine files `apps/openmw/mwrender/{neutralgrass,groundcoverbuild}.*`; new setting `[Game] neutral grass`.
Constraints: deterministic placement (seeded from world, cell, kind and square), density capped per cell, no per-frame allocation in `NeutralGrass::update`, pack called on the main thread only, nothing changes with the setting off or with Morrowind.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] GRAS decoded with hostile-input handling (wrong size counted, non-finite floats refused, cut subrecord a located error)
- [x] Grass of a grid square placed from the ground textures' GNAM lists, the dominant texture at each point, slope, water and a per-cell cap
- [x] The grass drawn in the game on Whiterun's plains with the setting on, absent with it off
- [x] With the setting off, Morrowind and Skyrim are drawn as before (Balmora frame hash, `worldobjects_check`)
Out of scope: tuft colour variation, wave period, position range, leaning on slopes, grass under water drawn differently, grass collision or shadows, LOD grass, a Slang twin check of the groundcover path, saving.

## Facts from the data (five official masters, aggregates; `testskyrimsegrassreal.cpp`)

- **GRAS** (VERIFIED): 58 records over the five masters (31 after the load-order merge), every one with a 32-byte `DATA` and a `MODL`, 0 odd subrecords. `DATA` layout: density u8 (3 to 90), minimum slope u8 (0 in all), maximum slope u8 (10 or more), unused, units from water u16 (up to 390), unused u16, units from water type u32 (only 0, 50 records, and 2, 8 records), position range f32 (up to 68), height range f32 (up to 0.5), colour range f32 (up to 0.3), wave period f32 (0.0001 to 600), flags u8 (`0x06` in all 58), 3 unused. The field names and offsets are UESP's/xEdit's and upstream's `components/esm4/loadgras.cpp` (remote `openmw-upstream`; the same 32 bytes); the sizes and ranges above are what the data shows. What each flag bit and each water type MEANS is RECALLED-VERIFY: `0x01` vertex lighting, `0x02` uniform scaling, `0x04` fit to slope; type 0 "above at least", 2 "below at least" (the rule is written for types 0 to 7; an unseen type only keeps grass out of water).
- **LTEX names its grass** (VERIFIED): `GNAM` entries, one form id each (decoded since task 0117). After the merge 26 of 91 ground textures list grass: 46 entries, every one a GRAS, at most 3 on one texture.
- **Where grass grows is NOT in the data**: a GRAS says how, an LTEX says which, and the engine's own rule (the texture under the tuft) is the unknown. Choice made here (decision for toddhow): the tuft stands where the texture that DOMINATES the picture at that point names its grass. Dominance is the share each layer ends with when the quadrant's layers are blended in draw order (`dominantTexture`: base weight 1, each layer takes its bilinear opacity and the ones below keep the rest; the largest share wins, a tie goes to the layer on top). The share is a property of what is drawn, so grass and ground agree.
- **Density meaning is a guess**: tufts per 128 unit square = `density * 0.3` (named constant `GrassPlacementOptions::mTuftsPerDensityPoint`, about one tuft per 22 units at density 90). RECALLED-VERIFY against the real game by eye.

## Design

- Pack (`grass.*`, `SkyrimTerrainIndex::grassForCell`): the GRAS records join the terrain index's merge (later plugin wins, deleted removes) and each LTEX's grass list is resolved to load-order ids. `grassForCell(world, x, y)` takes the land of the world, or of a parent that lends its land (the walk the core does), reuses `buildTerrain`'s `TerrainCell` (heights, ordered layers) and the cell's water, and places tufts with `placeGrass` (pure): 32 x 32 squares of 128 units; per square the kinds of the textures that dominate at its corners and middle are candidates; per kind `density * 0.3` tufts (stochastic rounding) at seeded random points; a tuft is kept only where the dominant texture at its own point lists the kind, the square's slope is inside the GRAS slope range and the water rule allows; height is bilinear in the square's four corners; yaw random; scale `1 +- heightRange` clamped to 0.25 to 4. If a cell would exceed 30,000 tufts every density is scaled down together.
- SDK: `GrassSource::grassForCell` returns a `GrassCell` (model paths and instances: model index, world x, y, z, yaw, scale). Main thread only, deterministic.
- Engine (`NeutralGrass`): one chunk per grid square within `[Groundcover] rendering distance` (6144; `[Groundcover] enabled` is NOT needed), the squares found again after the camera moved an eighth of a square. The pack is asked on the main thread, ONE square per update, nearest first; the models are read and instanced on the object's own worker thread by `buildGroundcoverChunk` (the Groundcover chunk build, shared), then compiled and shown by a later update; built chunks are cached (48). Drawing is Groundcover's: instance arrays and the `INSTANCE` pipeline mode, wind and stomp in `files/shaders/lib/groundcover.glsl`. Skyrim shapes get the groundcover pipeline through the pipeline override of the model options, which `SkyrimModel`'s reader now honours (`BuildContext::mOverride`, applied just before the static shape's pipeline is made; null for every other model).

## Decisions and limits for toddhow

1. **Density constant** `0.3 tufts per density point per 128 x 128 square` is my estimate; the frames look dense and slightly bushy next to Skyrim's. Tune by eye (`GrassPlacementOptions`, not a setting yet).
2. **The rule "where it grows"** (dominant texture) is a choice; the real engine's rule is not known from the data.
3. **Not applied from the data**: the GRAS colour range, position range and wave period (the shader's own wind is used), fit to slope (all 58 records have the bit; tufts stand upright), vertex lighting.
4. **The distance** is `[Groundcover] rendering distance` (6144, shared with Morrowind's groundcover; Skyrim's own default fades further).
5. **One square a frame** is asked of the pack (worst 3 ms a frame while squares stream in); two a frame would halve the time to fill the view.
6. The one shared-code change that touches Morrowind's path: `Groundcover` now builds through `buildGroundcoverChunk` and `makeGroundcoverContext` (moved, not rewritten); Morrowind groundcover is off by default and was not run in a game here.

## Stage log: grass tone and density (follow-up; branch `skygrass2` from `main` at `591826b211`; own build tree `I:\Projects\OpenEden-work\skygrass2`, Windows only, RelWithDebInfo; same hand wrote tests and code, no fresh reviewer; nothing committed or pushed)

toddhow: "bright saturated orange, far brighter than the ground under it, and too dense and bushy". Frames: `I:\Projects\OpenEden-work\skygrass2\final_{0..4}.png` (off | before | after, same spots, 480 x 360 each) and `sheetd_2.png`, `sheetd_3.png` (densities 1.0, 0.5, 0.35, 0.2 of the first estimate, albedo 0.65).

**Cause of the brightness, with evidence (aggregates, installed Skyrim SE data).**
1. **The grass blade textures are about twice as bright as the ground textures under them, and some are inherently orange.** Over the 31 pairs of a ground texture (LTEX, its TXST diffuse) and a grass model its GNAM names (water kelp and coral excluded), the ground texture's mean luminance divided by the grass texture's opaque (alpha >= 128) luminance has median 0.51, mean 0.53 (range 0.36 to 0.84; for example `LTundra01`'s ground 34 against `TundraGrassObj04` 69, `LFieldGrass02`'s 55 against `FieldGrassObj01` 107, `LReachGrass01`'s 35 against 59; ground = mean luminance of all texels). `TundraGrassObj01.dds`, the texture of `TundraGrass01.nif` that `LFieldGrass01` (Whiterun's plains) names, is dark twigs with orange tips (opaque median RGB (132, 73, 8), luminance 89, viewed): the orange is the file, not a lighting fault.
2. **Nothing of ours adds light.** `OPENEDEN_NIFSCENE_TRACE` (now also prints thresholds, emission, mean vertex colour and mean normal z) for the six grass models in the frames: emissive 0,0,0 in all; vertex colours dim, mean 0.75 to 0.88 (`TundraGrassObj01` 0.78, 0.72, 0.59; `FieldGrass01` 1.0); the vertex alpha is not used (the flatten sets it to 1 without the Vertex_Alpha flag); alpha thresholds 50 to 100 (of 255); mean normal z -0.51 to +0.27 (not pointing up, so no sun boost). The drawn ground averages luminance 36 to 46 in the frames and the drawn grass pixels 90 to 100 (the masked pixels of the pair frames, biased up because the mask picks pixels that differ from the ground), against texture luminances of 34 to 94 (ground) and 66 to 131 (grass): consistent with the textures being the difference. I did NOT separate lighting from texture exactly (no unlit capture of the grass).
3. **Land colour tinting would not fix it.** Land `VCLR` of 60 cells (x 2..7, y -7..2) of Tamriel (`Skyrim.esm`): channel means 0.79 to 1.0 (most 0.9 to 1.0), so tinting by it dims at most 10 to 20 percent, nowhere near the factor of two; and it needs a per-instance colour input in the groundcover vertex stage (GLSL, its Slang twin, the recorded `.interface` golden data), shared with Morrowind's groundcover. **Not built**; the GRAS colour range (up to 0.3), which also needs a per-instance input, is not either.
4. **Density**: tufts are big (box of `TundraGrass01` 110 x 100 x 72 units, `FieldGrass01` 78 x 59 x 64, `TundraGrassObj03/04` 57 x 59 x 38) and the first estimate (0.3 tufts per density point per 128 unit square) put one tuft per 20 to 40 units: in frames a continuous carpet with no ground showing. This machine's `Skyrim_Default.ini`, `Skyrim\SkyrimPrefs.ini` and the four quality inis hold only fade distances (`fGrassStartFadeDistance` 7000 at High and Ultra, 2300 Medium, 1000 Low, `fGrassMaxStartFadeDistance` 7000), no grid spacing, so there is NO data on the machine for the real density; a recalled `iMinGrassSize=20` default would give about 0.41 tufts per point (denser, not thinner), so the choice below is by eye, RECALLED-VERIFY.
