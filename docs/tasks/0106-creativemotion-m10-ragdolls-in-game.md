Design doc section: CreativeMotion `CLAUDE.md`, "Path to OpenMW", M10; `docs/tasks/0035-creativemotion-m10-physics-adapter.md` (the adapter, the layer, `RagdollHost`, the scale fix: all built); `docs/tasks/0065-creativemotion-push-sandbox.md` (the editor's use of the same adapter); `docs/decisions/0001-creativemotion-compiled-into-the-engine.md`, `docs/decisions/0003-plugins-do-not-change-physics-or-lua.md`, `docs/decisions/0005` (no save data for extensions; CreativeMotion is core).
Status: written 2026-11-10 (toddhow said "Yes" to starting M10 on 2026-11-10). Full loop (anything under `apps/openmw/mwphysics/` and the CreativeMotion adapters). Commit at the time of writing: `846dbb8849`.
Goal: with `[Physics] creativemotion ragdolls = true`, a non-player humanoid NPC that dies becomes a CreativeMotion ragdoll in the game's single Jolt world, and the ragdoll's pose drives the NPC's skeleton each frame, so the corpse falls and settles on the world instead of playing the canned death clip's final pose.

## Design
- **One Jolt world, one adapter.** Actor ragdolls live in the same `RagdollHost` / `HostedJoltWorld` as the debug ragdoll. At most `cMaxActorRagdolls` (8) at once; a ninth death keeps its death animation (logged once at Verbose). Handles are `{index, generation}`; a stale handle fails every call.
- **Trigger.** In `CharacterController::postAnimation`, main thread: the actor is dead (`isDead()`), is an NPC (`mNpc`), is not the player (the player's death reloads the game), the controller saw it alive earlier in its life (a corpse loaded from a save is not ragdolled: it lies in the death clip's last pose, which is not a standing pose), a `PhysicsSystem` exists, the pelvis bone exists in the actor and at least ten of the 17 skeleton-profile bones can be driven (a rig bone with no body, like the neck in the embedded humanoid rig, or an engine bone the actor lacks, is skipped and keeps the animation's local rotation under its driven parent) (`components/creativemotion/data/profiles/humanoid-to-eden.skeletonprofile.json`; the table is repeated in the driver), and the setting is on.
- **Build.** The embedded humanoid rig at the actor's feet (`Ptr` position, which is at the feet) and yaw, limp (`makeLimp`), every body given the actor's inertial velocity (`Actor::getInertialForce`, units per second, converted at the host's named boundary). The rig is in its standing rest pose; the actor's bones are NOT posed into the ragdoll (see "Limits"). Layers: `RAGDOLL` meets `WORLD`, `HEIGHTMAP`, `DOOR`, `RAGDOLL` only, so a ragdoll never meets any actor capsule, projectile or sensor, and existing queries never see it.
- **Collision of the corpse's own character controller.** Unchanged: the dead actor's capsule is on `ACTOR` and, after the death clip, `DEBRIS` (`Actors::update` -> `World::enableActorCollision(false)` -> `Actor::enableCollisionBody`). Neither layer meets `RAGDOLL`, so nothing needs to be removed; the capsule keeps resting where the actor died and is still what picking, activation and looting use.
- **Driving the skeleton.** Each frame, after the animation, the manual bone rotations and the IK controller (all in `postAnimation`, main thread), the driver reads every ragdoll body (`readActorRagdoll`, no allocation) and writes the mapped bones that have both a body and an engine bone (16 with the embedded rig: it has no neck body) parent first, through `BonePose` only. For bone `i`: `world rotation = ragdollBoneRotation(t) * delta_i`, where `delta_i = inverse(ragdollBoneRotation(0)) * actorBoneWorldRotation(0)` was captured on the spawn frame (so the first frame draws exactly the pose the animation had: no pop), and the local rotation is `inverse(parentWorldRotation) * world rotation`. The pelvis bone's origin follows the ragdoll pelvis's displacement since the spawn (world, in units, written as a local translation under its parent). Unmapped bones (clavicles, fingers, toes, hair, weapons, `Bip01` itself) keep playing the death clip. The animation is not stopped, so its text keys, sounds and end-of-death handling are as before.
- **Units.** Metres only inside the adapter and the rig. `RagdollHost` converts at its two named functions (`toMetres` for the spawn pose and velocity, `fromMetres` for the bone readback); the driver only sees game units.
- **Threads.** `startActorRagdoll`, `readActorRagdoll`, `stopActorRagdoll`, `RagdollHost::beginStep/endStep` and the driver all run on the main thread (`World::update`, `CharacterController::postAnimation`). Spawn and removal take `PhysicsTaskScheduler::getSimulationMutex()` like `spawnDebugRagdoll`, because the actor job may be querying. The adapter registers no Jolt callback; nothing runs on a Jolt worker. Ragdoll bodies are outside every mask the actor job uses.
- **Allocation.** `beginStep`, `endStep`, `readActorRagdoll` and the driver's per-frame `apply` allocate nothing (preallocated state vectors; bone handles found once). Spawning allocates (once per death).
- **Determinism.** No clock, no randomness; the same call sequence on the same build gives the same bodies (adapter test, task 0035). Which frame the ragdoll starts on depends on the game, as the death does.
- **Lifetimes.** The ragdoll is removed when: the actor is removed from the physics system (`PhysicsSystem::remove`, disable, delete, cell unload), its controller rebuilds its bone controllers in a live game (`createPoseControllers`: new bones, so the driver ends with the ragdoll), it falls `cRagdollKillDepthMetres` below its spawn, or the physics system is destroyed. The controller's destructor and `detachAnimation` only drop the driver and never call into the physics system, because the world can already be gone then (a quickload clears the world first); the ragdoll leaves with its actor through `PhysicsSystem::remove`, which `Scene::removeObjectFromScene` and the cell unload always call, or with the host at shutdown. A driver whose handle is stale stops writing bones and is dropped; the bones then keep the last pose they were given until the animation writes them again.
- **Setting.** `[Physics] creativemotion ragdolls` (existing, false). With it false: no `RagdollHost` is created for actors, no Jolt body of ours exists, the death path executes one bool test per frame of a dead NPC, and frames and physics are identical to the parent executable (checked, below).
- **Saves.** Not saved. A ragdolled corpse's pose is not in the save (decision 0005). On load the actor is a corpse in the death clip's last pose (the clip's final frame, as without ragdolls). A save made while a ragdoll is still settling therefore shows the corpse in its death-clip pose after the load. The actor's `Ptr` position never changes (the capsule does not follow the ragdoll), so the saved position is where the actor died.

## Limits of this slice (stated, not hidden)
1. The ragdoll starts in the rig's standing rest pose, not in the actor's pose at the moment of death. The rotation binding hides the pop, but a limb that the animation had raised starts from "rest rotation plus the physics' change from rest". Posing the rig to the actor's pose (a swing per bone, joint limits re-centred) is the next slice.
2. Only the pelvis translates; limb lengths are the skeleton's, not the rig's. The rig's body sizes are the CreativeMotion humanoid's, not the actor's race (beast races and tall or short NPCs use the same rig).
3. The corpse's capsule, activation box and hit boxes stay where the actor died; a ragdoll that slides far (a slope) draws away from its loot hitbox. A ragdoll that leaves the visible bounds may be culled with the actor's node.
4. Creatures, the player, summoned creatures and actors with a missing bone get no ragdoll.
5. No settling hand-off: when a ragdoll sleeps the driver keeps writing its (constant) pose each frame; it is removed only by the lifetime events above.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] (a) `RagdollHost` hosts up to 8 actor ragdolls beside the debug ragdoll in one adapter; spawn / read / remove are allocation-free in read and step, stale handles fail, the ninth is refused with a message, removal leaves the Jolt body count as found (unit tests, `openmw-tests`).
- [ ] (a) With the setting off no `RagdollHost` exists for actors and `stepSimulation` runs the same two null tests (unit test of the null path through `PhysicsSystem` helpers or the existing hosting tests).
- [ ] (b) The rotation maths: at the spawn frame the driven bone rotation equals the actor's; a ragdoll rotation `Q` applied to the body gives `Q * actor rotation` in world space; local = inverse(parent) * world round-trips (`ragdollbonemath.hpp`, unit tests incl. random rotations).
- [ ] (b) `ActorRagdollDriver::apply` writes the mapped bones through `BonePose` from a fake ragdoll source: at spawn the bone world rotations are unchanged, a body turned by 90 degrees turns its bone by 90 degrees about the same world axis, unmapped bones are untouched, a stale source ends the driver, no allocation per frame.
- [ ] (b) A dead NPC with the setting on gets exactly one ragdoll; a corpse created dead gets none; the player and creatures get none; removal on `PhysicsSystem::remove` (tests on the pure decision helper and the PhysicsSystem-side map).
- [ ] (c) In a quiet-window game, an NPC killed by script falls and settles: frames over 3 s viewed (no explosion, no sinking), the lowest ragdoll body stays above the floor spawn height minus a tolerance, settle time and top speed logged; two runs with `OPENEDEN_FIXED_FRAME_TIME` log identical final poses where the death is repeatable.
- [ ] With the setting off, the same scene's frames are byte-identical to the parent executable's and the Jolt body count is equal.
Out of scope: posing the ragdoll to the actor, creatures, the player, balance and get-up (M11), partial ragdolls (hit reactions), saves of the pose, cell-unload hand-off to a frozen pose, activation hitboxes following the ragdoll, Lua access, extension access (decision 0003), Eden Studio.

## Constraints
Full loop. One Jolt world (the adapter takes `JPH::PhysicsSystem&`). No heap allocation in per-step and per-frame code. Layer rules stay at 104 exceptions or lower. `components/creativemotion/` is not edited. clang-format 14 and `-Werror` clean. Settings: no new setting; the existing one is already in `tools/testprofile/` (checked in the stage log).
