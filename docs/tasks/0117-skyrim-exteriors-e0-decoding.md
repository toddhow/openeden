# Task: Skyrim exteriors, stage E0, pack decoding
Design doc section: `docs/research/0053-skyrim-exteriors-neutral-model.md` sections 2, 8 (stage E0) and 10 (toddhow's "go with your recommendations" adopted for every recommendation).
Goal: the Skyrim SE pack decodes WRLD, exterior cell water, LAND, LTEX, TXST and WATR into its own plain types, and the real data settles the RECALLED-VERIFY items of note 0053.
Inherited code touched: none. `components/esm4/loadland.*` and `components/esmbridge/esmterrain.cpp` were read as references only (cited in `land.cpp`). `records.*` untouched.
Inputs / outputs: new `game_packs/skyrimse/include/game_skyrimse/land.hpp`, `worldspace.hpp`, `src/land.cpp`, `src/worldspace.cpp`, internal `src/exterior_common.hpp`; tests `apps/components_tests/skyrimse/testskyrimseland.cpp` and `testskyrimselandreal.cpp`; `tools/skyrimse_land_check/check.sh`.
Constraints: no engine change, no SDK change (E1 adds `world_terrain.hpp`); nothing throws; every size is checked against the bytes that remain and against a cap before allocation; errors are located (file, offset, record type, subrecord); off by default by construction (nothing calls the decoders).
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] LAND (DATA, VHGT, VNML, VCLR, BTXT, ATXT, VTXT), LTEX, TXST, WRLD (WNAM, PNAM, CNAM, NAM2, NAM3, NAM4, DNAM, MNAM, DATA, NAM0, NAM9, ONAM, WCTR), WATR basic fields and exterior cell water (XCLW with `classifyCellWater`) decode on generated records
- [x] Hostile input: wrong sizes counted and skipped; VTXT over 289 entries, layers over caps, GNAM over cap, non-finite height offset, overlong paths, claimed sizes beyond the record, cut headers and every truncation refused with a located message
- [x] The walker places each LAND in its world, cell and XCLC grid (compressed records included), a bad record is an error and the walk goes on
- [x] The five RECALLED-VERIFY items are settled from the real data (below)
Out of scope: SDK types (E1), the neutral store, terrain storage, shaders, water drawing, LOD, the parent-chain resolution across plugins, resolving LTEX to TXST to file paths across a load order (the records are decoded; the join is E1/E3).

### Settled from the five official masters (merged, later plugin wins; aggregates only)

- **Totals:** 54,079 LAND records seen and parsed, 0 record errors, 0 odd subrecords (after MNAM was read as 28 bytes), 49 worldspaces, 91 LTEX, 711 TXST, 56 WATR. Every LAND has the 1,096/3,267/3,267-byte VHGT/VNML/VCLR forms; all BTXT/ATXT are 8 bytes, VTXT a multiple of 8; layer indexes are contiguous in every quadrant (0 gaps); at most 23 layers per cell, 6 per quadrant.
- **Height orientation.** Tamriel, 11,186 LAND: `heights[row * 33 + col]`, col = +X (east), row = +Y (north). An east neighbour shares this cell's column 32 with its own column 0 (11,067 of 11,092 pairs agree to 0.01 unit, 99.77 percent), a north neighbour row 32 with row 0 (11,032 of 11,067, 99.68 percent). No other pairing of the 4 x 4 edges, forward or reversed, exceeds 5 percent. The 60 disagreeing pairs (0.27 percent, note 0053's 49 of 22,159 with a coarser count) are seams in the data, as in Morrowind.
- **Quadrants and VTXT layout.** Quadrant 0 SW, 1 SE, 2 NW, 3 NE; VTXT position = `row * 17 + col` with the same axes. Shared-vertex test: q0 column 16 against q1 column 0 matches 60,114 of 86,657 edge entries (69 percent) against 2,064 of 87,469 (2.4 percent) for the transposed reading; q2|q3 59,733 of 86,213 against 2,099; q0 row 16 against q2 row 0 59,390 of 87,469 against 2,030; q1|q3 59,819 of 87,504 against 2,017. Base textures agree most across exactly the borders this implies (east: A.q1 with B.q0 66.3 percent and A.q3 with B.q2 67.1 percent, the next best 61.5; north: A.q2/B.q0 65.4 and A.q3/B.q1 65.3, the next best 61.5; the diagonal pairs inside a cell are the lowest, 64.6 to 64.9 against 68.4 to 70.3).
- **PNAM bits.** 0x04 map, 0x08 water, 0x10 climate certain: every child with the bit set has no MNAM (43 of 43), no NAM2 (7 of 7), no CNAM (22 of 22); with 0x08 clear all 37 have NAM2. **0x01 and 0x02 are never apart** (7 worlds have both, 37 neither), so which of them is land and which LOD is UESP's order and not settled by this data; worlds with them set have little LAND of their own (none has 90 percent of its cells, 5 of 7 have some). 0x20 and 0x40 have no matching decoded subrecord: still RECALLED-VERIFY. PNAM values seen: 0x0, 0x4 (14 worlds), 0xc, 0x14 (10), 0x1c, 0x24 (4), 0x44 (2), 0x54 (2), 0x57, 0x5f, 0x74 (2), 0x77, 0x7f (4).
- **XCLW.** All exterior cells (five masters merged): 72,117 hold the maximum float (the world's default), 675 a real height (-17,000 to 12,608), 128 a "no water" sentinel: -FLT_MAX (23), -2^31 (77) and 4.29495e9 (28, within a few float steps of 2^32). The 128 are all in cells with flag 0x02 set. The cell DATA flag 0x02 is set in 72,915 of 72,920 exterior cells, whatever the water, so it does not say whether water exists (the 5 cells with flags 0 hold the default value; unverified what they mean). Note 0053's attribution of the sentinels to -2^31 and 4.29e9 only was incomplete: -FLT_MAX is the third. `classifyCellWater` maps the maximum float to UseWorldDefault, magnitudes of 2^31 and beyond to NoWater, non-finite to Invalid.
- **Null ATXT.** 13,089 of 123,505 ATXT (10.6 percent) have texture 0; 13,071 of them carry VTXT entries (937,357 entries, opacity up to 1), 2,906 are alone in their quadrant, 9,270 are layer 0. A null BTXT is the common case (165,922 of 208,724 quadrants; 40,464 of 52,181 lands have no base at all). Reading: null means the game's default ground texture; `textures\landscape\dirt02.dds` and `dirt02_n.dds` (the file upstream's `assignDefaultTextures` names for form version 16+ Skyrim) are in the archives. One id (0x844 of Skyrim.esm) is used by 101 layers (27 ATXT, 74 BTXT) and is in no record of its plugin, a dangling reference to treat like null.
- **Layer overlap** (risk 2 of the note). Vertices by textures needed (base + every layer including null): 1: 53,343,913; 2: 5,634,749; 3: 1,165,002; 4: 162,206; **5: 14,512; 6: 835; 7: 19**. So four textures per texel truncate about 15,400 of 60.3 million vertices (0.026 percent); weight splat with 4 dominant layers is adequate.
- **Solstheim.** Grid -64..127 on both axes; 34,557 cells, 34,383 LAND. The map's usable rectangle (MNAM, cells x 8..17, y 1..17) holds 170 cells and 170 LAND; the other 34,213 LAND are filler outside it: 14,651 are completely flat, 13,718 of them at -4592, the world's default land height -4590 rounded to the 8-unit step (15 distinct flat heights), 1,465 of the filler have texture layers and 1,039 a base. So the count is sea floor and empty land around the island, not playable ground. Default water height 256. (Apocrypha also: 122 LAND in 10,547 cells, 117 flat at the default.)
- **Other facts.** Whiterun-style worlds: not re-measured here (note 0053's 7 own LAND stands). WATR DNAM sizes and LTEX/TXST joins were not studied beyond: 0 LTEX with a missing texture set, 1 LTEX without a set, 0 sets without a diffuse path.
