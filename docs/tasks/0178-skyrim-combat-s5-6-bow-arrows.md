## Survey (what exists, with file and function)
- **The contract has the slots and event kinds already.** `ClipSlot::BowDraw`, `BowRelease` and `ClipEventKind::BowDrawStart`, `BowDrawn`, `BowRelease` were declared in 0.1 (`combat_rules.hpp`) with no consumer. `classifyClipEvent` (`neutralcombatbridge.cpp`) knew `bowDrawStart`, `bowDrawn`, `BowRelease`, `BowReleaseFast`. **The data names the release event `arrowRelease`** (`animationdata/defaultmale.txt`, `Bow_Release`: arrowRelease 0.0333, `AttackWinStart` 0.58, `bowEnd` 0.74, `attackStop` 0.76; `Bow_DrawLight`: arrowAttach 0.3, bowDrawStart 0.8, bowDrawn 1.6667): it is added to the classifier.
- **Clips.** The humanoid bow clips of `Skyrim - Animations.bsa` (listing by `bsatool`): `meshes\actors\character\animations\bow_drawlight.hkx`, `bow_drawheavy.hkx`, `bow_release.hkx`, `bow_idledrawn.hkx` (and the `sneakbow_` variants). The pack uses `Bow_DrawLight` and `Bow_Release` (a separate `SlotTable::bow()`, because the pinned `humanoid()` table has 7 entries).
- **Records.** WEAP DNAM (+0 animation type: 7 is a bow, +4 speed, +28/+32 minimum and maximum range: 500 and 2500 for bows, +96 stagger) was decoded (`decodeWeapon`, `combat_stats.cpp`) except the two ranges. PROJ and AMMO had no decoder. xEdit layouts (RECALLED-VERIFY, settled by the data in the stage log): PROJ DATA 92 bytes (84 or 88 for six records): flags u16, type u16, gravity f32, speed f32, range f32; MODL names the model; AMMO DATA 16 bytes: projectile form id, flags u32, damage f32, value u32. GMSTs (note 0056 section 4): `iArrowInventoryChance` 33, `fCombatMissileStickDepth` 20, `fAttachedArrowAgeMax` 10.
- **How OpenMW's own arrows work (reference only, nothing reused).** `MWWorld::ProjectileManager` (`projectilemanager.cpp`) launches a `MWPhysics::Projectile` body (a Jolt body in `mProjectiles`, `projectile.cpp`, gravity factor 0: OpenMW moves it itself in `PhysicsSystem::updateProjectile`), draws it through `MWRender::Projectiles::add` (a model, a position, an attitude, a handle the manager moves) and resolves hits in `processHits` against Morrowind records (`MWMechanics::projectileHit`). It needs a `Ptr` for the caster and an `ESM::Weapon` for the ammunition, so a neutral actor cannot use it; the renderer half (`Projectiles::add`, `ProjectileHandle`) is generic and is reused.
- **Sweeps are the model.** The melee sweep is cast by the core at the moment of the request and told in the next frame (`NeutralCombatBridge::Writer::doRequestSweep`, `NeutralSweep::arcSweep`); a line of sight uses one Jolt ray (`NeutralSight::blocked`); actors are upright capsule `Object`s on `Layers::WORLD` (`NeutralActor`), listed by `NeutralCombatWorld::collectCandidates` and ignored by the rays (`PhysicsSystem::neutralSightIgnore`).
- **The player's attack key.** `PhysicsSystem::updateNeutralActors` feeds `NeutralPlayerAttackInput` from `NeutralPlayerState::mAttackHeld` and tells the pack a tap or a hold (task 0172). A bow needs the press and the release themselves.

## Design
1. **Decision 0003 and 0013: bodies are the core's.** The pack never creates a body. It ASKS (`CombatRequestWriter::launchProjectile`, a `ProjectileRequest` of plain numbers: origin, heading, pitch, speed, gravity, range, stick depth and time, a model path) and the core owns the shot, steps it and tells the pack how it ended (`CombatRules::onProjectileResult`, once, at the start of the next update, before the clip events, never from inside a request), the way a sweep is answered.
2. **A shot is a core-owned ballistic body in the one physics world, not a Jolt dynamic body (decision 1 for toddhow).** `NeutralProjectile::step` (pure, `neutralprojectile.*`) advances a point by semi-implicit Euler (velocity first, then position) and tests the WHOLE segment of the frame: actors and the player analytically (a segment against an upright capsule: cylinder plus two spheres), static geometry, terrain and closed doors by a Jolt ray cast (`NeutralSight::firstHit` on `WORLD | HEIGHTMAP | DOOR`, the actors' bodies ignored). A real dynamic Jolt body would move with the asynchronous physics step, sleep, need a new layer and a contact listener, and could not give a repeatable path to the byte; a 6,000 units a second arrow moves 100 units a frame, so only a segment test does not tunnel. The nearer of the actor and the world hit wins (a tie goes to the actor); the owner is never hit; a dead actor is skipped like in a sweep.
3. **Stick.** A world hit leaves the arrow drawn `stickDepth` (GMST 20) units along its flight into the surface for `stickSeconds` (GMST 10) and then removes it; an actor or player hit removes it at once (it does not follow the body: limit). At most 64 shots fly and 256 stuck arrows stay.
4. **Drawing.** The core draws a shot through `MWRender::Projectiles::add` (`neutralprojectiles.*`): the model is the PROJ's MODL, read through the Skyrim scene reader like any NIF; the attitude turns the model's +Y onto the flight direction (the construction of OpenMW's gravity bolts). The sink interface (`NeutralClipSink::spawnProjectile`, `moveProjectile`, `removeProjectile`) queues ops that `World::doPhysics` applies.
5. **The pack's rules (`combat.*`).** An NPC archer (the existing `combat test hostile` actors with a bow as `combat test weapon`) draws when the player is inside the bow's maximum range: `playClip(BowDraw, Bow_DrawLight)` then `setClipSpeed(weapon speed x (1 + BowSpeedBonus))`; at the `bowDrawn` event it plays `Bow_Release`; at the `arrowRelease` event it launches. The aim: from 100 units above the feet and 30 ahead, at the target's chest (80 above its feet), the pitch is the flatter solution of the ballistic equation for the PROJ's speed and gravity (`solveLaunchPitch`; gravity = PROJ gravity x 686.38 units a second squared: RECALLED-VERIFY, the field's real unit is not settled by the data). Damage on a hit: (bow damage + ammo damage) x min(1.5, 1 + archery skill / 200) x draw fraction, armour takes its percent off (`computeBowHit`), then the existing hit path (`applyHit`: block, recoil or stagger, death). The arrow ends in the victim's inventory with a deterministic roll against `iArrowInventoryChance` (there is no inventory yet: counted per fighter, S5-9). Test settings (all off by default, pack settings `[extension.skyrimse]`): `combat test ammo` (AMMO editor id), `combat test player bow` (seconds the player holds for each automatic shot), `combat test bow speed bonus`.
6. **The player's bow.** New `CombatRules::onPlayerAttackEdge(pressed)` (the core sends every change of the attack control). With a bow as the weapon the press starts a hidden draw clock (the player has no first-person arms), the release shoots with draw fraction = held time / (the draw clip's `bowDrawn` time / draw speed), speed = PROJ speed x (0.35 + 0.65 fraction), damage scaled by the fraction; a hold shorter than 0.2 s cancels. The draw clip's `bowDrawn` time comes from the sidecar at run time (`CombatRequestWriter::clipEventTime`), like the melee timing. The shot leaves the eye (118 above the feet) along the look direction (`SubjectState::pitch`, the camera's pitch).
7. **Draw speed.** `CombatRequestWriter::setClipSpeed(subject, multiplier)` makes the clip just started run at that multiplier: the bridge's clip clock runs that much faster (events fire earlier) and `SkyrimPose::Controller::setRate` speeds the drawn clip. BowSpeedBonus is a perk and enchantment actor value the pack does not read yet: it is 0 except for the test setting.
8. **Threads.** Everything is main thread, in `combatUpdate` and `World::doPhysics`; the shots, the results, the stuck list and the op lists are reserved when the first update with rules runs, so a frame allocates nothing (a test counts it).
