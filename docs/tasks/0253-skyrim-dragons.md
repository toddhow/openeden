# Task 0253: Skyrim dragons, first slice

Design doc section: `docs/design/game-packs.md` (the Skyrim SE pack), decision 0017 (neutral actors); tasks 0212, 0216 and 0246 (creature profiles), 0205 (shouts), 0190 (combat AI), 0174 (ragdolls).
Branch `dragons` from `3f24451a88`. Same hand wrote tests and code; tests first for every new pure rule and for the combat half, run red against stubs; no fresh reviewer; Windows only; local commit, not pushed.

Goal: a Skyrim dragon is a dragon: it walks, bites and tail-whips on the ground, breathes its shout's fire in a cone, takes off, circles the player at a height and lands, dies with its own clip and gives its soul to the player. Behind `[extension.skyrimse] dragons` (default false; needs `creatures`, and `combat ai` and `[Game] neutral combat` to fight) and the pack setting `dragons flight` (default true).

## 2. Design

- **`dragons.hpp` / `dragons.cpp` (pure).** `dragonProfile()` and `dragonProfileFor(skeletonPath)` (the dragon row is NOT in `creatureProfilesAll()` or found by `creatureProfileFor`: two older tests pin the dragon skeleton as having no profile, and one pins the table size); the `Flight` machine (take-off 4 s rising to the cruise height, a circle round the player at the glide's speed, an approach of 3.33 s to a landing point, a touch-down of 1.33 s; a dead dragon in the air is taken off its pose where it is); `breathCone`/`coneContains`; `soulAbsorbs`. Every number is a named constant, VERIFIED or OURS as the header says (cruise height 900 and circle radius 1500 so that the breath's 2000 reaches; 14 s on the ground, 16 s in the air; cone 25 degrees; soul after 4 s within 2000 units).
- **The flight is the pack's, not physics.** `SkyrimCombat::updateDragons` (new `combat_dragon.cpp`) steps `Flight` once an update and writes `AiMoveIntent::Fly` and `Land`; `SkyrimCellSource::update` turns them into the existing furniture requests: `useFurniture` (a new loop of the clip: take-off, glide, approach, touch-down) and `placeFurniture` every update (the capsule moves with the actor), `leaveFurniture` to land (the floor rules take over). The combat AI skips a dragon that is airborne or breathing (`dragonHolds`, one guard in `runAi`).
- **The breath is not cast through `beginCast` or the player's shout runtime.** Those pay magicka (a dragon has none) and wait for a `SpellFire` event the dragon's clip does not send under that name, and the shout state is the PLAYER's. The dragon takes the first spell of the first SHOU in its profile spells whose slot 0 is an aimed concentration spell (`DragonFireBreathShout01` gives `VoiceDragonFire01`), plays the breath clip, and 0.3333 s later (the sidecar event's time) every update for the cast duration every fighter inside a 25 degree cone from the mouth to the target takes the spell's per-second health effects (resistance and weakness as for a beam; the same rules as `tickBeam`, copied, not refactored). The cooldown is the shout's recovery (5 s) but never under 6 s. A breath starts when the target is farther than reach + 250 and within the projectile's range 2000, on the ground or while circling. The drawn flame is the existing drawn-only projectile stream (`combat spell visuals`).
- **Walking.** A dragon's go-to is a straight walk (`AiMoveIntent::mDirect`, `setMovement` at 384 units a second), not a navmesh path: an agent of radius 150 finds no path ("no way to the target" in every run). The mover still stops it at walls and steep slopes.
- **Death and soul.** The dragon's own `mtdeathintro` is played once whatever killed it (a death by a spell plays a humanoid stagger clip in `combat_magic.cpp`, which the dragon's skeleton lacks). The soul: `ShoutState::addSouls(1)` and `ShoutState::announce("Dragon soul absorbed")` once the player is within 2000 units 4 s after the death (needs the pack setting `shouts`; without it the soul is lost and traced). The shouts namespace gains `notification_count` and `notification(n)` and `shouts_player.lua` shows them as the game's message (the HUD indicator still needs `[GUI] rmlui`; the message does not). The counter is the shouts runtime's own, saved in its chunk (`souls`), and `ShoutState::unlock` spends it.
- **Settings** (all declared in `extension.json`): `dragons` (false), `dragons flight` (true); developer aids `dragons test place` + `dragons test replace` (`<editor id>` or `<editor id>@x,y`: the placed actors of that base, within 500 units of x, y when given, take the base of the dragon NPC), `dragons test ground seconds`, `dragons test air seconds`, `dragons test health`, `dragons test damage scale`, `dragons test kill at`. Not in the test profile as settings (pack settings; a comment line and a README line say so).

## 7. Decisions for toddhow

1. **The capsule radius 150 and the walk.** A dragon's go-to is a straight `setMovement` walk because the navmesh finds no path for its size; the mover blocks it at the first wall or slope. A smaller capsule (60 was tried) did not help; the flight avoids the problem.
2. **The breath is the pack's own cone, not the shout runtime.** It reads the shout's data (spell, recovery) but pays no magicka and does not touch the player's `ShoutState` but for the soul. The half angle (25 degrees), the mouth's place and the cooldown floor (6 s) are ours.
3. **Flight numbers are ours** (cruise height 900, circle 1500, 14 s ground and 16 s air, landing 900 units from the player): the clips' own rises (2048) are not applied.
4. **The soul needs the `shouts` pack setting** and is lost (traced) without it; its delay (4 s) and range (2000) are ours. Whether the corpse should burn away (the real game) is not built.
5. **A dead dragon stays** as a corpse in its death pose; nothing removes it and no loot window rule was added.
6. `creatureProfileFor` still does not know the dragon (two pinned tests); delete the dragon paths from `SkyrimCreatureTable.creaturesThisSliceDoesNotKnowAndPeopleHaveNoProfile` and `SkyrimCreatureThirdLookup.creaturesNoRowKnowsAndPeopleStillHaveNoProfile` to fold the dragon into the table (not edited).
