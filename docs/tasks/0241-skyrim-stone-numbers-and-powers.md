## 2. Design

* **`ability_numbers.*` (new, pure).** `sumAbilityNumbers(library, spells)` adds, over the sorted unique spell list, the effects that count: every ValueModifier or Peak effect of an ABILITY spell, and of any other spell only a Peak effect with a duration and no Hostile flag (a Fortify blessing; a known spell such as Healing is an instant ValueModifier and counts for nothing, a hostile Peak harms). The sign is the effect's own (`signedMagnitude`: a Detrimental flag makes it negative). It fills `AbilityNumbers`: Fortify of health, magicka and stamina (24 to 26), carry weight (32), the six resistances (40 to 45, signed: negative is a weakness), spell absorb (83), the regeneration rates (27 to 29) and "Mult" values (155 to 157). Every sum is cut to +-10,000, a non-finite number counts as 0. `abilityResistPercent` combines the element's own net resistance and the magic resistance as two independent shares (1 - (1 - a)(1 - b)), not for poison and disease, never twice for magic, at most 85; `abilityWeaknessPercent` adds the negative nets (at most 100). Combination rules and the magic-resistance scope are OURS (RECALLED-VERIFY).
* **Combat (`combat_abilities.cpp`, hook calls in `combat.cpp`, `combat_regen.cpp`, `combat_magic.cpp`, `combat_status.cpp`, `combat_skills.cpp`).** `preparePerks` (called when a spell list changes) ends with `applyAbilityNumbers`, for the player only: the numbers go into `Fighter::mAbilities`; the Fortify share of the three maximums is added to the profile (and taken off when the spell goes), the current magicka and stamina change by the same amount (the game adds a modifier to both), the health change is owed to the engine and sent by the next update as the same zero-damage report a level up's gain uses (`flushAbilityVitals`; a loss lowers the engine's health by what it took, never below 1). `syncProgression` keeps the applied share when it rebuilds the maximums. Regeneration: `regenRateFor` starts from the held abilities' rate and Mult sums, then adds the running effects' (task 0238), so (base + rates) x (1 + Mult / 100) with both sources; a rest uses it too (a timed effect does not apply in a rest, as before). The player's health bonus goes through task 0238's report. Resistances: `resistOf` returns the combined resistance for the player when he holds numbers; `weaknessOf` adds the held weakness (the Apprentice's) to the running ones. `playerAbilityNumbers`, `playerResistPercent`, `playerWeaknessPercent`, `abilityRevision` are the read side (the carry weight and a later disease task read them).
* **Carry weight.** `InventoryService::setCapacityBonus` / `capacity()`; the pack gives it the Steed's 100 when the combat's `abilityRevision` moves; the Lua `capacity()` binding returns it. Nothing in the pack enforces encumbrance (no movement penalty exists in the movement model), so the Steed's "no armour weight penalty" has nothing to remove.
* **Powers.** `CharacterService::powers()` lists the race's powers (when the creation is done) and then the powers among the spells the player holds beyond them (`Hooks::mExtraPowerSpells` = the stones service's active list; the combat's `racePowers` keeps the power types). The same cooldown (a game day) and `P` key (the first ready power; a second press after the race's power is used goes to the stone's). `powerSupportOf` classes Invisibility (11) and Paralysis (21) as Works. Invisibility is a timed status (`ActorStatus::mInvisibleUntil`) read by `playerInvisible()`; the sneak system's new hook `mInvisible` stops every observer from noticing the player while it runs (a hostile actor already engaged against him is unchanged).
* **`Actor.AddPerk` is saved.** The host's state is version 3 (the perk list after the filters; versions 1 and 2 are still read and give no perks); `perkRevision()` moves on a new perk and on a restore; the pack gives the player's perks to the combat (`setPlayerExtraPerks`; they join the perk list like the test setting's and leave it when the list changes).
* **The blessing clock.** The stones service's `mNow` is the game clock the rest script gives (`RestService::now()`, once it is above 0) and the host's script clock before that (without `rest` the clock is the host's, which a wait does not move).

## Decisions for toddhow

1. **The six start-up `AddPerk` calls now give the player perks.** The old runtime recorded and dropped them. Alternatives: ignore `AddPerk` calls made by start-up scripts before the character is created (they may be the engine's own placeholder perks), or keep them as they are (the game's own data).
2. **Magic resistance applies to every hostile effect except poison and disease, multiplied with the element's own** (RECALLED-VERIFY: the game's `ResistMagic` scope). The alternative is only for effects whose own resist value is 44.
3. **Fortify of a vital also raises the current value** (and lowers it again when it ends); the health change reaches the engine as a report that never takes it below 1.
4. **Spell absorb is a number only** (the Atronach's 50): no random absorption rule exists. Which random source the combat should use for it is your call.
5. **The stones' powers share the character service's list and cooldown, so they need `character creation`.** Without it (`character creation` off) there is no `P`, no list and no cooldown. Alternative: a power list in the stones service of its own (needs its own saved cooldown).
6. **Blessing clock without `rest`: the host's script clock** (a wait does not move it). The old saves' timed spells (task 0237, host clock, small numbers) end at once when the pack's clock is the game clock.
7. **Skill and shout fortifies of the blessings and the Tower stone are not built** (they need the skill-modifier layer of the progression).
