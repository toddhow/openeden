# Task: Skyrim spell delivery, target actor, target location (runes) and area (task 0242)
Design doc section: `docs/research/0056-skyrim-combat-survey.md` sections 4 and 8; decision `docs/decisions/0018-skyrim-combat-hooks.md`; tasks 0186 (magic runtime), 0191 (visible spells and beams), 0234 (scrolls).
Approval: coordinator's order of 2026-11-16 (branch `delivery` from `df34ba5615`). Full loop in form only: the same hand wrote tests and code; the delivery tests were written first and run red against stubs; no fresh reviewer; Windows only.
Goal: with `[Game] neutral combat` and the new pack setting `combat spell delivery`, a spell whose delivery is "target actor" (Heal Other) goes to the actor in the sights, a "target location" spell (the three runes) is placed where its projectile lands and goes off when an enemy comes near, and an aimed spell with an area (the Fireball) hurts everyone around where it ends.
Inherited code touched: none (all OpenEden code, `game_packs/skyrimse`).
Constraints: off by default (`[extension.skyrimse] combat spell delivery = false`, declared in `extension.json`; with it off every path is the old one); main thread, inside `combatUpdate`; no `CombatRequestWriter` virtual added (it uses `castRay`, `launchProjectile`, `subjectState` and the existing projectile result); no allocation per frame beyond the trace lines; determinism (no clock but the combat clock, fighters visited in their list order).
Out of scope: summons and raise dead, the explosion art and sound, knock back (EXPL force), a line-of-sight test for explosions, self-delivery area spells (Grand Healing), saving runes.

## 1. Survey (Skyrim.esm, a Python walker written for this task; aggregates)

`fireCast` (task 0186) refused delivery 3 and 4 ("is not supported") and ended an aimed spell's projectile only when it hit an actor: it applied the effects to that one actor, and an effect's area (SPEL EFIT +4, in feet) was read by nothing but race powers (`applyPowerEffects`, task 0228).

Data, VERIFIED on the five masters (`SkyrimDeliveryReal`, the Python survey):
- **PROJ DATA** (84, 88 or 92 bytes): the alternate trigger proximity is f32 at +28 (150.0 for `RuneFireProjectile`, `RuneFrostProjectile`, `RuneLightningProjectile` and the three `Trap...Rune` ones, 0 for the rest) and the explosion is a form id at +36 (an EXPL for 38 of the 134 PROJ of Skyrim.esm, 94 of the 237 over the five masters; 0 otherwise).
- **EXPL DATA** is 52 bytes for 136 of 143 records in Skyrim.esm (44, 40 and 48 for the others): six form words (light, sounds, impact data set, placed object, spell), then force f32 +24, damage f32 +28, radius f32 +32, image space radius f32 +36. `ExplosionRuneFlame01` (Fire Rune): force 240, damage 2, **radius 320**; the Frost and Shock rune explosions have radius 320 too (force 128); the Firebolt storm explosion 106. 278 EXPL over the five masters, all with a 40 to 52 byte DATA, 0 odd subrecords. The damage word is a placeholder (2.0 in the runes); the damage is the spell's effects. No falloff field: the flag word (+44) has Always Uses World Orientation, Knock Down, Ignore LOS and similar, nothing about distance.
- **The Fireball** (`FireballRightHand`, 0xBB969): aimed, one fire damage effect, magnitude 40, **area 15 feet** (15 x 64/3 = 320 units), PROJ `FireBallProjectile01` with NO explosion: the area is the effect's. So "an explosion" is either an effect area or the EXPL its projectile names, and the pack takes whichever the effect has: area first, else the EXPL radius.
- **Heal Other** (`HealOther` 0x12FD2, scroll `HealOtherScroll` 0xA44C1): delivery 3, effect `RestoreHealthFFActor` (value modifier, health, 75 a spell / 45 a scroll), PROJ `HealFakeProjectile`.
- **The runes** (`FireRune` 0x5DB90 and the scrolls 0x965A8 to 0x965AA): delivery 4, fire and forget, one effect (`RuneFireFFLocation`, magnitude 50 / 40, duration 1, area 0): so the explosion's 320 units are what reach the target. **The Fire Rune spell costs 234 magicka (SPIT +0, as the game logged "not enough magicka (100.000 of 234.000)"), above the stand-in player's 100**: the player can only cast a rune by scroll in this build.
- Delivery of the 99 scrolls: self 34, aimed 50, target actor 5, target location 10 (touch 0), as task 0234 said.

## 2. Design

`combat_delivery.cpp` (members of `SkyrimCombat`), a few hooks in `combat_magic.cpp`, the pure `deliveryIsCast` in `magic.*`:

- **Target actor.** `fireCast` (delivery 3, setting on) calls `targetActorCast`: `aimRay` (the player's from the eye along the view, an actor's at its aim target) with range `beamRange(spell)` (the effect projectile's PROJ range, else the SPEL's, else 768), `castRay`, and the effects go to the actor or player the ray meets through `applySpellEffects` (conditions, wards and resistances as any spell). It finds nobody when the ray meets the world, nothing, the caster or a corpse: the magicka is spent, the trace says "found no actor in the sights". Only a hostile effect puts the caster in combat.
- **Target location.** `fireCast` (delivery 4) launches the spell's PROJ as an ordinary projectile with `stickSeconds` = `kRuneLifetimeSeconds` (600, OURS) and `stickDepth` 4, so the core draws the rune glyph where it lands; `resolveSpellShot` hands the end to `deliverAtEnd`, which calls `placeRune` at the result's position (an actor hit places it at the hit point). A rune has the trigger distance of its PROJ (DATA +28, else `kDefaultRuneProximity` 150) and a lifetime. `tickRunes` (once per `updateMagic`) sets it off when a living fighter other than the caster, that is the caster's enemy (`relationOf` Enemy, or an AI actor engaged against the caster), is within the trigger distance (3D, to its feet); it ends it after 600 s; at most `kMaxRunes` 32 stand and a new one replaces the oldest; a rune whose caster is removed (`cancelCastsOf`) goes. Allies and neutrals walk over it.
- **Area.** `deliverAtEnd` for an AIMED spell with any effect that has a radius (`effectRadius`: area x `kUnitsPerFoot`, else the EXPL radius of the effect's projectile) calls `explodeAt` at the end point of a shot that hit an actor, the player or the world (an expired shot explodes nowhere). `explodeAt` visits the fighters in list order: the caster is skipped, as are the dead; one that the caster may not hit (`mayHit`, the real relation when `combat factions` is on) is skipped unless the shot hit it; the actor the shot hit gets every effect (the impact is on its body); the others get each effect whose radius reaches them (distance from the end point to their feet). No falloff (the data has none). A ward blocks per target.
- `ExplosionRecord`/`decodeExplosion`, `ProjectileRecord::mProximity` and `mExplosion`, `CombatData::explosionById` (null default; `SkyrimWorldView` implements it).
- `deliveryIsCast(delivery, beamsOn, deliveryOn)`: self and aimed always, touch with the beams setting, target actor and location with the delivery setting. Used by the real-data count.

## 7. Not run, limits, decisions for toddhow

Not run: the Linux gate and GCC `-Werror` (traps checked by hand: braced gtest ifs, no always-false 32 bit comparisons, escaped backslashes, `(void)` on discarded results), clang-format 14 (not installed; formatted by hand to 120 columns), clang-tidy, the Lua tests, `openmw-tests` (no engine file changed), a fresh review, mutation checks of the engine half (there is none).
Limits: runes are not saved; summons and raise dead are untouched; no explosion art or sound; no knock back; an explosion has no line-of-sight test, so it reaches through a thin wall (EXPL flag 8, "ignore LOS", is not read); self-delivery spells with an area (Grand Healing, Circle of Protection) still go to the caster only; a spell cast by an NPC at the player works for target location (tested) but the NPC AI never picks a target actor or a rune (`aiPickSpell` takes aimed, hostile, fire and forget spells); the trigger is the caster's ENEMY (relation Enemy or engaged against him), not "anyone but allies", and Skyrim's rule is RECALLED-VERIFY; the 600 s lifetime and the 32 rune cap are OURS.
Decisions for toddhow:
1. **The rune glyph stays after it goes off.** The glyph is the stuck projectile (`stickSeconds`), and no request removes a projectile. A `cancelProjectile(token)` virtual on `CombatRequestWriter` (and the HUD wrapper's forward) fixes it; I did not add one (the order). Until then a set-off rune still looks armed for up to 600 s.
2. **Default of `combat spell delivery`.** Off, like `combat spell beams`; it changes how the Fireball plays (area), so I did not make it default on.
3. **Who sets a rune off.** Enemies only; a rune also hurts allies standing inside its radius when it goes off (the caster is the only one spared). Skyrim spares followers; that needs the factions rules in `explodeAt` for the player's spells (`mayHit` returns true for a player attacker).
4. **A direct hit on an area spell.** The actor the shot hit gets every effect, including those with a radius that would not reach its feet; it is damaged once per effect, not once for being both direct and inside.
