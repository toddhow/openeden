# Task: The neutral world model, stage N4 engine half (doors and moving between cells)

Status: **DRAFT, awaiting toddhow's approval** (docs only; nothing built, run or changed when this was written).
Design doc section: `docs/design/neutral-world-model.md` sections 9 and 10 (10.1 "door destination, owner, lock" row, 10.3, 10.4 R6/R7/R9, 10.7); `docs/design/skyrim-se-implementation-plan.md` sections 4.1 and 4.2 (milestone M-S1); decision 0016
Goal: with `[Game] neutral world` on, activating a Skyrim SE teleport door in a neutral interior moves the player to the destination cell (built as a neutral cell) at the XTEL arrival position and rotation; an interior-to-exterior door is refused with a message; locked doors are refused with a message; doors with no teleport do not crash and do not teleport.

Everything below was read from the tree at `26d1265f52` (file and function cited). Numbers called estimates are mine, plus or minus 50 percent. **RECALLED-VERIFY** marks what was not checked in source or data.

## What exists (read)

- **The decoded data.** `game_packs/skyrimse/src/neutral_world.cpp` (`makeComponents`, about lines 100 to 135) turns a REFR's XTEL into `TeleportComponent` (`sdk/include/openeden/game/world_model.hpp`): `destinationDoor` (the pack id of the other door), `destinationCell` (filled from the index pass's door-to-cell table, `SkyrimWorldView::teleportDoorCells`, `world_view.cpp`; left `kNoWorldId` and counted in `mTeleportCellsUnknown` when the door is not in the table), `destination` (position and rotation quaternion, `eulerToQuat`; "arrival position and rotation" per the XTEL comment in `game_packs/skyrimse/include/game_skyrimse/records.hpp`) and `flags`. XLOC becomes `LockComponent` with `locked = true` whenever an XLOC is present (a decision flagged RECALLED-VERIFY in that file), `level`, and `key` (a KEYM base id). XOWN becomes `OwnerComponent`. Verified on the installed masters (changelog, task 0099 stage N4): 2,204 of 2,204 master doors lead back.
- **What the engine keeps.** `makeNeutralCellRef` (`apps/openmw/mwworld/neutralcell.cpp`) fills only id, `RefNum`, position, angles, scale and count of the `ESM::CellRef`: **teleport, lock and owner are not copied to the reference** ("kept on the ref and unused", design 10.1). They survive only in the snapshot `CellStore::neutralCell()` (`cellstore.hpp`, a copy of the `WorldCell`; `objects` are "in a fixed order: the placement id, ascending", `world_model.hpp`). A placement is recovered from a `Ptr` by its `RefNum::mIndex` (`neutralRefNum`, `neutralcell.cpp`: index = placement id, content file `kNeutralRefNumFile`).
- **The class.** `MWClass::Neutral` (`apps/openmw/mwclass/neutral.hpp/.cpp`) is the one class of every kind (design 10.3 (a)). It overrides `getName`, `getModel`, `getModelsToPreload` (empty), `insertObjectRendering`, `insertObject`, `insertObjectPhysics`; it has **no `activate`** (the `Class::activate` default, `apps/openmw/mwworld/class.hpp`, returns a null action) and `isDoor()` is the default `false`. The player's activate key goes `mwinput/actionmanager.cpp` (`player.activate()`) to the faced object's `Class::activate`.
- **How a Morrowind door works.** `game_packs/morrowind/src/class_door.cpp` `Door::activate`: refuses NPC teleports, checks lock and key against the actor's inventory, a trap, then for a teleport door returns `MWWorld::ActionTeleport(ptr.getCellRef().getDestCell(), ptr.getCellRef().getDoorDest(), true)` with the open sound; otherwise `ActionDoor` (animated door). `ActionTeleport::executeImp/teleport` (`apps/openmw/mwworld/actionteleport.cpp`) calls `World::changeToCell(cellId, position, true)` for the player. `World::changeToCell` (`worldimp.cpp` about line 885) pushes `MWState::ChangeCell` (`apps/openmw/mwstate/changecell.hpp`), which looks the cell up with `WorldModel::getCell(RefId)`, preloads it (`Scene::preloadCell`), and then calls `Scene::changeToInteriorCell(cellStore.getCell()->getNameId(), ...)` (`scene.cpp` line 955), which unloads every active cell, loads the new one (`loadCell`), `changePlayerCell`, fog and sky. The `onCellChanged` lambda in `changeToCell` moves and rotates the player and calls `adjustPosition(player, true)` (drops the player to the ground).
- **How a neutral cell is found.** `WorldModel::findInterior(name)` (`mwworld/worldmodel.cpp` line 274) tries `emplaceNeutralInteriorCellStore(name)` first: it asks the `NeutralWorldStore` (`components/worldmodel/store.hpp`) `findCellByEditorId` for each registered game, builds the cell, refuses an exterior ("exterior neutral cells are not supported yet", line 238), adds the base records, makes the synthetic `ESM::Cell` (`makeNeutralCellRecord`) and emplaces a `CellStore` in `mCells`, then `setNeutralCell`. **It is keyed by editor id only**; a destination in an XTEL is a pack `WorldId`, and the engine has no id-to-cell path. `WorldModel::findCell(RefId)` (line 309) looks in `mCells` and knows no neutral fallback (what it does for a plain string RefId, lines 331 to 350, was not read: RECALLED-VERIFY).
- **Door scene hook that must not fire.** `Scene::addObject` (`scene.cpp` line 181) treats `isDoor() && !getCellRef().getTeleport()` as an animated door and reads its collision shape for the navigator. `Neutral` keeps `isDoor()` false, so a neutral door never enters that branch; `mwlua/objectbindings.cpp` line 153 also keys the Lua door type on `isDoor()`.

## Recommended design (three sentences)

Give `MWClass::Neutral` an `activate` override that switches on the base kind (the same one-class rule as design 10.3 (a); no neutral door class, `isDoor()` stays false); for a Door whose placement has a `TeleportComponent` it returns an `ActionTeleport` to the destination cell, and for a locked one a `FailedAction` with a message. The destination is resolved by a new `WorldModel::findNeutralCell(gameId, WorldId)` (the existing `emplaceNeutralInteriorCellStore` body, keyed by pack id, also caching the cell under its editor id so `Scene::changeToInteriorCell`'s `getInterior(name)` finds it), the arrival `ESM::Position` comes from `destination` through `neutralRotationAngles`, and everything stays in `apps/openmw` with the SDK untouched. An interior-to-exterior door is refused with a message and nothing moves (no black screen, no half-loaded state), because the neutral model has no exteriors yet (design 10.4 R7).

## Scope

1. **Placement lookup.** `CellStore::neutralObject(WorldId placement)` (binary search of `neutralCell()->objects` by id, which is ascending; no new map) and `MWWorld::neutralPlacementOf(const Ptr&)` (`RefNum::mIndex` back to a placement id, the inverse of `neutralRefNum`). Pure and unit tested.
2. **Destination cell by pack id.** `WorldModel::findNeutralCell(std::string_view gameId, WorldId cellId)`: the body of `emplaceNeutralInteriorCellStore` split so both entry points share it (by editor id and by pack id); returns null with a logged reason for an unknown id, an exterior, or a cell that cannot be built. Registers the new `CellStore` in `mInteriors` under the cell's editor id so the later by-name lookup in `Scene::changeToInteriorCell` and `ChangeCell` finds the same store. A cell already built is reused (this is the "come back" path: the same `CellStore` is loaded again).
3. **The door behaviour** in `MWClass::Neutral::activate` (a private helper, `activateDoor`), for `ObjectKind::Door` only; every other kind keeps the null action. In order:
   - actor is not the player: `FailedAction` with no message (the same rule as `Door::activate`, which makes an NPC's teleport a no-op because of the Actors iterator invalidation FIXME);
   - the placement has a lock (`LockComponent::locked`): see "Locked doors" below;
   - the placement has a teleport: resolve `destinationCell` (if `kNoWorldId`, refuse "This door leads nowhere" and log once per placement), call `findNeutralCell`; null (exterior or unbuildable) refuses with the exterior message; otherwise `ActionTeleport(destCell.getCell()->getId(), position, false)` where the position is `destination.position` as floats and `neutralRotationAngles(destination.rotation)` in `mRot`, followers off (a neutral session has no followers);
   - no teleport: see "Doors with no teleport".
4. **Player feedback.** One message box through `MWBase::WindowManager::messageBox` for each refusal (locked, exterior, nowhere); new game UI strings go through OpenMW's YAML localisation (`files/data/l10n/...`, exact folder RECALLED-VERIFY) per the guide, not hard-coded English. No door sound (Skyrim's sounds are SNDR/SOUN records, not decoded; the open sound is left empty).
5. **Developer aid.** `OPENEDEN_NEUTRAL_DOOR_TRACE` (a trace line per activation: placement, kind of outcome, destination editor id, arrival position), following `OPENEDEN_NEUTRAL_COLLISION_TRACE`, and `OPENEDEN_NEUTRAL_UNLOCK_DOORS` (treat every lock as open, for testing only; **not** a settings.cfg key, so it needs no test-profile entry).
6. **Survey (stage a, before code).** An aggregate scan of the installed Skyrim SE masters through the pack's store (no per-record text committed): for every interior door with a teleport, whether `destinationCell` is filled, whether `findNeutralCell`'s precondition holds (the cell builds), interior or exterior destination, locked or not; and the **interior-to-interior pairs reachable from a named start cell**, to choose the acceptance route (see Acceptance). This replaces my assumption about the Bannered Mare, which I did not verify: it may have only an exterior door.

## Locked doors (recommendation: refuse, with an unlock aid for testing)

The pack reads XLOC as `locked = true` whenever the subrecord exists, `level` raw, `key` a KEYM base (RECALLED-VERIFY that a present XLOC always means locked; its flag byte is 0 or 4, "leveled lock"). The neutral session has no usable key: items are inert (design N5) and the stub player's inventory cannot hold a Skyrim base. Lockpicking, key checks, lock levels, the "unlocked by default" bit and the lockpick UI are Skyrim rules, which belong to the game pack and a later stage. Options:

- **(a, recommended) Refuse every locked door** with "The door is locked." (no level text), activation does nothing else. Honest, tiny, no invented rule. The `OPENEDEN_NEUTRAL_UNLOCK_DOORS` variable lets a test pass through them.
- (b) Ignore locks (everything opens): moves faster for testing but makes `LockComponent` meaningless and is wrong the moment N5 gives items.
- (c) Open when a placed key is "held": impossible until items are live things.

Owner (`OwnerComponent`): ignored (no crime in a neutral session; stage N5+ and the pack's rules).

## Doors with no teleport (load doors that are not portals, and plain animated doors)

A Skyrim "load door" is a door with an XTEL (it loads another cell); a door without XTEL is an in-cell animated door (a room door, a gate), which Skyrim opens with a behaviour graph or a Havok animation (RECALLED-VERIFY). Morrowind's `ActionDoor`/`World::activateDoor` rotate the door's own node by its model's animation; a Skyrim door model's hinge and animation are not read by any neutral code. Recommendation:

- **In this task:** such a door is **inert and stays closed**: `activate` returns the null action, nothing is drawn differently, its collision stays as built (the player cannot walk through it). No crash, no teleport. This is an honest, testable minimum and is what the code does today for every kind.
- **Optional sub-stage 4c (open question 2):** activation toggles the door's collision shape only (remove and re-add its Jolt body through `PhysicsSystem`, with no animation, so a closed-looking door becomes walk-through), so interior rooms are reachable. Cheap (about 1 day) but visibly a hack; I recommend asking toddhow before doing it, and recording it as "open = non-solid, model unchanged" if approved.

## Exteriors (R7): the behaviour defined

A teleport whose destination cell is an exterior (the front door of the Bannered Mare leads to Whiterun, an exterior of the WhiterunWorld worldspace) is **refused with a message** ("That door leads outside, which is not supported yet." as a localised string), logged once per destination at Info with the cell's editor id, the door stays closed and nothing else changes (no unload, no loading screen, the player stays where they are, no `FailedAction` sound beyond the default). Refusing was chosen over "load nothing": a load that begins and loads nothing leaves the player in no cell, which in this engine means a half-run `ChangeCell` state with the old cell unloaded (`Scene::changeToInteriorCell` unloads first). The check needs the destination `WorldCell`, which `NeutralWorldStore::cell` builds in full (an exterior cell may be large and is cached by the store's caps); the interface has no "is interior" query on a cell id, so we pay one build per refused destination, once (the store caches it). Fixing that cost is an additive source query (open question 5).

## Constraints

- **Morrowind unchanged, setting off and on.** Nothing runs for a `Ptr` that is not a `NeutralBase`; `Door::activate`, `ActionTeleport`, `World::changeToCell` and `Scene` are not edited. Proof: the `tools/worldobjects_check` dump (110 lines) byte-identical to the parent executable and a fixed-time Balmora frame SHA-256 equal to the parent's, with `[Game] neutral world` off and on (the comparison of N3a-3 to N3a-5), plus a Morrowind teleport door (Seyda Neen Census Office, or one in Balmora) taken in a quiet-window game before and after.
- **Layer rules:** `CI/check_layer_rules.sh` stays at **104 exceptions**, none added (all code in `apps/openmw`, host layer; no `components/extension` include of contentsource).
- **SDK additive only:** the plan is no SDK change at all (`world_model.hpp` stays 1.1, `kSdkVersion` 4.0). If the exterior-check cost forces a source query (question 5), it is added at the END of `WorldCellSource` with a "not supported" default, version policy of `world_model.hpp`, and `CI/check_extension_sdk.sh` and `worldmodel/testfrozen.cpp` updated in the same change.
- **Threading:** `activate` and the cell build run on the main thread (neutral cells are built on the main thread, adopted answer 5). Nothing in this task may call the store from the preload worker.
- **Determinism/allocation:** no per-frame work is added; the activation path allocates (a message, a cell build) on a user action only. Door lookup is a binary search, no map.
- **Off by default:** the whole path is reachable only with `[Game] neutral world` on and a game pack's source registered. No setting is added.
- **Saves:** still refused while a neutral source is registered (N3a-1 follow-up); a cell change must not make a save possible.

## Open questions for toddhow (each with a recommendation)

1. **Locked doors: refuse, ignore, or something richer?** Recommend **refuse all** with a message, plus the environment-variable test aid; real rules come with items (N5) and the pack.
2. **Doors with no teleport: inert, or collision toggle?** Recommend **inert in this task** and decide the collision toggle (about 1 day, a hack with no animation) once you have seen a walkable inn with its room doors; it can be sub-stage 4c.
3. **Interior-to-exterior: refuse with a message, or a placeholder?** Recommend **refuse with a message**; an exterior needs a land source (terrain, water, sky) and should be its own stage after N4, not a half-load.
4. **Does the acceptance have to be the Bannered Mare?** I could not confirm that it has an interior-to-interior door (RECALLED-VERIFY). Recommend the survey picks the route; the Mare covers the exterior-refusal case, and an interior pair (for example a Whiterun house, or Jorrvaskr and its lower floors; names RECALLED-VERIFY) covers go and return. Accept that substitution?
5. **An "is this cell an interior" source query?** Today refusing an exterior costs a full cell build. Recommend **not adding it yet** (one build, cached, bounded by the store caps); add an additive `WorldCellSource` query only if the survey shows exterior builds are slow enough to matter.
6. **Where do refusal messages come from?** Recommend new YAML localisation strings in the engine's own l10n table (not Skyrim's strings, which are the pack's `STRINGS` tables and not yet wired to the UI).
7. **XTEL arrival semantics.** The pack reads the XTEL position and rotation as the player's arrival in the destination cell (comment in `records.hpp`; the UESP-style layout is RECALLED-VERIFY for the meaning, VERIFIED for the 32-byte size). Recommend we **check it in game** (acceptance frames) before building anything on it, and treat a mismatch as a pack fix.
8. **`LockComponent::locked = "an XLOC is present"`** (already flagged in the N4 decoding row). Recommend keeping it for now (refuse, which is the safe direction); revisit when the lock-flag byte (0 or 4) is understood from data.
9. **Followers and the stub player.** The engine teleports followers with the player by default; recommend passing `false` here and reconsidering with N5.
10. **Full loop?** This touches `mwworld` and `mwclass`, not a listed full-loop area; the N3a stages used the full loop anyway. Recommend the **full loop** (tests first by a separate writer, fresh reviewer) because a cell change unloads and rebuilds the world.
