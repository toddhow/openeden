# Task: Skyrim exteriors, water in the game (M-S2)
Design doc section: `docs/design/skyrim-se-implementation-plan.md` 4.5 (water; decisions of 2026-11-11 in section 7: D10 PBR-leaning look behind a setting, D13 full loop for engine work); `docs/tasks/0125-skyrim-exteriors-e2-terrain-in-engine.md` (the neutral exterior this builds on); `docs/tasks/0117-skyrim-exteriors-e0-decoding.md` (what XCLW and DNAM mean in the data). Approved under toddhow's standing "go with your recommendations".
Goal: a neutral Skyrim SE exterior draws water over each grid square at the square's water height (its own XCLW or the world's default, none where the data says none), with the engine's existing water shader.
Inherited code touched: `apps/openmw/mwworld/scene.cpp` (`Scene::loadCell` water block, `unloadCell`), `apps/openmw/mwworld/cell.{hpp,cpp}` (a third `Cell` constructor), `apps/openmw/mwworld/worldmodel.cpp` (`findNeutralExterior`), `apps/openmw/mwrender/water.{hpp,cpp}`, `apps/openmw/mwrender/rendermanager.{hpp,cpp}` (`setWaterEnabled`, two new functions), `components/pipeline/mode.hpp` and `graphics.cpp` (one vertex mode `WATER_CELL`), `files/shaders/water/main.vert` (one `#ifdef` path) and its recorded interface. Morrowind paths are untouched: the new code is reached only for a neutral exterior (`CellStore::isNeutral()`), and `setWaterEnabled` only gains "the infinite plane is visible again".
Inputs / outputs: new SDK header `sdk/include/openeden/game/world_water.hpp` (`CellWater`, `CellWaterSource`, DRAFT 0.1; a new interface found by `dynamic_cast` on the registered `WorldCellSource`, the precedent of `ActorAppearanceSource`, so the pinned `WorldCellSource` is not touched); `SkyrimTerrainIndex::cellWater` (the pack resolves the rules); `Render::WaterCells` (pure bookkeeping, `components/render/watercells.hpp`); `MWRender::Water::setCellWater`/`removeCellWater`/`setGlobalVisible`; `RenderManager::setNeutralCellWater`/`removeNeutralCellWater`.
Constraints: off by default (nothing runs without `[Game] neutral world` and a pack whose source implements `CellWaterSource`); main thread only; no per-frame allocation added.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The pack answers a square's water from the data's rules: own height, "no water" sentinels, the maximum float and a missing XCLW take the world default, the water bit of PNAM takes the parent's default, a square the world does not list is read in a parent that lends its land (tests that failed against stubs first)
- [x] `Render::WaterCells` and `MWWorld::Cell` with a water height, and `WorldModel::findNeutralExterior` giving each cell its water (also an empty square) (tests that failed against stubs first)
- [x] In a game, water is drawn at Whiterun's river or a lake, only inside its squares, and not over lower squares
- [x] With the setting off and on, Morrowind is unchanged (`worldobjects_check` dump and the fixed Balmora frame match the parent build)
Out of scope (recorded as follow-ups in the stage log): swimming and buoyancy (the physics water level is one global height), waterfalls, WATR colours and fresnel/noise parameters beyond what the shader takes, the underwater view from beneath a cell surface, water in the LOD and the maps, a second pack's water.

## Stage log

### What the data says (five official masters, `SkyrimWaterReal`, aggregates)
- DNAM's second float is the world's default water height: -14000 in Tamriel and WhiterunWorld (the sea; far below every playable square). Real heights come from XCLW and make rivers and lakes: around Whiterun a lake block at -5050 (squares x 11 to 14, y -1 to 0 and neighbours), a river that steps down by whole squares (-3800, -2450, -1300 around (8, -6) to (7, -8)), shallow water near -300 to 0 in the south-west. Squares with a "no water" sentinel are few (3 of 81 near the origin in Tamriel). WhiterunWorld lends its land (PNAM land bit) to Tamriel, so most of its squares have no CELL of their own and read the parent's XCLW.
- The engine's water was ONE infinite plane at ONE height (`water/main.vert` builds it from the frustum). With Skyrim's per-square heights that would flood every lower square of the world, so a bounded surface was added.

### Design (smallest complete form)
- SDK: `world_water.hpp` (DRAFT 0.1): `CellWater` and `CellWaterSource`, a new interface found by `dynamic_cast` on the registered source (the `ActorAppearanceSource` precedent; the pinned `WorldCellSource` is not touched, `CI/check_extension_sdk.sh` passes).
- Pack: `SkyrimTerrainIndex::cellWater` merges each plugin's exterior CELL XCLW (later plugin wins, a deleted CELL goes), the world's DNAM water height and PNAM bits: own height, no-water sentinel, maximum float / no XCLW / no CELL record take the world default, the water bit takes the parent's default, a square the world does not list is read in a parent that lends its land.
- Core: `MWWorld::Cell(record, worldspace, optional<float>)`; `WorldModel::findNeutralExterior` asks the source once per new square (an empty square too, the sea needs no record) and logs `NWATER (x, y) water at H` at Info.
- Render: `Render::WaterCells` (pure bookkeeping), `MWRender::Water::setCellWater/removeCellWater/setGlobalVisible`: one `StateGroup` (the water pipeline with the new vertex mode `WATER_CELL` and the same bump map) with one `Anim::Transform` child per square (translation = the square's corner and height, scale = its size); `water/main.vert` places a unit quad by `pc.modelview` under `#ifdef WATER_CELL`, the fragment stage is the engine's own. `RenderManager::setNeutralCellWater/removeNeutralCellWater` hide the infinite plane and turn `Render_Water` on while any surface exists; `setWaterEnabled` (everything else) shows the plane again. `Scene::loadCell/unloadCell` call them only for a neutral exterior.
- Swimming: `World::isUnderwater(cell, pos)` already reads the cell's own water level, so it is right per square for free; the PHYSICS water level (`PhysicsSystem::enableWater`, one global height) is not used for neutral exteriors, so buoyancy and swim movement are a follow-up.
