# Task: Skyrim exteriors, water in the game (M-S2)
Design doc section: `docs/design/skyrim-se-implementation-plan.md` 4.5 (water; decisions of 2026-11-11 in section 7: D10 PBR-leaning look behind a setting, D13 full loop for engine work); `docs/tasks/0125-skyrim-exteriors-e2-terrain-in-engine.md` (the neutral exterior this builds on); `docs/tasks/0117-skyrim-exteriors-e0-decoding.md` (what XCLW and DNAM mean in the data). Approved under toddhow's standing "go with your recommendations".
Goal: a neutral Skyrim SE exterior draws water over each grid square at the square's water height (its own XCLW or the world's default, none where the data says none), with the engine's existing water shader.
Inherited code touched: `apps/openmw/mwworld/scene.cpp` (`Scene::loadCell` water block, `unloadCell`), `apps/openmw/mwworld/cell.{hpp,cpp}` (a third `Cell` constructor), `apps/openmw/mwworld/worldmodel.cpp` (`findNeutralExterior`), `apps/openmw/mwrender/water.{hpp,cpp}`, `apps/openmw/mwrender/rendermanager.{hpp,cpp}` (`setWaterEnabled`, two new functions), `components/pipeline/mode.hpp` and `graphics.cpp` (one vertex mode `WATER_CELL`), `files/shaders/water/main.vert` (one `#ifdef` path) and its recorded interface. Morrowind paths are untouched: the new code is reached only for a neutral exterior (`CellStore::isNeutral()`), and `setWaterEnabled` only gains "the infinite plane is visible again".
Inputs / outputs: new SDK header `sdk/include/openeden/game/world_water.hpp` (`CellWater`, `CellWaterSource`, DRAFT 0.1; a new interface found by `dynamic_cast` on the registered `WorldCellSource`, the precedent of `ActorAppearanceSource`, so the pinned `WorldCellSource` is not touched); `SkyrimTerrainIndex::cellWater` (the pack resolves the rules); `Render::WaterCells` (pure bookkeeping, `components/render/watercells.hpp`); `MWRender::Water::setCellWater`/`removeCellWater`/`setGlobalVisible`; `RenderManager::setNeutralCellWater`/`removeNeutralCellWater`.
Constraints: off by default (nothing runs without `[Game] neutral world` and a pack whose source implements `CellWaterSource`); main thread only; no per-frame allocation added.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The pack answers a square's water from the data's rules: own height, "no water" sentinels, the maximum float and a missing XCLW take the world default, the water bit of PNAM takes the parent's default, a square the world does not list is read in a parent that lends its land (tests that failed against stubs first)
- [ ] `Render::WaterCells` and `MWWorld::Cell` with a water height, and `WorldModel::findNeutralExterior` giving each cell its water (also an empty square) (tests that failed against stubs first)
- [ ] In a game, water is drawn at Whiterun's river or a lake, only inside its squares, and not over lower squares
- [ ] With the setting off and on, Morrowind is unchanged (`worldobjects_check` dump and the fixed Balmora frame match the parent build)
Out of scope (recorded as follow-ups in the stage log): swimming and buoyancy (the physics water level is one global height), waterfalls, WATR colours and fresnel/noise parameters beyond what the shader takes, the underwater view from beneath a cell surface, water in the LOD and the maps, a second pack's water.
