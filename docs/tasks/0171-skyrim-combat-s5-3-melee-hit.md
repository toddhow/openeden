# Task: Skyrim combat S5-3, melee hit and damage (first playable slice)
Design doc section: `docs/research/0056-skyrim-combat-survey.md` section 8 (row S5-3); decision `docs/decisions/0018-skyrim-combat-hooks.md`; contract `sdk/include/openeden/game/combat_rules.hpp` (DRAFT 0.2, additive over 0.1).
Goal: A Skyrim NPC and the player trade melee blows in the Bannered Mare: the clip's HitFrame asks for a sweep, the nearest body in the arc takes damage by the weapon, skill and armour formulas, an NPC plays a hit reaction, and a health that reaches 0 is a death, reported once. One-handed sword and unarmed, humanoids only.
Inherited code touched: none of OpenMW's logic. Edited OpenEden code: `apps/openmw/mwphysics/neutralcombatbridge.*` (sweep, damage, death, facing), new `neutralsweep.*`, `physicssystem.*` (the combat world, player hooks), `apps/openmw/mwworld/worldimp.cpp` (the player hooks: the engine's health), `apps/openmw/mwrender/skyrimpose.cpp` (a developer trace only), the pack's `combat.*`, `world_view.*` (the numbers), `neutral_world.hpp`, `skyrimse_game_pack.cpp`, `extension.json`. Base commit `847ac788e6` (combat S5-0, S5-1 and S5-2 merged on main's line).
Inputs / outputs: core to pack: `onClipEvent` (the HitFrame), `onSweepResult` (a frame after the request), `onClipFinished`, `onPlayerAttack`; pack to core: `requestSweep`, `reportDamage`, `reportDeath`, `playClip` (recoil, death), `faceHeading`, `subjectState`. SDK 0.2 adds `SubjectState`, `CombatRequestWriter::subjectState` and `faceHeading` (trailing virtuals, default refuse).
Constraints: main thread only; no allocation per frame in the sweep and delivery path (counted test); everything behind `[Game] neutral world`, `[Game] neutral actors`, `[Game] neutral combat` (off) and test settings that are all off.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Sweep geometry as a pure function (arc in and out, reach edge, attacker excluded, deterministic order, cap) with tests
- [x] Damage, health and stamina bookkeeping against hand-calculated numbers; death at 0 exactly once
- [x] The bridge casts at request time, tells the result a frame later before `combatUpdate`, never from inside a request; no per-frame allocation

Out of scope: blocking, bashing, power-attack input (S5-4), ragdolls (S5-5), bows and magic, inventory weapons and armour (S5-9), first-person arms, upper-body clips over locomotion (S5-9), ATKD per race.

## Design

- **Sweep (core).** `MWPhysics::NeutralSweep::arcSweep` is a pure function: a wedge of a disc (reach, half angle, a height band from a step below the feet to a head above) against upright capsules (radius 22, height 128 for actors, the same for the player). A body is hit when its surface is within the reach and the angle to its centre less the angle the body covers is inside the half angle; a body the attacker stands inside is always hit (distance 0). Nearest first, ties by kind (actors before the player), then placement id, then session, so the order never depends on the order the bodies are listed in; at most 16 hits, the rest counted. **It is a geometric test, not a Jolt shape cast** (neutral actors and the player are capsules the physics system already holds): no wall is looked for between attacker and target (decision 3 below).
- **Bridge.** `requestSweep` casts at the moment of the request (inside the pack's `combatUpdate`, main thread) against `NeutralCombatWorld::collectCandidates` (the neutral actors in `PhysicsSystem`, plus the player through the world's hook), drops the dead, keeps the result in a fixed table (64) and tells it at the start of the NEXT `update`, before the clip events and before `combatUpdate`. A result whose attacker has gone is dropped and counted. `reportDamage` forwards a hit on the player to `applyPlayerDamage`; `reportDeath` marks the actor dead once (a second report returns true and does nothing) and tells the world. A **Death-slot clip is not stopped when it ends**: the body keeps its last pose (the renderer already holds a clip's last pose until it is stopped).
- **Pack.** `SkyrimCombat` keeps health and stamina per fighter (actors seeded lazily from `CombatData::fighter`, the player from a stand-in: 100 health and stamina, skills 15, a Nord's unarmed hit; health regenerates at the race's rate, slower in combat per the GMSTs). On a Hit event of an attack slot it asks for a sweep (reach = WEAP reach x `fCombatDistance`, or the race's unarmed reach; half angle 35 degrees, ATKD +20 default), spends the attacker's stamina (clamped at 0: a swing is never refused) and, a frame later, damages the NEAREST valid target only (not the attacker, not the world, not a corpse): `computeMeleeHit` with the attacker's skill and the target's armour mitigation. An NPC target plays `1HM_RecoilRight` (its own swing is replaced); at 0 health the pack stops nothing but plays the stagger-back clip as a Death-slot clip and reports the death. The weapon is the WEAP named by `combat test weapon` (empty: unarmed); inventory weapons wait for S5-9.
- **Player.** The player has no clip (no first-person arms): its swing runs on a timer from the sidecar's numbers (HitFrame 0.367 s, length 1.3 s, constants in `combat.hpp` taken from the `1HM_AttackRight` trace of task 0170). It starts on `onPlayerAttack` or, with `combat test player swing = N`, every N seconds (first at 1 s); a press during a swing is dropped. **The player's health is the engine's own:** the pack reads it through `SubjectState` (the world reads `MWMechanics::CreatureStats::getHealth()` of the player's Ptr: current and modified) and `reportDamage` with `victimIsPlayer` makes the core write `healthAfter` back with `CreatureStats::setHealth` (`World::World`, the hooks in `setNeutralPlayerHooks`). The HUD's health bar follows (seen in the frames). If the engine has no maximum (0) the pack's stand-in is used; the engine's stub player has 100.
- **Hostile test.** `combat test hostile = <editor id>`: that actor attacks the player when the horizontal distance to the player is within reach + 18 (reach + body radius - 4), turns to face it (`faceHeading`, `atan2(dx, dy)`), and swings no more often than every 2.5 s. There is no pathing and no AI yet (S5-9).
- **Numbers.** `FighterProfile` per NPC from the actor and combat records (`SkyrimWorldView::fighter`): health and stamina totals and skills from `computeActorStats`, armour mitigation from `computeActorArmour` (default outfit), regeneration and unarmed damage and reach from the RACE. The combat records are read once on the first fight (a second read of every plugin, `CombatIndex`), like the actor index.
- **Threads.** All of the new callbacks run on the main thread: `onClipEvent`, `onSweepResult`, `onClipFinished`, `onPlayerAttack` and `combatUpdate` from `PhysicsSystem::updateNeutralActors` (called from `World::doPhysics`); the world's player hooks are called from inside `combatUpdate`, the same thread. Nothing runs on a physics or update worker.

## The sink (toddhow's report) and root motion

toddhow saw the NPC sink "to about the knees" during the swing. Measured, with `hkxconvert pose` on `1hm_attackright.hkx` and the male `skeleton.hkx` (world positions relative to the root, units): the toes stay at the floor for the whole clip, **toe height -0.4 to 1.8 at t = 0, 0.325, 0.65, 0.975** (left toe 0.05, 1.40, 1.78, 1.14; right toe 0.02, 0.06, 0.01, -0.42), the foot bones 6 to 10 above them; the centre of mass drops from 62.5 (t = 0) to 50.9 (t = 0.65) and comes back to 61.8 at t = 0.975 (the idle `mt_idle` holds it at 68.9). So the clip is authored with its feet on the floor and its lunge lowers the pelvis by up to 18 units: the figure in the old frame is the clip's crouch behind the bar counter, which hides the legs (the actor's origin and capsule were never lowered). **Nothing was changed in the placement**: compensating the pelvis would lift the feet off the floor. Shown with a frame where the whole body is in view, feet included: `I:\Projects\OpenEden-work\combat3\s6\user\screenshots\screenshot028.png` (crop `s6\crop28.png`: the deep lunge, a foot on the floor) and the contact sheet `s6\sheet.png`; the clip's first pose is 6.5 lower than the idle's (62.5 against 68.9), so the idle to clip switch pops down 6.5 units at the start (the renderer hard-switches; blending is a later stage).

**Root motion from the sidecar:** the sidecar's motion rows (`anims_defaultmale.txt`) of `1HM_AttackRight`, `1HM_AttackPower` and `1HM_AttackLeft` are ONE row each, `<length> 0 0 0`: the sword attacks do not move the root, so there is nothing to apply and no code path was built for it (it would never run). The clips that do move the root are the stagger-back (`1HM_StaggerBackMedium`: about -68 forward over 2 s, rows up to 0.97 s) and the bash (`1HM_BlockBash`, 238 forward): they belong to S5-4 and S5-5. The death clip used here plays in place, so the body's feet slide against the floor while it goes down. The developer trace `NPOSE` (OPENEDEN_NEUTRAL_ACTOR_TRACE) now samples the clip's root, centre of mass and pelvis at eight times.

## Decisions for toddhow to confirm
