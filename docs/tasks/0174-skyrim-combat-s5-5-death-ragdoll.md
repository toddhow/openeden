# Task: Skyrim combat S5-5, death and ragdoll
Design doc section: `docs/research/0056-skyrim-combat-survey.md` section 8 (row S5-5) and section 9 item 6 (a baked skeleton map is acceptable: toddhow, decided 2026-11-14); decision `docs/decisions/0018-skyrim-combat-hooks.md`; ragdoll infrastructure `docs/tasks/0106-creativemotion-m10-ragdolls-in-game.md`; skeleton maps `docs/tasks/0070-skeleton-mapping-for-extended-skeletons.md`, `docs/research/0036-extended-skeletons-measured.md`.
Approval: toddhow, 2026-11-15, "start on s5-5". Full loop (ragdolls, `mwphysics`, SDK untouched).
Goal: With `[Game] neutral combat` and `[Physics] creativemotion ragdolls` on, a Skyrim neutral actor (humanoid) that the pack reports dead becomes a CreativeMotion ragdoll in the game's one Jolt world: it starts in the pose it died in, falls, settles, and its corpse is moved to where it lies. Everything else (creatures, no bone table, either setting off) keeps today's behaviour: the death clip's last pose.
Inherited code touched: `apps/openmw/mwphysics/physicssystem.{hpp,cpp}` (`remove`, `updateNeutralActors`, `NeutralCombatWorldImpl::actorDied`, new `startNeutralActorRagdoll`, `forEachNeutralRagdoll`, `restNeutralCorpse`), `apps/openmw/mwrender/actorragdolldriver.{hpp,cpp}` (additive overloads taking a bone set; the Morrowind constructors delegate with Morrowind's set, unchanged behaviour), `apps/openmw/mwrender/scene.{hpp,cpp}`, `renderinginterface.hpp` (four virtuals with do-nothing defaults), `skyrimactor.{hpp,cpp}` (`readSkeletonPlan`), `apps/openmw/mwworld/worldimp.{hpp,cpp}` (`doPhysics`). New: `apps/openmw/mwrender/skyrimragdollmap.*`, `skyrimragdoll.*`. Base commit `5b73d81d2c`.
Inputs / outputs: no SDK change. Core side only: `CombatRequestWriter::reportDeath` (already built, S5-3) -> `NeutralCombatWorldImpl::actorDied` -> a queue of dead `Ptr`s -> `World::doPhysics`.

## Survey (what exists, with file and function)
- **Death is reported and held.** `NeutralCombatBridge::Writer::doReportDeath` (`neutralcombatbridge.cpp`) records the actor in `mDead` and calls `NeutralCombatWorld::actorDied(key, handle)`; the physics side's implementation (`PhysicsSystem::NeutralCombatWorldImpl::actorDied`) only cleared the actor's movement request (S5-3). The pack plays the Death-slot clip, which `SkyrimPose::Controller::playOnce` holds in its last pose (`skyrimpose.cpp`). This is the "today's behaviour" a failed ragdoll falls back to.
- **A neutral actor is not an `MWPhysics::Actor`.** It is an upright capsule `MWPhysics::Object` on `Layers::WORLD` (`PhysicsSystem::addNeutralActor`), with a `NeutralActorEntry` in `mNeutralActors` (`physicssystem.hpp`). So `PhysicsSystem::startActorRagdoll` (Morrowind: `mActors.find(ptr.mRef)`) refuses it, and a neutral actor has no `CharacterController`, hence no `CharacterController::updateRagdoll`.
- **The ragdoll machinery is generic enough.** `RagdollHost::spawnActor(feet, yaw, velocity, error, pose)`, `readActor`, `actorStatus`, `removeActor` (`ragdollhost.hpp`); `mActorRagdolls` (keyed by `Ptr::mRef`) and `PhysicsSystem::remove` -> `stopActorRagdoll`; `decideCorpseRest` (`mwmechanics/corpserest.hpp`, pure); `MWRender::ActorRagdollDriver` writing mapped bones through `BonePose` (`actorragdolldriver.cpp`), `makeActorStartPose`, `ActorRagdollRest`.
- **But the driver and the rest/pose helpers have Morrowind's 17 bone names in a static table** (`sMappings`: "bip01 pelvis" ...) and the pelvis' parent hard-coded as "bip01" (`sRootBone`); without a parent the pelvis would silently stop translating (`mHasPelvisTranslation`). The 17 rig bone names equal the 17 core roles of a skeleton map (`Body::canonicalRoles()`: Pelvis, Spine, Chest, Neck, Head, LeftUpperArm ... RightFoot).
- **How a Skyrim neutral actor is drawn and posed.** `SkyrimActor::buildActorNode` (`skyrimactor.cpp`) makes the skeleton as named `Anim::Transform` nodes in the rest pose plus skinned parts, `MWAnim::create` gives an `ObjectAnimation` with `Anim::Bones`; `attachNeutralIdle` (`scene.cpp`) adds the idle / locomotion controller and then the `SkyrimPose` controller (furniture and combat clips) to the object, kept alive in `Object::keepAlive`; clips decode on workers, `run()` of a controller may be on an update thread (`SkyrimPose` documents it).
- **Skeleton map tools (task 0070).** `Body::suggestSkeletonMap` (`components/body/skeletonsuggest.hpp`) turns a skeleton's rest bones into a `SkeletonMap` whose ROLES are the 17 core roles plus optional ones, resolved by names and tokens; `writeSkeletonMap` / `readSkeletonMapFile` give the canonical `openeden.skeletonmap` v1 text; `skeletonDigest` ties a map to its skeleton. Extras stay suggestions (`accepted=false`) until a person accepts them.
- **Physics layers.** `JoltObjectLayerPairFilter`: `RAGDOLL` meets `WORLD`, `HEIGHTMAP`, `DOOR` and `RAGDOLL`; `DEBRIS` meets only `WORLD` and `HEIGHTMAP`; a `DEBRIS` body is in broad phase `DEBRIS`, which `RAGDOLL` does not visit (`joltlayers.hpp`). The Morrowind corpse capsule is moved to `DEBRIS` by `Actor::enableCollisionBody`.

## Design
1. **Trigger.** `NeutralCombatWorldImpl::actorDied`, only with `[Physics] creativemotion ragdolls`, pushes the actor's `Ptr` on `mNeutralDeaths` (reserved when an actor is added, cleared at the start of `updateNeutralActors`). `World::doPhysics` reads it right after `updateNeutralActors` (so before the step) and starts the ragdoll that frame. Main thread throughout.
2. **Bone table = baked skeleton map.** `MWRender::SkyrimRagdoll::makeBoneTable(plan, folder)` (`skyrimragdollmap.*`): reads the skeleton plan of the actor's skeleton NIF (`SkyrimActor::readSkeletonPlan`), runs `Body::suggestSkeletonMap` on its rest bones (no skin weights, no role table), writes the map to `<user data>/openeden/skeletonmaps/<first 16 hex of the skeleton digest>.skeletonmap.json` (canonical text; a person may correct a role and the correction wins on the next run), and uses only the map's ROLES (accepted by construction). The extras are never accepted or consumed (nothing here accepts a suggestion silently). A file with another digest or profile, or one that cannot be read, is baked again with a warning. A table needs the pelvis and at least 10 of the 17 rig bones (the Morrowind driver's rule); otherwise no table: the actors of that skeleton keep the death clip's last pose, one warning per skeleton and session. One table per skeleton file and process (a mutex-guarded map in `scene.cpp`), made on the main thread at the first actor of that skeleton. Nothing is committed: the map is derived from the user's own skeleton and lives in the user data folder.
3. **The controller.** `SkyrimRagdoll::Controller` (`skyrimragdoll.*`) is added to every drawn neutral actor that has a table, AFTER the idle, locomotion and pose controllers (so its bone writes win), only with `[Game] neutral combat` and `[Physics] creativemotion ragdolls`. At construction (the object was just built: bones in the rest pose) it takes the `ActorRagdollRest` snapshot of the mapped bones. Until armed it does nothing (a mutex lock and a flag test). On death the main thread (a) asks it for the start pose (`startPose`: the mapped bones now against their rest, via `makeActorStartPose`), (b) PhysicsSystem builds the ragdoll in that pose, (c) `arm` creates the `ActorRagdollDriver` for this skeleton's bone set (the driver captures its per-bone offsets from the ragdoll's first state and the bones as they are, so the first frame draws the pose the actor had: no pop). Each frame, after `stepSimulation`, the main thread copies the ragdoll's bodies into the controller's `Snapshot` (`setStates`, under a mutex); `run()` (an update thread) applies them through the driver. `run()` never touches the physics world and does no bone lookup; it allocates nothing.
4. **The driver** gains a `RagdollBoneSet` (a span of `{rig bone, engine bone}` plus the pelvis' parent bone name); the old constructors delegate with Morrowind's set, so the Morrowind driver, its 17-entry table and its tests are unchanged. An entry with an empty engine bone is skipped (a bone the skeleton lacks), as a bone the actor lacks already was.
5. **The corpse's own capsule.** The capsule is a `WORLD` body and `RAGDOLL` meets `WORLD`: left alone it would hold its own corpse up like an invisible pillar (the start placement would also push the ragdoll out of it). `startNeutralActorRagdoll`, under the simulation mutex and before the spawn, moves the capsule to `DEBRIS` (restored to `WORLD` when the spawn fails). The combat sweeps are geometric and skip the dead; the line-of-sight ray ignores neutral bodies; a `DEBRIS` capsule is not met by the player or the mover. Other living neutral capsules remain `WORLD` and do collide with the ragdoll (limit).
6. **The corpse at rest.** While an entry holds a ragdoll, `updateNeutralActors` skips its fall and walk code (as for a seated actor) and every 10 frames asks `restNeutralCorpse`: when every body sleeps (`actorRagdollAtRest`) it applies Morrowind's pure rule `MWMechanics::decideCorpseRest` (settled, 0.35 m to 40 m, ground found by a ray from the pelvis) and, if it says move, sets the `Ptr`'s position and puts the actor on `mNeutralMoved`, which `World::doPhysics` already turns into `mRendering->moveObject` + `mPhysics->updatePosition`. The driver draws the bones at the ragdoll's absolute world place (the pelvis' local translation is computed from its parent's current world matrix every frame), so moving the root does not move the drawn body. `World::moveObject` is NOT used (it looks a neutral id up in the ESM store).
7. **Lifetime.** `PhysicsSystem::remove` (object branch) now ends the actor's ragdoll before the entry goes (cell unload, disable, delete). A ragdoll the host removes on its own (kill depth) is reported once by `forEachNeutralRagdoll` with `alive == false`; the controller is disarmed and the bones keep the last pose written; the capsule stays non-colliding. Not saved (decision 0005 spirit, as Morrowind's ragdolls): a loaded corpse is not re-ragdolled; neutral sessions refuse saving anyway (N6).
8. **Threads.** `actorDied`, `startNeutralActorRagdoll`, `forEachNeutralRagdoll`, `restNeutralCorpse`, `World::startNeutralRagdoll` / `updateNeutralRagdolls`, `Controller::startPose` / `arm` / `setStates` / `disarm`: main thread, in `World::doPhysics`. `Controller::run`: an update thread or the main thread; it takes the controller's mutex, then the snapshot's, and reads no physics state. Spawning takes `PhysicsTaskScheduler::getSimulationMutex()` like Morrowind's.
9. **Allocation.** Per frame with a live ragdoll: `forEachNeutralRagdoll` (a `std::function` over a two-pointer lambda: no allocation) and `readActorRagdoll` into a stack array; `Snapshot::set/get` copy 16 states; `Controller::run` allocates nothing (the driver is the Morrowind one). Spawning allocates once per death.
10. **Determinism.** No clock or randomness is added. The start pose comes from the bones the renderer last wrote (clip decode timing is a known source of run-to-run difference in the combat traces, see task 0171 S5-3b); the trace of a repeat is checked three times in game (below).
11. **Settings.** No new setting: the two existing ones, both off by default (`[Game] neutral combat`, `[Physics] creativemotion ragdolls`, both already in `tools/testprofile/profile.cfg`; the ragdoll one is on its coverage list). With either off the code path is one setting read at actor insert (the controller is not made) and one more `mNeutralDeaths` test per death.

## Out of scope
Creatures and other non-humanoid skeletons (no table), the player's death, a death clip played before the ragdoll (the start pose is the actor's pose at the report), partial ragdolls (hit reactions), get-up, ragdoll saves, colliding with living neutral capsules (the ragdoll passes through them... they are `WORLD` bodies and DO block it: see limits), a role table supplied by the pack (the suggestion engine is used alone: see the stage log for what that resolved), accepting the map's extra entries.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] The table from a Skyrim-named skeleton resolves the 17 rig bones; no pelvis, a creature's skeleton and fewer than 10 bones give no table; a missing bone is listed and the rest work (unit tests).
- [x] The baked map is written to a file named by the skeleton digest and read back; a person's correction wins; a damaged or foreign file is baked again; an unwritable folder still gives a table (unit tests).
- [x] The controller draws the first frame as the actor was, then follows the ragdoll's rotations, translates the pelvis through its real parent bone, stops when disarmed, writes nothing unarmed, refuses too few states and a ragdoll without a pelvis body (unit tests).
- [x] A capsule on `WORLD` holds a ragdoll built inside it out; on `DEBRIS` it does not (Jolt unit test).
- [x] Morrowind's driver, ragdoll host and corpse rule tests pass unedited.
- [x] In game (Bannered Mare, `tools/neutral_session_check` combat scenario, fixed frame time): Hulda is killed, falls and settles; frames viewed; three runs give a byte-identical trace.
- [x] Off means unchanged: with `[Physics] creativemotion ragdolls` off, or `[Game] neutral combat` off, no ragdoll, no controller, and the S5-4 trace lines equal the parent build's; Morrowind: `tools/worldobjects_check` equal, the fixed Balmora frame hash equal to the parent's with the settings off and on.

## Stage log (2026-11-15; same hand wrote tests and code, no fresh reviewer; Windows only, local commit on the worktree branch, not pushed)

Plain words: with `[Game] neutral combat` and `[Physics] creativemotion ragdolls` on, a Skyrim humanoid the pack kills (Hulda in the Bannered Mare) crumples from the pose she died in, falls onto the floor and lies there; her skeleton follows the ragdoll through a skeleton map that the engine bakes from the user's own skeleton file. Creatures, a skeleton with no map, or either setting off keep the death clip's last pose.

- **Real skeleton (measured in game).** The suggestion engine, with no role table and no skin weights, resolved **17 of 17 rig bones on both Skyrim skeletons** (`skeleton.nif`, 99 bones, digest `1b678ffe4a90648b`; `skeleton_female.nif`, digest `c6930063234a1f2f`), e.g. Pelvis = `NPC Pelvis [Pelv]`, Spine = `NPC Spine [Spn0]`, **Chest = `NPC Spine1 [Spn1]`** (`NPC Spine2 [Spn2]` is not mapped and keeps its clip rotation under the chest). So no pack role table and no SDK change were needed. The maps are written to `<user data>/openeden/skeletonmaps/<digest16>.skeletonmap.json` (canonical `openeden.skeletonmap` v1, extras unaccepted and never used). Never committed.
- **Two faults found only by running the game (both are now fixed and have a test).** (1) A controller that is not auto-play is never run for a neutral actor, so the first build drew nothing (the unit tests call `run()` directly): `hints.autoPlay = true`, test `itAsksToBeRunEveryFrameLikeTheOtherNeutralActorControllers` (written after the finding, not run red). (2) **In Skyrim's skeleton the spine is the pelvis' SIBLING** (path `NPC Root > NPC COM > NPC Spine`, and `NPC COM > NPC Pelvis`): the Morrowind driver translates the pelvis bone, so the legs followed the ragdoll and the whole upper body stayed behind, standing in the air. Found with the new `RAGDOLLDRAW` trace (drawn against ragdoll positions of eight bones: the pelvis matched to 0.001, the spine's drawn position did not move at all). New `RagdollBoneSet::mTranslateRootBone` (Skyrim true, Morrowind false): the pelvis' PARENT takes the translation, placed so that the pelvis origin lands on the ragdoll's pelvis, and everything under it goes along. The test `thePelvisFollowsTheRagdollThroughItsRealParentBone` was extended (spine and head must move with it) and was RED with the flag false (1 of 23 failed), green with it true.
- **Tests (23 new, `openmw-tests`, `apps/openmw_tests/mwrender/testskyrimragdoll.cpp`).** `SkyrimRagdollMap` 11 (all 17 bones; no pelvis, a creature, fewer than 10 bones; one missing bone listed; bake to a digest-named file and read back; a person's correction wins; a damaged file baked again; an unwritable folder still gives a table; digests; the rotation of rest bones), `SkyrimRagdollSnapshot` 1, `SkyrimRagdollController` 9, `SkyrimRagdollLayers` 2 (ragdoll against `WORLD` and `DEBRIS`; a capsule on `WORLD` pushes a ragdoll built in it 5+ units aside, on `DEBRIS` it does not). **Red first (partly):** the tests were written after the first code, then run red against hand-written stubs of the two new `.cpp` files (nothing mapped, nothing armed): **18 of the first 22 failed**; the 4 that passed are two absence tests (a stub's null equals the expected null for no pelvis and for a creature) and the two layer tests, which pin existing physics. After the code: 22 of 22 passed. Then 1 failed red, as above, and the autoPlay test was added. `openmw-tests` whole: **2,613 tests, 2,611 pass, 2 skipped** (data-gated real-data tests); the Morrowind ragdoll tests (`ActorRagdoll*`, `RagdollBone*`, `RagdollTrigger*`, 86 with the new ones) pass unedited. No existing test was edited.
- **In game** (`tools/neutral_session_check` `n5trade` and the new `n5death`, Bannered Mare, `OPENEDEN_FIXED_FRAME_TIME`, `combat test hostile = Hulda`, build `I:\Projects\OpenEden-work\ragdoll5\build`): Hulda dies at the tenth hit (`NCOMBAT death`), `Ragdoll started` the same frame, **every body asleep after 415 steps (6.917 s)**, lowest and highest body centre 70.1 and 87.8 units (spawn pelvis height 69, so she lies on the floor), pelvis at rest (318.892, -419.792, 78.108), 17.1 units from where she died, under `decideCorpseRest`'s 0.35 m = 24.5 units, so "Corpse ... stays where it died" (the move itself is covered by Morrowind's tests of the same pure rule and was not seen here).
