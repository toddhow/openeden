# Task 0204: Skyrim crime for the player (theft, assault, murder, witnesses, bounty, guards, jail)

Design doc section: decision 0018 (combat); follow-up of task 0197 (faction combat, guards, sight), task 0182/0184 (inventory), task 0189 (barter).
Approval: toddhow, 2026-11-15/16, "as much Skyrim work as possible overnight".
Goal: the player's crimes are noticed by witnesses, cost bounty in the witness's crime faction, mark stolen goods that vendors refuse, and bring a guard who offers pay, jail or resist; the state is saved.
Inherited code touched: none. OpenEden: `game_packs/skyrimse` (new `crime.*`, `crime_service.*`; hook edits in `actors.*` (FACT crime data), `combat.hpp`/`combat_ai_run.cpp` (roster and guard moves), `combat_ai.hpp`, `inventory_service.*` (one hook), `barter_service.*` (one hook), `world_view.*` (owner lookup, jail destination), `neutral_world.hpp` (wrapper in the combat chain), `skyrimse_game_pack.cpp`, `extension.json`), Lua `files/data/scripts/openeden/skyrimse/crime_*.lua`, `files/data/openeden/skyrimse/crime_windows.lua`, `builtin.omwscripts`. Not touched: `crafting*`, map and compass files, `papyrus_host*`, the world source service, lockpicking and book files.
Constraints: everything behind `[extension.skyrimse] crime` (default off, declared in `extension.json`; needs `inventory`-side `neutral inventory` for theft and `combat ai` plus `combat factions` for the guards). With it off every trace equals the parent's (`1e1a1c110c`). Morrowind (which has its own crime system) is not touched.

## 2. Data (Skyrim.esm, aggregates, `I:\Projects\OpenEden-work\combatai\py\crime1.py`, `crime2.py`, `crime3.py`)

* FACT subrecords: CRVA 1,083 of 1,084 (sizes 20: 969, 16: 112, 12: 2), JAIL 31, WAIT 34, STOL 12, PLCN 12, CRGR 48 (an FLST), JOUT 33 (an OTFT), all 4 bytes each except CRVA. Every JAIL, WAIT, STOL and PLCN target is a REFR, CRGR an FLST, JOUT an OTFT (counted, 0 other). JAIL exists only with CRVA (0 without).
* **CRVA layout, settled by the hold factions** (`CrimeFactionWhiterun`: `01 01 e803 2800 0500 1900 .. 0000003f 6400 e803`): arrest u8 at 0, attack on sight u8 at 1, murder u16 at 2 (1000), assault u16 at 4 (40), trespass u16 at 6 (5), pickpocket u16 at 8 (25), one u16 at 10 (0, but 16128 in 2 factions: garbage, not read), steal multiplier f32 at 12 (0.5), escape u16 at 16 (100), werewolf u16 at 18 (1000; matches GMST `iCrimeGoldWerewolf` 1000 and `iCrimeGoldStealHorse` 100). The 16 byte form (112) stops after the multiplier, the 12 byte one (2) after pickpocket and the unused word: every missing field reads 0. The names of the two flag bytes are RECALLED-VERIFY (CK crime values: Arrest, Attack on Sight); the numbers are the data's. The 8 hold factions with real values carry (1000, 40, 5, 25, 0.5, 100, 1000); 926 factions have 1,1 and zeros (all other crime-less factions).
* `CrimeFactionWhiterun` (0x267ea): JAIL 0x267e5 (a REFR of base 0x4 with XTEL: a teleport marker whose destination is the jail), WAIT 0x267e6 (`WhiterunJailFollowerWaitMarker`), STOL 0x9dc64 (base `EvidenceChestStolenGoods`), PLCN 0x267e8 (`WhiterunJailEvidenceChest`), JOUT jail outfit.
* GMSTs (crime-named, 5 masters): `iCrimeGoldWerewolf` 1000, `iCrimeGoldStealHorse` 100, `iGuardWarnings` 2, `fGuardPackageAttackRadiusMult` 0.5, `fKarmaModStealing` -5. No GMST for the bounty of theft, assault or murder: those are the CRVA fields.
* Owned containers: the Bannered Mare holds one (REFR 0x9cafb, owner 0x5091b); the Whiterun street guards have no owned containers near them. A guard of the hold's crime faction stands in 16 interior cells with owned containers (Dragonsreach basement, Jorrvaskr).

## 3. Design

**Pure rules** (`crime.hpp/.cpp`; standard library, the SDK combat types and save chunk types only; no clock, no records):
* `CrimeValues` (the CRVA fields above) and the faction's `JAIL/WAIT/STOL/PLCN/JOUT` form ids, in `FactionRecord::mCrime`.
* `bountyFor(Kind, values, itemValue, count)`: Murder, Assault, Trespass are the CRVA numbers; Theft is `ceil(value * count * stealMultiplier)` and at least 1 for a stolen item of value above 0 (RECALLED-VERIFY: the vanilla formula; the multiplier and the values are the data's, the rounding and the minimum are OURS). Non-finite or negative multipliers read as 0.
* `Ledger`: bounty per crime faction (capped at 1,000,000 a faction), stolen counts per item, a canonical save form (`crime` chunk, version 1: faction count, pairs ascending; item count, pairs ascending; caps; a truncated or over-cap chunk changes nothing).
* `witnessSees(settings, observer, crimePosition, lineClear)`: alive, not an enemy of the player's side nor engaged against the player, within `witnessDistance` (OURS 2,000 units: vanilla uses detection and sight range; RECALLED-VERIFY) and `witnessHeight` (200) of the crime, the line clear. A guard witnesses too.
* `CrimeSystem` (the same file pair, a state machine over an abstract `CrimeWorld` the engine side implements): `onTheft`, `onDamage`, `onDeath`, `update(seconds, writer, world)`; pending crimes are evaluated inside the next `combatUpdate` (rays only work there) against the roster; a witness's `mCrimeFaction` (NPC_ CRIF) gets the bounty. An unwitnessed theft still marks the item stolen and costs nothing. A crime against a hostile (an enemy of the player's side, or an actor already engaged against the player) is no crime. Followers hit by the player count as crimes (they are citizens).
* Guards: when a faction has a bounty and a guard of that crime faction (CRIF equal) is loaded within `guardCallRadius` (OURS 6,000), it walks to the player (run beyond 500), and within `talkDistance` (OURS 220) it stops and the state is `offer`. A bounty at or above `attackOnSightBounty` (OURS 5,000; all hold factions say "attack on sight = 1", which cannot distinguish) goes straight to `resisted`. Offer time out and give up rules below.
* Choices: **pay** removes the bounty gold from the player's inventory (refused without enough gold), clears the faction's bounty and confiscates the stolen items; **jail** confiscates, clears the bounty and sends the player to the jail destination for `jailSeconds` (OURS 6 s of game; vanilla advances days) then back; **resist** makes the guard (and guards within `guardCallRadius`) hostile through the faction AI and keeps the bounty. After a choice the guard is released; a refused offer repeats at most each `offerCooldown` (OURS 20 s). Murder and an attack on a guard do not need a witness-faction guard: any guard of the faction that sees it comes.

**Pack integration**
* `FactionRecord::mCrime` decoded in `readFaction`; `AiProfile::mCrimeFaction` filled in `SkyrimWorldView::fighter` from the NPC's CRIF; `SkyrimCombat::crimeActors`, `crimeGoTo`, `crimeStop`, `crimeProvoke` (public, thin: they use the roster, the intent queue and `aiBegin` against the player).
* `CrimeWriter` is the OUTERMOST wrapper of the combat chain in `neutral_world.hpp` (it must see a blow before `AiDamageWriter` provokes the victim); it adds no virtual, so `HudWriter`, `AiDamageWriter` and `ScriptEventWriter` needed no new forwarding.
* Theft: `InventoryService::Hooks::mTaken(owner, item, count)` is called by the `transfer` and `take_all` bindings after a successful move from a non-player owner of kind `container` (not a corpse) to the player; the pack asks `SkyrimWorldView::ownerOfPlacement` (a map filled by `loadCell`) whether the placement is owned by someone other than the player and, if so, calls `CrimeSystem::onTheft`. Pickup of ground items does not exist in the neutral world yet (not built).
* Barter: `BarterService::ServiceHooks::mStolenCount(item)`; `sell` refuses an item whose stolen count is positive unless the vendor's `mBuysStolen`; the ledger forgets stolen counts the player no longer holds.
* Window: the Lua player script opens a small window when `skyrimse.crime.state()` is `offer`; three buttons call global events that call `crime.choose`. The jail destination and the teleport are in the global script.
* Bindings `skyrimse.crime` 1.0: Read `state`, `bounty`, `bounty_total`, `guard_name`, `can_pay`, `stolen_count`, `message`, `revision`, `jail_cell`, `jail_position`; Mutate `choose(string)`, `released()`.

**Decisions and limits for toddhow.**
1. The vanilla guard dialogue (Papyrus fragments such as `SendPlayerToJail`) is not used: a pack-side window with three buttons. Vanilla topics would need the conditions world to answer `GetCrimeGold` and the Papyrus natives `PayFine`/`SendPlayerToJail`.
2. Murder: a calm NPC killed with a lethal first blow, or after an assault, is a murder; killing one that has hurt the player is self defence and no murder (a fleeing victim that never hit back is murdered). Unclear in vanilla (RECALLED-VERIFY).
3. Theft is only taking from an owned container through the window; ground items cannot be picked up in the neutral world yet; pickpocketing and trespass are not built; a corpse is never theft.
4. All numbers that are ours (witness distance 2,000 and height 200, guard radius 6,000, talk distance 220, attack-on-sight bounty 5,000, jail 6 s of game time, cooldown 20 s, timeouts 40 and 120 s) are named constants in `Settings`; none is a pack setting yet.
5. Paying and jail both confiscate every stolen item (removed, not moved to the STOL chest); jail does not advance days, take skills or dress the player in the jail outfit; the bounty is per witness crime faction (an unwitnessed theft marks the item and costs nothing).
6. Resist makes every loaded guard of the faction hostile; after 20 s the state lapses, the bounty stays and the guard offers again; the attack-on-sight flag is not used (all hold factions set it); no escape from jail, no guard warnings (`iGuardWarnings` unused).
7. Guards need `combat ai` (resist) and the Lua window needs `[GUI] rmlui`; `crime test guard = <editor id>` is a proof aid (the Mare has no guard).
8. The test frames show the window and the jail, not the guard's face: the scenario camera looks at Hulda and the guard walks up behind the player.
9. Murder was not run in game (a lethal kill needs the player to out-damage Hulda); it is covered by unit tests only.
