## 3. What is ours (named constants in `combat_ai.hpp`, none from the data)

Decision period 0.5 s; sight distance 1,800 (and within 200 in height: no walls are known to the pack, so a hostile in the next room can engage); retarget a goal at most every 0.4 s and when the target moved 48 units; run beyond 500 units; block hold 0.8 to 1.6 s; circle hold 1.0 to 2.0 s; fallback hold 0.6 s and step 160; the flee health fraction by confidence 0..4 = 1.0 / 0.5 / 0.25 / 0.1 / never (a coward flees as soon as it is engaged); the attack weights and the power and bash chances (section 4); the 60 percent of `iAICombatRestoreHealthPercentage` ends a flee only when health regenerates to it (it does not in combat: `fCombatHealthRegenRateMult` 0); a flee is over when the player is `fFleeDistanceInterior` (interior) away, we use 3000 everywhere (the pack does not know exterior from interior in this call: noted).

## 4. Design

**Records.** `combat_ai.*` (new, pure): `CombatStyleRecord`, `decodeCombatStyle` (a CSTY record), `AiProfile` (aggression, confidence, `hostileOnSight`, the style's numbers with defaults). `CombatRecords::mStyles` / `CombatIndex::style(FormId)` are added in `combat_stats.*` (the walker takes CSTY). `FighterProfile::mAi` carries the profile; `SkyrimWorldView::fighter` fills it from the resolved NPC's AIDT, ZNAM and `computeActorDisposition`.

**Pure decision.** `aiThink(AiActor&, const AiView&, const AiParams&) -> AiOut`. `AiActor` is the per-actor state (mode, timers, decision counter, seed, circle direction, flee attempts, cornered, block hold). Every random number is `splitmix64(seed + counter)` with the seed from the actor's reference and a per-engagement number, the counter advanced per roll, time only from the pack's combat clock (the `mClock` that does not advance while the data is not ready, task 0183). Modes: Advance (closing in; ranged users stop at 0.8 of their range), Duel (in reach: roll every decision: attack, block, circle, fallback, wait, cast), Block (guard up for the hold, then a new decision), Circle, Fallback, Retreat (flee), Lost (calm again). A swing of the player starts a decision at once (a reactive block), the other decisions are periodic. Weights (ours, scaled by the CSTY numbers): attack = max(0.05, offensive) x (stamina fraction above 0.15 ? 1 : 0.2); block = defensive x (target swinging ? 2 : 0.5) x (stamina fraction above 0.1 ? 1 : 0); circle = circle mult x (dueling flag ? 1 : 0.5); fallback = fallback mult x (stamina fraction below 0.3 ? 2 : 0.5) x (dueling flag ? 1 : 0); wait 0.15. Attack kind: bash with chance min(0.9, 0.5 x bash mult) when the target is blocking else min(0.5, 0.1 x bash mult); power with chance 0.25 (stamina fraction above 0.5, else 0.05) x (target blocking ? power blocking mult : 1); the every-N test settings override the choice when set. A cast (when the NPC has an aimed fire-and-forget spell and the magicka for it) is chosen outside melee reach with chance min(0.5, 0.15 x magic score).

**Engagement.** An actor engages when (a) it is named by `combat test hostile` (test override), or (b) its AIDT/factions make it hostile on sight and the player is within sight, polled twice a second per such actor, or (c) the player hurt it (any hit, arrow or spell damage resolved through `applyHit`; an unaggressive actor fights back unless its confidence is 0, which makes it flee). The target is always the player (no NPC against NPC). It ends when the player dies, the NPC dies, a flee reaches the flee distance, or the actor is removed.

**Movement** (new SDK use, no SDK change): the AI writes `AiMoveIntent`s (Stop, GoTo with a speed), `SkyrimCellSource::update` drains them after `mActors.update` and calls `goTo` / `cancelGoal`; the brain is suspended by `onConversation(ref, true)` at engage and resumed with `false` at lost, so a package walker stops and takes its package up again afterwards (a one frame delay: the combat update of frame N is acted on in the actor update of frame N+1). `onGoalFinished` is forwarded to the AI (a failed flee path moves to the next candidate; all failed: cornered, the actor fights). Limits: no strafe or backward clips (the locomotion controller plays the forward cycle: a circling actor walks along the circle and turns to face the player before each swing), no navmesh query (flee candidates are tried in turn), no line of sight.

**Block.** A raised guard is the rules' `isBlocking` (the AI's state, beside the named test blockers) plus the clip `1HM_BlockIdle` played once on top of the idle (`ClipSlot::BlockStart`, `SlotTable` entry added): it exists in `Skyrim - Animations.bsa` (`actors\character\animations\1hm_blockidle.hkx`), and is replayed while the guard stays up.

**Settings.** `[extension.skyrimse] combat ai` (bool, off): switches the AI on. With it on the test settings stay overrides: `combat test hostile` names forced aggressors, `combat test power every` / `bash every` fix the attack kind, `combat test block` makes the named actors block always. With it off every existing trace is the parent's. The engine bridge needs no change.

**Decisions and limits for toddhow.**
1. The rule that combines the style numbers (weights, periods, the flee table, 15 s of rest after giving up, six failed goals) is OURS; everything is a named constant in `combat_ai.hpp`.
2. **Nothing was changed outside the new files except hook calls** in `combat.cpp`, a `FighterProfile` field, `world_view.cpp` (the profile), `neutral_world.hpp` (drain, forward, the damage wrapper), the `extension.json` setting. `combat_magic*`, the render side, `papyrus_host*`, `quest*`, `dialogue*` and `inventory*` are untouched.
3. A flee in an interior never reaches `fFleeDistanceInterior` 3000, so it ends by a recovered health (60 percent, `iAICombatRestoreHealthPercentage`: it regenerates outside the 10 s combat window), by the player coming near (cornered: it fights) or not at all (it waits).
4. Limits: the target is always the player; no line of sight (a hostile in the next room can engage; sight is 1,800 units and 200 in height); no strafe or backward clips; NPC casting uses an aimed fire and forget spell with a hostile first effect from the NPC's own spell list (none of the 6,361 was checked for having one) and was not run in game; the bow path of the AI (an archer closes to 0.8 of its range) was not run in game; a conversation that ends with an engaged actor resumes its package (the pack's own `onConversation`) mid-fight; a seated or sleeping NPC that engages is stood up by the brain's talk handling (a sitter stays seated and so does not move: not run); the player has no block input, so the AI's view of a blocking target is always false.
5. NPC against NPC does not exist (the crowd rules of another task are untouched); with the AI on, damage the player does by any means provokes (a spell through the wrapper was not run in game).
