# Task: Skyrim real weapons and shields in combat (task 0214)
Design doc section: `docs/research/0056-skyrim-combat-survey.md` sections 6 and 8; decision `docs/decisions/0018-skyrim-combat-hooks.md`; tasks 0170 (clip slots), 0171 (melee), 0172 (block, bash), 0184 (the player's equipment), 0190 (AI), 0193 (sounds).
Goal: with `[Game] neutral combat` and the new pack setting `[extension.skyrimse] combat real gear` a fighter uses the weapon and shield of its own inventory and outfit, plays the clips of that weapon's class, and the player's equipped weapon of any melee type works the same way. Off by default; with it off every combat trace equals the parent's.
Inherited code touched: none of OpenMW's. OpenEden code: `game_packs/skyrimse/{include/game_skyrimse,src}/combat*.{hpp,cpp}`, `combat_gear.{hpp,cpp}` (new), `world_view.*` (the gear lookup), `actors.*` (ARMO `BAMT`), `skyrimse_game_pack.cpp`, `extension.json`, and `apps/openmw/mwrender/skyrimclipmeta.cpp` (the sidecar name of a clip).
Constraints: main thread only; no allocation per frame (the gear is looked up once per fighter, in `ready()`); no clock, no random number; the SDK is not changed (the contract stays 0.x as it is); every writer wrapper is untouched because no virtual is added to `CombatRequestWriter`.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The best melee weapon and the best shield of an NPC come from a deterministic rule (below), unit tested on records
- [x] Clip tables for the one-handed class (sword, dagger, axe, mace), the two-handed sword, the two-handed axe and hammer, and the shield
- [x] Shield block: its own clip, a block bonus, the shield's armour rating in the wearer's mitigation, `BAMT` for the block sound
- [x] The player's equipped weapon of any melee type uses its class's clips and timing
- [x] With the setting off, nothing changes (traces compared, see the stage log)
Out of scope: dual wielding, hand to hand clips for unarmed NPCs, bows and crossbows for NPCs by gear (the test weapon still makes archers), enchantments, poison, weapon speed, the player's block input.

## Survey

**What the code does today.** `SkyrimCombat` keeps ONE weapon, `mWeapon` (the record named by `combat test weapon`, `combat.cpp` `ready()`), and `weaponFor(fighter)` (`combat.cpp`) returns the player's equipped melee weapon (task 0184) else `mWeapon`. The clip table is `SlotTable::humanoid()` (seven entries, weapon animation type 1) and every call site passes the constant `kWeaponAnim1HSword` to `clipFor`: `beginAttack`, `runPlayerSwing`, the victim's reaction in `applyHit`, the death clip, the player's timing kick, and `aiRaiseGuard` (`combat_ai_run.cpp`, `SlotTable::guard()`). An NPC's armour is the default outfit only (`wornArmour`, `combat_stats.cpp`), computed in `SkyrimWorldView::fighter` (`world_view.cpp`). The hit numbers (`computeMeleeHit`) already read the weapon record (damage, weight, reach, skill, stagger), so a different `WeaponRecord` gives different damage, stamina and reach without any new formula.

**WEAP animation types (WEAP DNAM +0, VERIFIED values 0 to 9 in the data, names in `WeaponAnimType`).** 1 sword, 2 dagger, 3 axe, 4 mace, 5 two-handed sword, 6 two-handed axe and hammer, 7 bow, 8 staff, 9 crossbow.

**Clip sets settled by the data** (`Skyrim - Animations.bsa` listing, `meshes/actors/character/animations`, and the generators of `meshes/animationdata/defaultmale.txt`; aggregates only, nothing of the game is committed):
- `1hm_*` (106 clips) is the one-handed set. There is **no dagger, axe or mace set**: the only other one-handed attack clips are `dw_*` (dual wield) and `1hmshield*`. So types 1 to 4 share the `1HM_*` clips; they differ in damage, speed and reach (the WEAP numbers).
- `2hm_*` (84 clips) is the two-handed SWORD set and `2hw_*` (67) the two-handed AXE AND HAMMER set (`weapon` = hammer or battleaxe). Both have attack right and left, power attack, block hit, block bash, recoil, stagger and block idle.
- `shd_*` (15 clips): `shd_blockhit`, `shd_blockidle`, `shd_blockbash`, `shd_blockbashpower` for a raised shield. There is no shield attack clip.
- Events: `2HM_AttackRight` has `HitFrame` 0.2 (the generator of that exact name exists); `2HW_AttackRight` and `2HW_AttackLeft` have **no generator of that name**, only `2HW_AttackRightNPC` and `2HW_AttackLeftNPC` (HitFrame 0.8); `Shd_Bash` and `shd_BashPower` are the sidecar names of the `shd_blockbash` and `shd_blockbashpower` files. The sidecars never name the clip file (note 0054, F44 to F53), so `SkyrimClipMeta::makeClipMeta` links a clip to its events by name and duration. A small, tested alias rule was added there: when no generator has the stem's name, the stem with `NPC` appended and then a short table of known sidecar names is tried, always under the same duration check. Without it these clips would play with no `HitFrame` and never hurt anyone.

**Gear of an NPC.** `ActorIndex::resolve` gives the effective inventory (`CNTO`, following templates by the inventory flag) and the default outfit. Items can be leveled lists (`LVLI`); `ActorIndex::pickLeveled` resolves them as the armour already does. `WeaponRecord`s are in `CombatIndex`; an ARMO is a shield when its slot mask has the shield bit (`ArmourSlot::Shield`, slot 39). The ARMO `BAMT` (the material a shield counts as when it blocks) was not read; `ArmorExtra::mBlockMaterial` is new.

## Rules (the decisions)

- **Weapon.** From the inventory entries (count above 0) and the outfit, leveled lists followed (depth at most 4): the melee weapon with the highest base damage; ties go to the lowest form id; types 0 (hand to hand), 7, 8, 9 never qualify. No weapon: the fighter is unarmed as before (the `1HM_*` clips are kept for it, hand to hand clips are out of scope). **The test weapon, when set, overrides every NPC's weapon** (and with it the bow test rules keep working).
- **Shield.** Only with a one-handed weapon or no weapon (a two-handed weapon needs both hands). The ARMO with the shield slot and the highest armour rating, ties to the lowest form id. Its rating counts in the wearer's mitigation through `armourOf` (it is one more piece).
- **Armour.** The outfit's pieces as before, plus inventory armour for the slots the outfit leaves free (highest rating first, no overlap). This is "worn armour" as far as the data says: Skyrim has NPCs with armour in their inventory and no outfit.
- **Clips.** The class of a fighter's main weapon picks attack, power attack, left attack, recoil, stagger and (AI) guard clips. A fighter with a shield uses the shield clips (`SHD_BlockHit`, `SHD_BlockBash`, `SHD_BlockIdle`) for block hit, bash and guard, whatever its weapon class. A victim reacts in the clips of ITS OWN class.
- **Damage, stamina, reach, stagger.** The weapon record decides, through the formulas that already exist (a greatsword of damage 20 and weight 20 costs 20 + 20 stamina, reaches 1.3 x 141 units). Two-handed stamina and reach therefore need no new rule.
- **Shield block.** Block fraction = min(fBlockMax, base + scaling x skill x mult / 100 + 0.01 x shield rating), then the power attack factor as before. The 0.01 is OURS (`kShieldBlockPerRating`; the five masters have no shield GMST), tunable. The block sound uses the shield's `BAMT` material instead of the weapon's.
- **The player.** Its equipped weapon already replaced the test weapon (task 0184). With real gear its clip class follows that weapon (the timing the engine is asked for is the class's attack clip; the timing cache is per class) and a shield among its equipped pieces counts for armour, and is ignored while the weapon is two-handed. The player has no block input, so a shield changes only its armour.
- **Dual wielding: not built.** It needs a left-hand weapon slot, per-hand damage and the `dw_*` clips (10 attack generators, a separate event vocabulary: `weaponLeftSwing`, `AttackWinStartLeft`); that is not cheap and the combat style's `AllowDualWielding` flag is not read yet.
