Design doc section: `docs/research/0056-skyrim-combat-survey.md` sections 4 and 8; decision `docs/decisions/0018-skyrim-combat-hooks.md`; task 0186 (the magic runtime) and its log.
Approval: toddhow chose "Visible spells" on 2026-11-15. Full loop in form (the same hand wrote tests and code; tests first and run red against stubs; no fresh reviewer).
Goal: with `[Game] neutral combat` and a Skyrim pack, the Firebolt is seen in flight, a Ward shows a body model while it is up, and the held aimed spells (Flames, Frostbite, Sparks) and touch spells work as a ray each frame with a drawn stream, with damage per second equal to a hand calculation.
Inherited code touched: none of OpenMW's. Out of scope: particle systems, the effect shader's palette lookup, falloff cone, soft depth fade and its controllers (a stand-in is drawn instead, stated below), the hit effect on a Firebolt's target (its art is a shader, not a model), explosions, the beam's own model (a stream of the projectile's model stands in), a ward that absorbs a beam, area and target delivery, saving.

## Survey (at `ee63db9771`)
- **Where a spell projectile is drawn.** `MWWorld::NeutralProjectiles::apply` (`apps/openmw/mwworld/neutralprojectiles.cpp`) takes the bridge's spawn, move and remove operations and calls `MWRender::Projectiles::add` (`mwrender/projectiles.cpp`, shared with Morrowind), which reads the model with `mContext.readNode`; for a Skyrim SE file that is the scene reader `SkyrimModel::makeSceneReader` (`skyrimmodel.cpp`), which flattens the NIF with `NifScene::Flat::flatten` (`components/nifscene/flatten.cpp`) and builds one draw per material key.
- **Why the Firebolt was invisible (task 0186 could not say).** Found by experiment: with `OPENEDEN_NIFSCENE_TRACE=1` a run of `n5bow` with Hulda casting Firebolt logs no `NIFSCENE` line for `meshes/magic/fireboltprojectile.nif` and `NIFSCENE-SKIPS` shows `effect 3`; `flatten` skips every shape whose shader is a `BSEffectShaderProperty` (`flatten.cpp`, `material.mKind == ShaderKind::Effect`, left out since task 0110 because they need blending and animation), so the reader returns an empty group ("a model that draws nothing is still a model"), no warning, and the projectile renderer draws nothing. The model (`Meshes1.bsa`, 67 blocks) is two `BSTriShape`s with effect shaders (`Cylinder11`, the cone: 208 vertices, `BrightGlowMesh02:1`, a glow card) and one `NiParticleSystem` (`pFireballCore09`); a `NiParticleSystem` is not geometry in a `Nif::Scene` and is never drawn.
- **The effect shader's values** are already decoded (`Nif::Scene::ShaderMaterial`: source texture, greyscale texture, base colour and scale, falloff): measured on the Firebolt, the cone's base colour is grey (0.39) with base alpha 0 and the glow's grey 0.25, the greyscale texture is a gradient (`GradFireCloak01`, `GradFlame01`, 256 x 64) whose rows run from transparent to opaque and whose columns run from dark orange to white; the ward's `GradWardColor` is 512 x 32 and blue. In the game those gradients are looked up per pixel by the source texture's greyscale and animated by controllers.
- **Ward and hit art.** MGEF DATA (152 bytes): +92 casting art and +96 hit effect art are ARTO form ids (VERIFIED on the data: `WardConcSelf0` has `WardInHandFX` and `WardHitEffect`, whose MODL is `Magic\WardBodyFX.nif`; Firebolt's `FireDamageFFAimed` has `Fireball01HandEffects` as casting art, a shader `FireFXShader` as hit shader at +32 (not a model) and an impact data set at +100). `WardBodyFX.nif` has 6 shapes (466 blocks): `Shine04:0`, `WardShield05:1` and `HitFlash:2` are effect shapes and are drawn; `WardEmitterMesh01`, `c_wardShield:0` are hidden; `PArray02` is a particle system.
- **The aimed held spells** (`SPEL` Flames 14 a second, Frostbite, Sparks: concentration, delivery aimed; effects ValueModifier or Dual; PROJ type flame (8), speed 1000, range 768, model `Magic\FireSprayFX01.nif` for Flames; the spray types of Frostbite and Sparks are the same shape) were refused by `fireCast` ("a held spell that is not cast on its caster is not supported").
- **Ray and projectile code.** `NeutralProjectile::step` (`neutralprojectile.cpp`) tests one segment against actor capsules (`segmentCapsule`) and the world (`WorldRay`); a ray is one step of a gravity-free shot with speed = range per second.
- **A wrapper trap found on the way.** `HudWriter` (`game_packs/skyrimse/hud.hpp`) wraps the bridge's writer and forwarded each virtual one by one: a new virtual that it does not forward silently reaches the pack as the refusing default (the first run showed "ward body ... refused" with no sign in the bridge). It now forwards the three new ones, and the class comment says so.

## Design
1. **Core: effect shapes (`NifScene::Flat::Limits::mEffects`, default off).** A `BSEffectShaderProperty` shape is kept: `MaterialKey::mEffect`, `mDiffuse` = the source texture, `mEffectColor` = base colour times its scale (a scale of 0 or a non-finite colour means 1; a base alpha of 0, the start value of an alpha controller, is drawn as 1), `mPalette` = the greyscale texture when `Greyscale_To_PaletteColor` (flags 1 bit 4) is set, the blend from the shape's `NiAlphaProperty` (additive: source alpha, one), double sided from the flag. Refracting shapes stay out. Only models whose path contains `meshes/magic/` are read with it, and only with `[Game] neutral spell effects` (read once, like the other neutral world switches): the model cache is keyed by path, so a model is never both ways in one session, and world models (a torch's flame cards) are unchanged.
2. **Host: drawing an effect shape (`skyrimmodel.cpp`).** Unlit (colour mode 3: vertex colour times texture, alpha included, no fog), blended and not writing depth, depth sorted. The colour is the base colour times the file's vertex colour; with a palette, ONE texel of it replaces the base colour (the lookup and the controllers are not played): the column five eighths along, in the upper three quarters of the rows the texel with the largest chroma times (alpha + 32). On the installed gradients that is orange-yellow for the fire ones and a mid blue for the ward's.
3. **Contract 0.5 (`combat_rules.hpp`, additive).** `ProjectileRequest::visualOnly` (trailing: flown and drawn, hurts nothing, never told to the pack, never sticks); `CombatRequestWriter::castRay(from, RayRequest, RayHit&)` (synchronous, first actor, player or static body along a line; the caster and dead actors are never hit); `showEffect(subject, EffectRequest)` and `hideEffect(token)` (a model kept on a subject every frame, at most 16, a token shown twice replaces); all defaults refuse. No new `CombatRules` virtual. `kCombatRulesVersion` 0.5.
4. **Bridge (`neutralcombatbridge.*`).** `castRay` is one `NeutralProjectile::step`; `showEffect` shares the projectile sink calls (`spawn`, `move`, `remove`) so no sink changed: the renderer's `NeutralProjectiles` draws it. `followEffects` moves every shown effect to its subject after the shots are stepped; an effect whose subject has gone is removed and counted; with no rules everything is removed.
5. **Pack (`combat_magic.cpp`, records).** ARTO decoded (`ArtRecord`, `CombatIndex::art`, `CombatData::artModelById`); MGEF `mCastingArt` and `mHitEffectArt`. With `combat spell beams`: a held spell that is aimed or touch is a *beam*: each update, after the magicka is paid (cost a second, the last tick scaled by the magicka left, as for Healing), the pack aims a ray (the player's from the eye along the view, an actor's at the player with the bow's aim heights), range = the PROJ's range (768 for Flames), casts it and applies the per-second effects to the first actor or player it meets: ValueModifier, Dual (the second value by the MGEF weight) and Absorb, resisted by the victim's resistance as everywhere, damage = magnitude x seconds paid, through `changeVital` (so damage reports, deaths and the HUD are the melee path's). A ward does not stop a beam (the ward only takes instant effects: unchanged rule, decision for toddhow). A touch spell (fire and forget) casts a ray of `kTouchReach` (150, ours) and applies its effects as a thrown spell does. With `combat spell visuals`: a held beam launches a drawn-only copy of its projectile every `kBeamVisualInterval` (0.05 s), flying to the hit point; a ward shows the hit art of its effect (`Magic\WardBodyFX.nif`) at `kWardBodyHeight` (40, ours) while it is held and hides it when it ends (a pending list, hidden in the next update).
6. **Settings.** `[Game] neutral spell effects`, `[extension.skyrimse] combat spell beams`, `combat spell visuals` (extension.json, off). The test profile has `neutral spell effects`; the two pack settings are listed in the profile README with the scenarios.
