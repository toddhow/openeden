# Task: A second, high (cirrus) cloud layer
Design doc section: `docs/tasks/0042-rendering-volumetric-clouds.md` (decision D2: "a second thin high layer (cirrus) as a flat textured sheet"; D4 coverage and wind; D13 weathers that keep the vanilla sky) and `docs/research/0008-modern-rendering-proposal.md` (R6, the sky); the model, its physics and the sources are in `docs/research/0041-cirrus-clouds.md` (read it first; every source is named there, and what could not be read is marked). Builds on task 0034 (the atmosphere), task 0038 (lit clouds), task 0040 (the sun glow) and task 0047 (the procedural sun: the pattern for a Slang-only sky layer).
Goal: draw a thin, fibrous, high-altitude cloud layer above the existing cloud layers, computed from noise in a Slang fragment shader on its own sky dome, that drifts with the wind, follows the weather, is lit by the sun's true height (staying bright after sunset and tinted at dawn and dusk), casts no shadow, and is entirely absent unless a setting is on.

## Facts this is built on (checked at `7bc9c7fcc9`)

- **Two textured layers exist, both children of `Sky::mSwitch`** after the night dome, the sun and the two moons: `mClouds` and `mNextClouds`, each a `Clouds` node (`weather.cpp`, lines 122 to 174 and 629 to 636) over the NIF dome `meshes/sky_clouds_01.nif`, drawn by the `sky/clouds` pipeline (`depthOff`, blend). The NIF is a flat disc, not a hemisphere (`Render::Cloud::domeRadius` 0 to 1000, `domeHeight` 306.7 down to 100): its rim is about 5.7 degrees above the horizon, and the vertex shader fades its lowest rows with the vertex index (`sky/clouds/main.vert`).
- **The sky is camera-relative.** `Sky` is a `vsgUtil::ViewRelative` node (`mNode`), so a dome moves with the eye.
- **A Slang-only sky layer has a precedent.** `Sky::Sky` checks `Graphics::hasSlangShader("sky/sunproc")` and pushes the path on `Graphics::slangOnlyPaths`; `Graphics::createShaderStage` then reads `<path>/main.vert.spv` and `main.frag.spv` and builds a `ShaderStage` with entry `main`. Both stages are Slang, so the "mixed pipeline draws nothing" rule (`files/shaders/CLAUDE.md`) does not arise. The build produces the pair with `openeden_add_slang_shader()` (`files/shaders/CMakeLists.txt`).
- **The existing sky stages carry pinned SPIR-V** (`SlangPermutations` checks every Slang twin of a GLSL stage; `PostPassesSwitchOff` pins the post passes) and `apps/openmw/mwrender/CLAUDE.md` records that new specialization constants in existing shaders blanked every frame on this machine's NVIDIA driver. A new shader pair in a new folder touches none of that.
- **The weather inputs that exist:** `WeatherResult::mCloudTexture`, `mNextCloudTexture`, `mCloudBlendFactor`, `mCloudSpeed`, `mGlareView`, `mIsStorm`, `mFogDepth`, `mNight`, `mNightFade`, `mCloudTint` (`weatherdata.hpp`). The records have **no coverage or cirrus field** (task 0042, D4). `Sky::applyVolumeClouds` already turns the pictures into a coverage (`coverageFromThickness`, `blendCoverage`, built from `mCloudMeanThickness`). **That mean is built only when cloud shadows or the volumetric clouds are on:** `Sky::getOrCreateCloudTexture` fills `mCloudGrids` and `mCloudMeanThickness` inside `if (mCloudShadowsOn || mVolumeCloudsOn)` (`weather.cpp`, about line 1055), so with `cirrus` on and both of those off the lookup returns 0, `coverageFromThickness(0)` is 0.15 and the share would be about 1 in every weather, rain and overcast included. The cirrus must make the condition `mCloudShadowsOn || mVolumeCloudsOn || mCirrusOn` (the grid is 64 by 64 and cheap, `docs/research/0019-cloud-cost.md`). `volumeShareTarget` is given `weather.mFogDepth` there, and the cirrus passes the same value. Also `volumeShareTarget(isStorm, fogDepth)` and `easeShare` give a storm/fog gate eased over 3 s (`cloudvolumemath.hpp`).
- **The wind is summed in double in `Sky::update`** (`windStep(cloudSpeed, dt)` along `windDirection`, wrapped by `wrapWind`) and the vanilla scroll in `mCloudAnimationTimer`; nothing reads a clock.
- **The scene light is the mirrored moon light at night, with positive z** (`atmospheremath.hpp sunElevation(sunPosition, night)`), and `applyAtmosphere` receives a `sunHeight` that, with `[Rendering] sun follows colour tables` (default on), is `Render::Sun::tableElevation(hour, windows)`, not the drawn sun's height. The shader sees neither: it must be given the elevation.
- **Noise with a C++ twin and pinned regression values:** `Render::CloudVolume::hashUint`, `hashCell`, `perlin`, `perlinFbm` (`cloudvolumemath.hpp`; tests `testcloudvolumemath.cpp`).
- **The cloud shadow data is separate:** `cloudgpu.hpp` (binding 13's end), `volumecloudshadow.glsl`/binding 16; `shadow()` in `lib/view/shadow.glsl` is the single place a sun term takes its shadow from.
- **Reflections draw the vanilla layers only** (`components/render/CLAUDE.md`, "Not built": "the clouds seen in water reflections (the reflection pass draws the vanilla layers)"); whether a node added to `mSwitch` also reaches the reflection scene is **verify** (stage c checks a lake frame).

## Why a dome and a shader of its own

Editing `sky/clouds/main.frag` (or its Slang twin) would change SPIR-V that `SlangPermutations` pins and risk the NVIDIA specialization-constant pitfall; the vanilla dome's geometry and fade rows are wrong for a layer that must run to the horizon; and the picture-based path needs a texture that must be authored or generated. A third node in `mSwitch`, built only when the setting is on, drawn by a Slang-only pair on a dome made in code, touches nothing that exists: with the setting off the node is never made and the frames are what they were. (Alternatives considered and rejected in research note 0041 section 4: a textured flowmap layer, a second ray-marched slab, analytic terms in the sky dome.)

## Design

**Settings** (`[Rendering]`, read at start, all off or neutral by default): `cirrus` (bool, false; needs `hdr` and `atmosphere`, like the volumetric clouds, so the elevation model and the linear light exist; with either missing a warning is logged and nothing changes), `cirrus coverage` (0 to 2, default 1: scales the weather's share), `cirrus opacity` (0 to 1.5, default 1: scales the optical depth), `cirrus altitude` (kilometres, 6 to 12, default 9: the layer's height for the geometry and for the sunset limit), `cirrus wind scale` (0 to 20, default 6: multiplies the low layer's wind speed), `cirrus quality` (`low`, `medium`, `high`: 3, 4, 5 octaves and 0, 1, 2 warps), `cirrus debug` (`none`, `density`, `share`, `light`, `footprint`: the debug values replace the colour).

**Geometry and order.** A hemisphere of about 32 by 8 quads reaching 3 degrees below the horizon, built in code (no model file), as a child of `Sky::mSwitch` inserted after `mSecunda` and before `mClouds` and `mNextClouds`, `depthOff`, blended. The sun and moons show through it (it dims the sun slightly, as a real veil does); the low layers cover it. Its pipeline options are `Clouds::pipelineOptions`' with `shader.path = "sky/cirrus"`, registered on `slangOnlyPaths` when `Graphics::hasSlangShader("sky/cirrus")` is true; a build without `slangc` logs a warning and draws no cirrus (as the procedural sun does).

**The layer (`cirrus.frag.slang`).** For the view direction `d` (unit, Z up, from the vertex): `dz <= horizon cut` returns alpha 0. The distance to the shell `t = -R dz + sqrt(R^2 dz^2 + 2 R h + h^2)` (`R` 6,371,000 m, `h` the altitude setting; `Render::Cirrus::shellDistance`), the layer position `p = t * (dx, dy)` in metres, plus the drift `(wx, wy)` the CPU sums, rotated into the wind frame (the low layer's `windDirection` rotated by `veerRadians`, 0.52 rad, a named constant with the reason in a comment: the wind veers with height, research note 0041 section 2) and stretched across the wind by `fibreStretch` (6). Density `d = smoothstep`-remapped `perlinFbm(warp(p), time)` with the octave count of the quality, a warp from the same noise with shear along the wind, a fall-streak elongation term, each octave faded by the pixel's footprint on the layer (`Render::Cirrus::octaveWeight(footprintMeters, wavelengthMeters)`, footprint `alpha t (R + h) / (R dz + t)` (the sphere form; the flat-plane `alpha t^2 / h` overstates it about 2 times at the horizon) with `alpha` the pixel's angle, from `scene.resolution` and the projection), optical depth `tau = tauMax * share * coverageScale * opacity * d` (`tauMax` 0.3), opacity `1 - exp(-tau)`. The pattern repeats after `noisePeriodCells` (128) cells of `cellMeters` (2000): 256 km, more than the view reaches at any altitude above 10 degrees.

**Light.** `colour = base * (ambient + sun)`: `base` as the existing layers' tint (`toEncodedIfView(scene.fogColor) + 0.13`, times `material.diffuse.xyz`, in linear light as `sky/clouds/main.frag` converts it); `ambient` a constant share (`ambientShare` 0.6); `sun = tint * litWeight * phase * sunGain`. `litWeight(e, h)` = 1 for the sun elevation `e >= 0`, else `1 - smoothstep(0, depressionLimit(h), -e)` with `depressionLimit(h) = acos(R / (R + h))` (3.0 degrees at 9 km): the cirrus stays lit after the ground is dark. `tint = sunGlowTint(e)` of `atmospheremath.hpp` (the model's transmittance normalised, softened toward white). `phase` the forward lobe `min(henyeyGreenstein(cosTheta, 0.75) * 4 pi, phaseCap)` against the sun direction in dome space (`phaseCap` 8, set by frames: it must not add a second sun to the glow of task 0040). **The elevation and the weight come from the CPU** in the material (the same device as `Material::setCloudShare`: a field the cirrus shader reads and the other sky shaders do not), not from `scene.lightViewPos`, so the night's mirrored moon light is never taken for a sun; at night `litWeight` is 0 and the layer shows its ambient term only.

**Weather.** A target share `s = (1 - smoothstep(0.55, 0.95, lowCoverage)) * volumeShareTarget(isStorm, mFogDepth)` with `lowCoverage = blendCoverage(coverageFromThickness(meanThickness(current)), coverageFromThickness(meanThickness(next)), mCloudBlendFactor)`, eased to its target over `shareSeconds` (3 s, `easeShare`) in `Sky::update`, with a first value set at once. Expected values (to be checked in stage a against the real pictures): clear 1, cloudy about 0.9, foggy, overcast, rain and thunderstorm near 0, ash, blight, snow and blizzard 0. Optional (stage d): a factor from the game date, `0.4 + 0.6 * hash(day number)`, so some days have more cirrus.

**Drift and time.** The CPU sums `windStep(cloudSpeed * cirrusWindScale, dt)` along the veered direction in double (as `Sky::update` sums the volumetric wind), wraps it by the noise tile, and sends the two floats; the shape evolves by a third noise coordinate `time / morphSeconds` (600 s of game time) wrapped by the period. No wall clock.

**Shadows: none, on purpose.** The layer never enters `shadow()`, the cloud shadow header or the volumetric cloud shadow map: a 0.03 to 0.3 optical depth dims the sun by at most 26 percent and the sun's angular size blurs its shadow over 9 km into a penumbra of about 84 m (research note 0041 section 2). The sun's own colour and strength are not reduced either (a possible later change).

## Constraints

Determinism: the density is a pure function of the layer position, the wind sum, the morph time and the seed (`Render::CloudVolume::noiseSeed` plus a named cirrus offset); no clock, no unseeded randomness; the share eases in game-time steps. Allocation: the dome's buffers and descriptors are made once at construction; the per-frame work is writing the material's fields and two push or uniform values in place (no heap allocation). Threading: everything runs on the main thread (`Sky::update`, `setWeather`), written between frames and read when recording, as the cloud share is. Ownership: `Sky` owns the cirrus node through its `mSwitch` (a `vsgUtil::Composite`/`vsg::ref_ptr`, as the other layers) and the material descriptor by value, with the reason in a comment. Off by default and unchanged when off: with `cirrus = false` no node, pipeline, descriptor or shader file is made or read, `Sky::Sky` takes one setting read more, the SPIR-V of every existing stage is unchanged, and the frames equal the commit before. Units: metres in the pure maths (`Render::Cirrus`), converted at the one boundary the cloud volume already uses (`metersToUnits`); the dome is a unit hemisphere scaled like the other domes.

## Interfaces (pure parts, for tests written from this spec alone)

Header `components/render/cirrusmath.hpp`, namespace `Render::Cirrus`, header-only, floats unless stated; components in place of `*` between two `vsg::vec3` (the project's maths rule). The noise is `Render::CloudVolume::perlinFbm` and its hash (not re-implemented).

```cpp
constexpr double earthRadiusMeters = 6371000.0;
constexpr float minAltitudeKm = 6.f, maxAltitudeKm = 12.f, defaultAltitudeKm = 9.f;
constexpr float tauMax = 0.3f;                 // visible optical depth of the thickest cirrus (Sassen and Cho: thin <= 0.3)
constexpr float fibreStretch = 6.f;            // length over width of the fibres
constexpr float veerRadians = 0.52f;           // the high wind's turn from the low layer's, by height
constexpr float cellMeters = 2000.f;           // one lattice cell of the base octave
constexpr int noisePeriodCells = 128;          // the pattern repeats after 256 km
constexpr float morphSeconds = 600.f;          // game seconds for one unit of the evolution coordinate
constexpr float ambientShare = 0.6f;
constexpr float phaseAnisotropy = 0.75f;       // Henyey-Greenstein g for ice crystals (Fu 1996)
constexpr float phaseCap = 8.f;
constexpr float shareSeconds = 3.f;            // as Render::CloudVolume::shareSeconds
constexpr float horizonCutZ = -0.05f;          // directions below this return at once

float shellDistance(float dirZ, float altitudeMeters);   // -R dz + sqrt(R^2 dz^2 + 2 R h + h^2), in double inside; dz clamped to [-1, 1]; equals h at dz = 1; sqrt(2 R h + h^2) at dz = 0; finite for every dz in [-1, 1] and altitude in [6000, 12000]
vsg::vec2 layerPoint(const vsg::vec3& unitDir, float altitudeMeters);   // shellDistance * (dx, dy)
vsg::vec2 windFrame(const vsg::vec2& p, const vsg::vec2& windDir);       // rotate p so +x is along windDir, then scale the across-wind axis by 1 / fibreStretch
float depressionLimit(float altitudeMeters);             // acos(R / (R + h)) in radians: 2.49 / 3.04 / 3.52 degrees for 6 / 9 / 12 km (tolerance 0.02 degrees)
float litWeight(float sunElevation, float altitudeMeters);   // 1 for elevation >= 0; 1 - smoothstep(0, limit, -elevation) below; 0 at and under -limit; monotone non-decreasing in the elevation
float octaveWeight(float footprintMeters, float wavelengthMeters);   // 1 when the footprint is <= wavelength / 4; 0 when >= wavelength; smooth between; 1 for footprint <= 0
float footprint(float pixelAngleRadians, float dirZ, float altitudeMeters);   // alpha * t * (R + h) / (R * dz + t) with t = shellDistance (the sphere form; equals alpha * h at dz = 1, alpha * (R + h) at dz = 0), clamped to [0, 1e7]; denominator floored at 1
float shareTarget(float lowCoverage, bool isStorm, float landFogDepth);   // (1 - smoothstep(0.55, 0.95, lowCoverage)) * Render::CloudVolume::volumeShareTarget(isStorm, landFogDepth); in [0, 1]; monotone non-increasing in lowCoverage; 0 for a storm
float easedShare(float current, float target, float seconds);   // Render::CloudVolume::easeShare's rule with shareSeconds; reaches the target and does not overshoot
float opticalDepth(float density, float share, float coverageScale, float opacityScale);   // tauMax * clamp(density, 0, 1) * clamp(share, 0, 1) * max(coverageScale, 0) * max(opacityScale, 0)
float opacity(float tau);                                // 1 - exp(-max(tau, 0)); 0 at 0; < 1 always
float forwardPhase(float cosTheta);                      // min(4 pi * henyeyGreenstein(cosTheta, phaseAnisotropy), phaseCap); > 1 forward, < 1 back; never above phaseCap
float density(const vsg::vec2& windFramePoint, float morph, int octaves, int warps, float coverage);   // the fibre field in [0, 1]: warped perlinFbm, remapped by 1 - coverage; deterministic; 0 for coverage 0; octaves clamped to [1, 5], warps to [0, 2]
std::array<float, 2> driftStep(float cloudSpeed, float windScale, float seconds);   // windStep(cloudSpeed * windScale, seconds) along the veered direction; (0, 0) for a non-positive input
bool useCirrus(bool setting, bool hdr, bool atmosphere);   // true only when all three are true
enum class Quality { Low, Medium, High };
std::optional<Quality> parseQuality(std::string_view);   // "low", "medium", "high" (case sensitive), else nullopt
enum class DebugView { None, Density, Share, Light, Footprint };
std::optional<DebugView> parseDebugView(std::string_view);   // "none", "density", "share", "light", "footprint", else nullopt
```

## Risks

1. **Looks wrong, not wrong in numbers.** A procedural layer is judged by eye: fibres too regular, a hard edge at the density threshold, a texture-repeat at 256 km, the wrong colour at dusk. Stage c's frames are the test; the constants are named so they can be tuned without changing the structure.
2. **Aliasing near the horizon** if the footprint fade is wrong (the sphere form of the footprint is first-order for a thin layer); the `footprint` debug view exists for this.
3. **Double sun.** The glow of task 0040 and the cirrus's forward lobe both brighten the region around the sun; `phaseCap` and the frames of the interplay criterion control it.
4. **The elevation source.** The colour-table hour and the drawn sun disagree by design (`sun follows colour tables`); if the cirrus follows the wrong one it glows at the wrong time. Using the atmosphere model's elevation (the same value its colours use) is the choice; stage b records which function supplies it.
5. **Draw order and the sun's occlusion query.** The cirrus is drawn after the sun with depth off; the query counts samples that pass the depth test, which a depth-off draw cannot change (**verify** in a frame with the glare on).
6. **A new `Pipeline::Material` use.** The layer reads fields (`specular.a`, `emissive.a`) the other sky shaders also interpret; the cirrus has its own material instance, so no collision, but the field assignment must be documented next to `setCloudShare`.
7. **Weather share from pictures** is a guess for modded weathers (a mod's picture may have any thickness); the gate by `isStorm` and fog depth keeps the extremes right.

## Decisions for toddhow (recommendation first)

1. **Procedural noise on its own dome** (recommended) against a textured layer with a generated map or a second ray-marched slab. The first is cheapest, needs no asset, and leaves every existing file untouched.
2. **Requirements:** `hdr` and `atmosphere` (recommended), as the volumetric clouds; the alternative is to let it run in the default path, which needs the encoded-light variant of the colour maths and is not proposed.
3. **Wind:** 6 times the low layer's speed, turned 30 degrees (`veerRadians`), which is about twice the low layer's angular drift (research note 0041 section 2). Keep, or make the veer a setting?
4. **Date factor** (some days with more cirrus than others), stage d: recommended yes, it costs nothing and removes the sameness; or a fixed share.
5. **Shadows and sun dimming:** recommended none (physical and invisible); the veil dimming the scene's sun by up to 26 percent is left out, so a thin-cirrus day is as bright on the ground as a clear one. Do you want that effect later?
6. **Water reflections:** left out unless the lake frame shows the cirrus appears for free.

- **Redesigned** the density (`Render::Cirrus::density`, `cirrus.slang`; same signature and inputs, no new setting): a patch field (10.7 km lattice) with clear gaps and clumps that also thicken the fibres; a type field mixing long wide streaks (lattice 8 km by 1 km, octaves down to the old 4 km by 500 m) with short sharp tufts (2 km by 500 m, sharper edge, stronger warp so they hook); a swirl displacement (16 km lattice, up to 1.6 cells) that turns and bends the fibres from place to place; heads and fading tails (a sawtooth along the fibre with a noise-wandering phase, faded out for large footprints); softer edges for long fibres; a faint veil taken from the patch field. Fibres are not evaluated where the gate says the sky is clear (the same value). The shader's field constants are generated from the header (`cirrus/v2/gen_slang.py`, markers in `cirrus.slang`) and read back by tests.
- **Tuned by eye** with a standalone preview of the C++ density (`cirrus/v2/preview.cpp`, an equirectangular camera at 45, 75 and 10 degrees up) and then in the game (`new_h<hour>_<view>.png` in the same folder). Frames viewed at noon, dusk (18.5), dawn (6.5) and night (23), looking up at 45 and 75 degrees and toward the horizon.
- **Twin check:** the `pattern` debug view of the game against the C++ density at the same 1280 x 720 grid: mean absolute difference 0.9 of 255 over the pixels that are pure grey (outside scenery, gamma-corrected), 1.4 percent of them over 10 (scenery edges and the HUD).
- **Tests:** 83 pass in the cirrus file (built standalone: `components-tests` itself does not link while another session's `testcloudvolumemath.cpp` is half edited). The eight pinned values of `RegressionValuesAtFixedPoints` changed with the field (stated in the test). New `RenderCirrusVariety` tests, red first against a stub density (an along-wind sine), green with the real one: the 64 km square has clear and cloudy 8 km blocks (block means 0.02 to 0.48), the fibres' dominant direction varies from block to block (R = 0.85 against above 0.99 for one direction), a small step of the morph coordinate changes the field little and a large one a lot, and the fibres' sharpness differs by a factor of at least 10 between blocks. Shader constants for the new fields are read back.
- **Off means unchanged:** `cirrus = false`, clear noon, two runs of each executable (the commit before and this one) with `OPENEDEN_FIXED_FRAME_TIME` and `--random-seed`: all four frames identical, 0 of 921,600 pixels differ.
- **Cost** (RTX 4070, 1920 x 1080, sky-filling look-up scene, GPU main scene median of 500 frames, three interleaved runs of each executable, a shared machine so noisy): the layer on top of off was about +0.40 ms at medium before (0.38, 0.38, 0.55) and about +0.5 to +0.9 ms now (0.50, 0.70, 0.93); high +0.57 to +0.7 before, +0.64 to +1.06 now. So about 1.5 times the old cost, over the spec's 0.1 ms target (which the old field already exceeded in this worst-case scene). The always-evaluated fields (patch, type, swirl) are three noise lookups; the rest only where the sky is not clear. If it matters: the large fields could be baked into a small texture that follows the camera, or evaluated on a coarser grid.

Variety at other coverage and quality settings was only viewed in the preview.
