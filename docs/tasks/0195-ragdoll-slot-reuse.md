# Task: Ragdoll slot reuse (a settled corpse gives its slot to a new death)
Design doc section: decision 0018 (combat); tasks 0106 (CreativeMotion ragdolls), 0174 (Skyrim death ragdolls), 0192 (many fighters, decision 3)
Goal: when all 8 ragdoll slots are taken and one ragdoll has settled (every body asleep), the new death takes that slot and the settled corpse keeps its final ragdoll pose, frozen.
Inherited code touched: `apps/openmw/mwphysics/ragdollhost.{hpp,cpp}` (`spawnActor`, `removeActor`, `readActor`, `actorStatus`, `endStep`), `ragdolltypes.hpp`, `PhysicsSystem::actorRagdollCount` (`physicssystem.cpp`). No driver is edited.
Status: approved by toddhow 2026-11-15 (task 0192, decision 3: "a settled corpse gives its slot to a new death and keeps its last pose. The cap of 8 stays").

## Survey (file and function, at `5b886a0bf8`)
- `RagdollHost::spawnActor` took the lowest free `ActorSlot` of `mActorSlots` (8, `cMaxActorRagdolls`) and otherwise failed with "all actor ragdoll slots are in use". The handle was `{slot index, slot generation}`; `clearActorSlot` bumped the generation. A slot was held until `removeActor` (the actor left physics, `PhysicsSystem::stopActorRagdoll`) or the kill depth removed it.
- Everyone reads a ragdoll through its handle every frame: Morrowind, `PhysicsRagdollSource::read` (`mwmechanics/character.cpp`) -> `PhysicsSystem::readActorRagdoll` -> `RagdollHost::readActor` (driver `MWRender::ActorRagdollDriver`); Skyrim, `World::updateNeutralRagdolls` -> `forEachNeutralRagdoll` -> `readActorRagdoll` -> `Objects::setNeutralRagdollStates` (`SkyrimRagdoll::Controller`). Both also use `actorRagdollAtRest` (needs `actorStatus(handle).allAsleep`, then `readActor`) to move the corpse to where it lies (`placeCorpseAtRagdollRest`, `restNeutralCorpse`). The handle is held by `PhysicsSystem::mActorRagdolls`, the neutral actor entry and the Morrowind `CharacterController`.
- A frozen pose therefore needs no driver change IF the handle stays valid and `readActor` keeps answering. A handle tied to a slot cannot do that (the slot is reused), so identity must leave the slot.

## Design
- **Entries.** New `ActorEntry` table (`cMaxActorRagdollEntries` = 256, preallocated, in `ragdolltypes.hpp`). A handle is `{entry index, entry generation}`. An entry is either Live (it names a slot that holds Jolt bodies) or Frozen (no bodies; it holds the bone states and the lowest and highest body height). The 8 `ActorSlot`s keep the Jolt data and name their entry. The lowest free entry and the lowest free slot are used (so a removed handle's index is reused, as before).
- **Freezing.** When no slot is free, the host picks the victim: among live slots whose ragdoll is asleep NOW and was recorded settled, the one with the smallest settle step; ties go to the lowest slot index. A live ragdoll (any body awake) is never taken; with no candidate the spawn fails with the old message. The new ragdoll is built first (the body and joint caps have room for 9: 192 bodies against 9 x 16 + the debug ragdoll), then the victim's bone states are read with `readActor` into its entry (`std::array<RagdollBoneState, 32>`, no allocation), its Jolt ragdoll is destroyed (`destroyRagdoll`), the entry becomes Frozen and the slot is handed over. Bodies are asleep, so the stored pose equals what was last drawn (a test pins bit equality).
- **Settle step.** `endStep` counts steps (`mStepCounter`); every `cSettleCheckSteps` (10) it reads each live slot into its preallocated state vector and records the step at which the slot is first seen all asleep (cleared when seen awake again: a corpse a falling ragdoll woke is not settled). No allocation.
- **Frozen entries answer like live ones:** `hasActor` true, `readActor` copies the stored states, `actorStatus` gives present, the body count, the stored lowest and highest, speed 0 and `allAsleep` true (so `actorRagdollAtRest` and the corpse move work unchanged). `removeActor` and the kill depth free a frozen or live entry; the handle goes stale. `actorRagdollCount` counts entries (live and frozen) so `World::update` keeps drawing frozen poses; `liveActorRagdollCount` counts slots in use.
- **How the pose is held per actor kind.** Morrowind: `ActorRagdollDriver` keeps reading `PhysicsRagdollSource::read`, which now returns the frozen states each frame (it writes the same bone transforms every frame, so nothing returns to the clip). Skyrim: `updateNeutralRagdolls` keeps calling `readActorRagdoll` for every neutral entry whose handle is valid. Neither driver is changed. After eviction the corpse has no Jolt bodies: it no longer pushes or blocks anything (a live ragdoll falling onto it passes through it).
- Cap of 8 live ragdolls stays. 256 entries per session is a hard cap (refusal message names it).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Eight settled ragdolls and a ninth death: the ninth gets a ragdoll; the victim is the oldest settled one (smallest settle step, then lowest slot); the victim's handle stays valid and `readActor` returns bit-identical states to the last live read, now and after more steps.
- [ ] A ragdoll with any body awake is never taken; with eight live ones and none settled the spawn is refused exactly as before and nothing changes.
- [ ] A frozen entry holds no Jolt bodies (body count of the world equals 8 live ragdolls), `removeActor` frees it, a stale handle never works.
- [ ] Two runs of the same sequence give identical choices and poses.
- [ ] Per-step calls allocate nothing.
- [ ] In game (Skyrim crowd, `n5many`) every death that finds a settled corpse gets a ragdoll, settled corpses keep their pose, three runs byte-identical; Morrowind `tools/ragdoll_check` unchanged below 8 deaths; Morrowind unchanged by `worldobjects_check` and the fixed Balmora frame.
Out of scope: raising the cap, queueing a refused death for later, driver changes.
