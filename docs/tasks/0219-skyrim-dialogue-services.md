## 1. Survey (citations at commit `360da14ff9`)

- **Recognition (task 0155).** `classifyService` (`game_packs/skyrimse/src/speech.cpp`) names a response from the string table of its fragment's `.pex`: `showbartermenu` to "barter", `showtrainingmenu` to "training", `showgiftmenu` to "gift", `rentroomscript` to "rent a room". `DialogueRuntime::fillTurn` sets `DialogueTurn::service` and `endsConversation`. The engine (`MWWorld::NeutralDialogueSession::choose`, `apps/openmw/mwworld/neutraldialogue.cpp`) opens the barter window for "barter" (task 0189) and logs "service not implemented: ..." for the rest.
- **Training data (VERIFIED on the five masters, `/i/Projects/OpenEden-work/bow6/py3`).** A trainer's class has CLAS DATA byte 4 = skill slot (0 to 17, 255 none) and byte 5 = the level it teaches up to (50, 75, 90 or 100). The class editor ids confirm both bytes: `TrainerSmithingJourneyman` is (4, 50), `TrainerBlockExpert` (3, 75), `TrainerEnchantingMaster` (17, 90), `TrainerMarksmanJourneyman` (2, 50), `TrainerOneHandedMaster` (0, 90), `TrainerSpeechcraftExpert` (11, 75) ... every `Trainer<Skill><Journeyman|Expert|Master>` class has its skill and its rank's cap (50, 75, 90); the slot order is the SDK's 18 skills (One-Handed, Two-Handed, Archery, Block, Smithing, Heavy Armor, Light Armor, Pickpocket, Lockpicking, Sneak, Alchemy, Speech, Alteration, Conjuration, Destruction, Illusion, Restoration, Enchanting). `ClassRecord::mTrainedSkill`/`mMaxTrainingLevel` (`actors.hpp`) already decoded it (task 0118: "RECALLED-VERIFY meaning"; this settles it). `NjadaStonearm` (0x1A6D9, Whiterun's Jorrvaskr) has `TrainerBlockExpert`.
- **Training GMSTs in Skyrim.esm:** `fTrainingMultCost` = 10.0, `iTrainingMasterCost` = 5, `iTrainingExpertCost` = 3 (the last two are not used here), plus the message strings `sInsufficientGoldToTrain`, `sCanNotTrainHigher`, `sCanNotTrainAnymore`. **There is no GMST for the number of trainings per level** (the first guess `iTrainingNumAllowedPerLevel` does not exist).
- **Rent (the script, decompiled from `Skyrim - Misc.bsa` with `housecarl_decompile_script`):** `RentRoomScript extends Actor` has properties `WI` and `Bed` (an ObjectReference). `RentRoom(DialogueGenericScript q)` does: `Bed.SetActorOwner(player's actor base)`; `RegisterForSingleUpdateGameTime(q.RentHours)`; `player.RemoveItem(q.Gold, q.RoomRentalCost.GetValueInt())`; `SetActorValue("Variable09", 1.0)`; `WI.ShowPlayerRoom(self, Bed)`. `ClearRoom()` (called by `OnUpdateGameTime` and `OnDeath`) gives the bed back (`SetActorOwner(innkeeper's base)`) and sets Variable09 to 0. `DialogueGenericScript` (quest `DialogueGeneric` 0x13EB3): property `RentHours = 24.0` (script default), `RoomRentalCost` = global 0x9CC98 (`RoomCost`, FLTV 10.0), `Gold` = Gold001. Hulda's NPC_ (0x13BA3) VMAD attaches `RentRoomScript` with `Bed` = REFR 0x616CA (XOWN, at (-79.7, 241.7, 320) in the Mare). So: **10 gold, 24 game hours, the bed becomes the player's for that time** (all VERIFIED from the data and the decompiled script).
- **Bed checks that must allow it.** Task 0210's `rest::Hooks::mBed` (`skyrimse_game_pack.cpp`, `startRest`) calls a bed owned by someone but the player "owned"; the activation then sends `trespass_bed` (task 0213, `crime_service.cpp::trespassBed`, hook `mPlacementOwner`). Both read the owner from `SkyrimWorldView::ownerOfPlacement`.
- **Gifts.** `Game.ShowGiftMenu`: a transfer menu; the container window of task 0182 already moves items between the player and an owner registered by `InventoryService::open(placement, base, "actor")` (a living NPC registers like a corpse: the contents are the NPC's CNTO and outfit). The follower's "carry" use is just that container (not built separately).

## 2. Design

1. **Pack, `services.*` (pure + the service):** `makeTrainer` (class data to a trainer: slot below 18, maximum above 0), `trainingCost(level, mult)` = round(level x `fTrainingMultCost`), `decideTraining` (not ready, skill at the cap, limit, gold: in that order), `TrainingLedger` (trainings done at which player level: the limit resets at a level up), `RentalBook` (rented bed placements with the game second they end; at most 64; sorted; dropped when ended), `ServicesChunk` (save chunk `services` v1: u32 level, u32 count, u32 rental count, then u32 bed and f64 expiry each; strict), and `ServicesService`, the state machine behind the namespace `skyrimse.services` 1.0 (see `services.hpp` for the function list). Hooks give it the trainer and bed lookups (the world view), the skill level and raise (the progression), gold and payment (the inventory model) and the NPC's inventory owner (the inventory service).
2. **Tags.** With the setting on, `DialogueRuntime` names the three services `open:training`, `open:rent`, `open:gift` (`services::openedServiceTag`), so the engine can tell "the pack builds this window" from "not implemented" without reading a pack setting; barter keeps its own name.
3. **Engine (small):** `decideNeutralService`/`neutralServiceEventFields` (`neutralinventory.*`) and one branch in `NeutralDialogueSession::choose` that sends the event `openeden_skyrimse_service_opened` (service, placement, base, name) and closes the box, exactly as barter does.
4. **Follower inventory ("carry").** The line "I need to trade some things with you" of a follower (Njada in Jorrvaskr) leads to "Yes. Let me show you.", whose fragment calls `Actor.OpenInventory` (22 `TIF__` scripts and 47 scripts in all name it). Task 0155 left such lines as plain text. With the setting on, `services::classifyCarry` names them "carry" (`open:carry`) and `services_global.lua` opens the container window of task 0182 on the NPC's owner (`inv.open(placement, base, 'actor')`), with no state in `ServicesService`. Off, the line is plain text as before (`classifyCarry` is asked only after `setOpenServices(true)`).
5. **Lua:** `services_global.lua` (the clock, the event, the Mutate calls), `services_player.lua` (mode and events), `services_windows.lua` (the window logic, headless-tested). Builtin lines carry `REQUIRES=core.skyrimse.services>=1.0`.
6. **Rules that are OURS (RECALLED-VERIFY, named in `services.hpp`):** five trainings per player level; the price of one training is skill level x 10 (the GMST value; Skyrim's own formula is not in the data); a trainer teaches while the skill is below its class maximum; the rental clock is the game time a global script hands the pack (`set_clock`), twice a second.
7. **Rental and the beds.** `rest::Hooks::mBed` and `crime::Hooks::mPlacementOwner` ask `ServicesService::isRented(placement)`: a rented bed is not owned (rest allows sleeping) and its owner is the player (no trespass).
8. **Training pays after the skill rises:** `ProgressionService::raiseSkill` first (it refuses at a skill's top), then the gold is removed, then the ledger counts.

## Findings and decisions for toddhow

1. **The numbers that are ours** (RECALLED-VERIFY, in `services.hpp`): five trainings per player level (Skyrim.esm has no GMST for it), the price is skill x `fTrainingMultCost` (10.0; the Journeyman, Expert and Master multipliers `iTrainingExpertCost` 3 and `iTrainingMasterCost` 5 are not used), and a trainer teaches while the skill is below its class maximum. Decide whether to use the rank multipliers.
2. **Hulda's rent topic shows "<Global=RoomCost> gold"**: the dialogue text tags are not expanded (an existing limitation of task 0141). The cost the pack charges (10) is the global's value. Expanding `<Global=...>` from the GLOB records is a small change in `dialogue_runtime`; not done here.
3. **With the setting on, 22 follower and trader lines that call `Actor.OpenInventory` now end the conversation and open the container window** (take allowed: it is the follower's own inventory). The same lines stay text with it off.
4. **Gift lines are quest-gated**; only the window and the transfer were shown, with the engine's event sent by the scenario, not a spoken line.
5. **Not reproduced from `RentRoomScript`:** `Variable09` (what it gates is unknown), `WI.ShowPlayerRoom`, the innkeeper's death clearing the room, a rental ending while the player sleeps. The rental clock is the game time that `services_global.lua` passes in twice a second.
6. **Training needs `player progression`** (the skill level and the raise are the progression's); without it the window says "Not ready yet." and nothing is paid.
7. **A harness note, not a product fault:** in the scenarios the first click after a window opened through a script event was swallowed once (the gift window); a `mouse` move a few frames before the press makes it reliable.
