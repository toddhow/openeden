## 2. What was built

- `werewolf.hpp/.cpp`: the clip names and times as constants, `playerBeastProfile()` (the older `playerProfile()` keeps the combo, an existing test pins it), `BeastEnding` (the 1.799 s event timer), `BodyClips` (clips the pack asks the body to play, announced 3 frames after they are queued so the rebuilt body has adopted the token first) and `BodyClipMux` (one token and clip for the combat's swings and the pack's own clips). `SkyrimCellSource::playerBody` composes them (third person plays only the swing token).
- Glue (`skyrimse_game_pack.cpp`): a fresh transformation queues `ww_howl`; feeding queues `ww_specialfeeding`; the clock running out starts `ww_bleedoutstart` and the human body comes back 1.799 s later (`beastEnd`), the form still counts as on meanwhile; the Howl: the three words are unlocked (only those the player did not have), `HowlWerewolfFear` is equipped, the shout he held is given back and the words forgotten at the end (`ShoutState::forget`, new); a form restored from a save takes every Howl word as its own.
- `combat_shout.cpp`: in beast form the player's shout goes `werewolf::kHowlFireSeconds` after it began (the core does not classify `Voice_SpellFire_Event`: an existing test pins that) and the body plays `ww_howlforward` (`SkyrimCombat::playerBeastBodyCount/Clip`).
- `skyrimclipmeta.cpp`: the alias `AttackRightPower_State` for `ww_attackright`.
- Vampirism: `ScriptPort::cure()` (a virtual with a refusing default, so older ports build), `VampirismService::cure()`, `scheduleCure(days)`, the port calls `VampireCure(player)`; the existing poll already notices a cure the game's scripts make (status 0). `vampireRaceOf`/`humanRaceOf` and `SkyrimWorldView::setVampireLook`: the player's body is drawn with the vampire race while he is a vampire (a vampire race the body cannot be made from falls back to his own, so he is never undrawn). Test aid `vampirism test cure = <game hours after the turn>`.
