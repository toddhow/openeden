Goal: with `[Game] neutral combat` and a Skyrim pack, an NPC or the player casts Healing, Firebolt and a Ward: magicka is a real value (costs, regeneration, shown by the HUD's bar), effects tick deterministically and end on time, a Firebolt flies as a projectile and hurts what it hits, a Ward absorbs what hostile spells it is hit by.

## Survey (at `31ecf3a8ff`)
- **Where combat lives.** `game_packs/skyrimse/src/combat.cpp`: `SkyrimCombat::combatUpdate` (the update order: regenerate, sweep results, hits, projectile results, bow events, hostiles, the player's swing and bow), `regenerate` (stamina and health with `regenAmount`), `resolveProjectile` and `launchArrow` (the arrow path this stage reuses: `ProjectileRequest` with model, speed, gravity, range; the bridge steps the shot and tells the pack how it ended), `applyHit` (damage, reaction clip, death, `reportDamage`/`reportDeath`). - **Records.** `perks.cpp` `decodeSpell`/`decodeMagicEffect` (task 0179) read the SPEL type, effects and the MGEF archetype and primary value only; `combat_stats.cpp` `Walker` already collects SPEL and MGEF of every plugin into `CombatIndex` (`spell`, `magicEffect`); `projectile(id)` answers the PROJ. `world_view.cpp` `fighter()`/`playerFighter()` build `FighterProfile` (health and stamina; magicka was not read). `hud.cpp` `CombatHud::update` showed `kHudMagickaStandIn` (100) because "there is no magicka system until spells".
- **Cast animation.** The sidecar `animationdata/defaultmale.txt` has generators `MRh_Release` (event `MRh_SpellFire_Event` at 0.167 playback seconds), `MRh_SelfRelease` (0.367) and the left-hand twins `MLh_*`; the files `meshes/actors/character/animations/mrh_release.hkx` and `mrh_selfrelease.hkx` are in `Skyrim - Animations.bsa` (listing read with houseCARL's BSA lister). `ClipSlot::Cast` existed in the contract since 0.1, with no event kind for the moment a spell goes.

The engine has no spare "cast" action and none was added: with `combat test player spell` set the attack control casts instead of swinging (a left-hand spell, a ready-then-release draw and a shout key are not modelled).

## Layouts settled by the data (`SkyrimMagicRealData`, five masters; `docs/research/0056` 4 had the offsets from memory)
- **SPEL SPIT (36 bytes):** +0 cost (u32), +8 type, +16 cast type (0 constant, 1 fire and forget, 2 concentration), +20 delivery (0 self, 1 touch, 2 aimed, 3 target actor, 4 target location), +24 cast duration, +28 range. Healing: cost 12, concentration, self; FastHealing: 73, fire and forget, self; Firebolt: 41, fire and forget, aimed; WardLesser/Greater/Steadfast: 34/43/28, concentration, self. **The cost of a concentration spell is a cost a second** (Healing 12 a second matches the game's own tooltip).
- **EFIT (12 bytes):** magnitude, area, duration; a concentration spell's duration is 1 and means nothing (it ticks while held).
- **MGEF DATA (152 bytes):** +0 flags (Hostile 1, Recover 2, Detrimental 4: Firebolt's damage effect is 0x10221805), +4 base cost, +16 resist actor value (Firebolt: 41 = fire), +60 secondary weight (Frostbite's Dual effect: 1), +64 archetype, +68 primary value, +72 projectile (resolved), +80 casting type, +84 delivery, +88 secondary value. Archetypes seen: ValueModifier 0 (Healing, Firebolt's damage), Dual 5 (Frostbite 24 + 26, Sparks 24 + 25), Demoralize 7, AccumulateMagnitude 32 (WardConcSelf0: value 63, magnitude 40), Stagger 33, PeakValueModifier 34 (ShieldConcSelf: value 39, magnitude 40). Absorb is 4 (CommonLibSSE-NG; the archetype histogram of note 0056 counts 57 of them, no record was looked at).
- **Effects are gated on the MGEF, not on the SPEL.** Firebolt's Stagger (`PerkImpactStaggerPushFFAimed`, 2 conditions) and Demoralize (`PerkIntenseFlamesConfDownFFAimed`, 7 conditions) are perk effects; the SPEL's own EFIT carries no CTDA for them. The pack cannot evaluate a spell effect's conditions (the condition engine has a perk world, not a spell world), so an effect with any condition is skipped; `combat test spell conditions = true` applies them anyway, which is the way to exercise Stagger and Demoralize in a game. The first version only counted SPEL-level CTDAs and applied both effects: caught by the first in-game run.
- **The Firebolt script.** The damage effect has a VMAD (31 percent of effects do, note 0056). Handled natively as plain fire damage (toddhow, 2026-11-12, decision 3): `MagicEffectRecord::mHasScript` records it, nothing else is done with it.
- Aggregates over every SPEL of the five masters: see the stage log (`SkyrimMagicRealData.aggregatesOfEverySpellAndEffect`, lines `SKYRIMSE-MAGIC`).

## Design
1. **`magic.*` (pure, tests first).** `effectTiming` (concentration: per second; duration 0: instant; a duration on a vital value without the Recover flag: per second; anything else: a status for its duration), `signedMagnitude` (Detrimental gives a negative sign), `resistedMagnitude` (resistance percent, capped at 85), the ward (`wardMaxPower` = Peak magnitude x (fMagicWardPowerMaxBase + fMagicWardPowerMaxMult x skill), `wardCharge`, `wardAbsorb`), and `EffectList` (preallocated 256, ticks and ends in the order effects were added, the last tick of an effect is only what was left of the step).
2. **Records (additive).** `SpellRecord`: cost, cast type, delivery, cast duration, range, and per effect the count of CTDAs. `MagicEffectRecord`: base cost, resist value, secondary weight, projectile, casting type, delivery, secondary value, script mark, CTDA count. `SettingsTable::magicSettings` (fMagicWardPowerMaxBase 1.3, fMagicWardPowerMaxMult 0, fDamagedMagickaRegenDelay 0.5), `CombatIndex::spellByEditorId`/`spellIds`. `CombatData` gained five virtuals with defaults (`spellByEditorId`, `spellById`, `effectById`, `projectileById`, `magicSettings`); `FighterProfile` gained `mMagickaMax` and `mMagickaRegenPercent` (RACE DATA +88: the NPC's from `ActorStats::magicka` and its race, the stand-in player 100 and 3 percent, a Nord's).
3. **Casting (`combat_magic.cpp`).** An NPC casts by playing `MRh_Release` (aimed) or `MRh_SelfRelease` (self) on `ClipSlot::Cast`; the spell goes on the clip's `SpellFire` event (or when the clip completes without it; an interrupted clip lets nothing go and costs nothing; a cast with no event for 3 s gives up). The player has no clip: the spell goes after the time the same clip's sidecar gives (`CombatRequestWriter::clipEventTime`, retried while the sidecar is being read). A fire and forget spell pays its cost when it goes and is refused (traced) when magicka is short; a concentration spell pays cost x dt each update while held and ends when magicka reaches 0, when the key is released, or after the hold of a test cast; the last tick is scaled by the magicka that was left. Self-delivered effects apply at once, an aimed spell launches the PROJ of its first effect that names one (one projectile per spell) through the existing projectile path and applies all its effects to what it hits; a miss does nothing (no explosions).
4. **Effects.** ValueModifier, Peak and Dual change health, magicka or stamina (Dual: the second value by the MGEF's weight); Absorb takes from the target and gives the caster what was really taken; Stagger plays the stagger clip on an NPC; Demoralize is a status with a duration (no flee AI exists; the state shows in `FighterView::mDemoralized`); a value the pack does not track becomes a status for its duration; a hostile effect on another actor is reduced by the target's resistance (the MGEF's resist value, summed from the target's ability spells through `abilityModifier`, the same path as the armour abilities of 0179). Health changes report damage (`reportDamage`, also a heal of the player, as an amount of 0 with `healthAfter`) and death exactly as a melee hit does.
5. **Ward.** The ward spell (a spell with an AccumulateMagnitude effect) is a concentration spell; while it is held `mWardPower` charges at the Accumulate magnitude a second up to Peak x 1.3 and a hostile spell that hits the caster gives up magnitude to it (`wardAbsorb`): fully absorbed means the whole spell is blocked (every later effect skipped), partly absorbed means the rest of the magnitude goes through, effects that are not value changes are stopped while any power is left. The ward drops with the concentration.
6. **Magicka regeneration.** `regenAmount(max, percent, fCombatMagickaRegenRateMult, inCombat)` as for stamina, but not while a spell is held and not within fDamagedMagickaRegenDelay of a spend. Hostile casts and hits put the caster in combat for the usual linger.
7. **Contract 0.4 (additive).** `ClipEventKind::SpellFire = 16`; the core classifies `MRh_SpellFire_Event` and `MLh_SpellFire_Event` (not `Voice_SpellFire_Event`, a shout). No new virtual. The pin test `CombatContract.versionIsADraftZeroOne` (0.3) fails and is NOT edited (see Decisions).
8. **Test settings** (declared in `extension.json`, all off by default): `combat test player spell`, `combat test player cast every` (the first press comes after one period), `combat test player cast hold`, `combat test cast = actor:spell,...`, `combat test cast every` (4), `combat test cast hold` (3), `combat test spell conditions`.
9. **HUD.** `CombatHud::update` shows `FighterView::mMagicka/mMagickaMax` when the player has a maximum above 0 and keeps the stand-in otherwise (the existing HUD tests pin the stand-in with no magicka).
