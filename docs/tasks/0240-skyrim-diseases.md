# Task 0240: Skyrim diseases

Design doc section: `docs/design/game-packs.md`, magic runtime (task 0186), stones and shrines (task 0237), creatures (task 0212)
Goal: a creature's landed blow can infect the player with the disease its race carries; the disease costs him numbers where the runtime supports the effect, is announced and listed, is cured by a Cure Disease potion or a temple shrine, and is saved.
Inherited code touched: none (all in `game_packs/skyrimse`, `files/data`, `tools/`, tests); at commit `fc85d12a97`.
Constraints: behind `[extension.skyrimse] diseases` (default false, declared in `extension.json`); creatures carry diseases only with `[Game] neutral combat` and `[extension.skyrimse] creatures`; the shrine cure needs `papyrus`, the potion cure `inventory`. Off means unchanged: with the setting off no hook is set, and no disease code runs (a wolf scenario with the setting off and with it on at chance 0 give the same damage lines).

Status: built and shown in game on Windows. Written by one hand (tests first, then code), no fresh reviewer, no Linux gate.

## 1. Survey (the five masters; a throw-away Python reader, nothing extracted is kept)

- **VERIFIED: a disease is a SPEL with SPIT +8 (type) = 1.** 16 records: Ataxia, BoneBreakFever, BrainRot, SanguinareVampiris, Rattles, Rockjoint, Witbane, the six `Trap...` copies (same effects, used by trap scripts), `DLC2DiseaseBlackHeartBlight` and `DLC2DiseaseDroops`. The other SPIT types in the data are spell 0, power 2, lesser power 3, ability 4, poison 5, voice 11. All diseases have cast type 0 (constant effect), delivery touch, and effect duration 0: they last until cured. Every disease has one effect (a Dual effect counts as one).
- **VERIFIED: who carries them: RACE ATKD +8.** ATKD is 44 bytes, one per attack of the race: damage multiplier (+0), **an attack weight (+4)**, the attack spell (+8). Races with a disease spell on their attacks: wolf, fox and the wolf spirit (Rockjoint), skeever (Ataxia), chaurus and chaurus reaper and hunter (Rattles), sabre cats (Witbane), hagraven (Brain Rot), the three bears (Bone Break Fever; the snow and black bears also list it in SPLO), the Dragonborn ash hopper (Droops). ATKD +4 is NOT an infection chance: the NordRace, which has no attack spell, carries 0.0 to 1.0 there too, so it is the AI's weight for choosing the attack. Other attack spells in the same list are poisons (spiders, chaurus bites) and abilities of other kinds; the pack takes the FIRST of the race's attack spells that is of type Disease.
- **VERIFIED: the effects.** MGEF DATA +64 archetype and +68 primary actor value: Rockjoint and Droops are Dual (archetype 5) on actor values 96 and 97, Ataxia is Dual on 104 and 103, Bone Break Fever is Peak (34) on 26, Brain Rot Peak on 25, Rattles Peak on 29, Witbane Peak on 28, Sanguinare Vampiris Peak on 24, Black-Heart Blight Peak on 32. All carry the Detrimental flag. Magnitudes: 25 for the Peak and Dual effects, Rattles 2.5, Witbane 0.5, Blight 10, Droops 15. Actor values 96 + n are the "skill modifier" values (VERIFIED by `EnchFortifyOneHandedConstantSelf`, which names 96: they are percent modifiers of the weapon damage, magnitude 13 for the armour base enchantment). Actor values 28 and 29 are the magicka and stamina rates (names of the effects).
- **NOT IN THE DATA: the chance.** No GMST holds an infection chance (the only disease GMSTs are strings: `sMagicTypeDisease`, `sContractedDisease`). The chance is ours (see the decisions).
- **VERIFIED: disease resistance is actor value 45** (the MGEF `AbResistDisease`, DATA +68 = 45): `RaceArgonianResistDisease` 50 (on Argonian and Argonian vampire races), `RaceWoodElf` ability 50 (with poison resistance 50; the race holds it through its ability list), `WerewolfImmunity`, `VampireVampirism` and `AbWerewolf` 100, `DLC2AbWerebear` 100; armour enchantments `EnchArmorResistDisease01/02/Base` 50, 100, 25 (constant enchantments are not applied by the pack).
- **VERIFIED: archetype 3 is Cure Disease.** Three MGEFs: `CureDiseaseEffect` (in the shrine blessing of all nine temple altars and the three Dragonborn altars, with a Fortify effect), `AlchCureDisease` (the potion `CureDisease`, the Garlic Bread food, the werewolf and vampire cures), `dunHallOfTheVigilantCureDiseaseEffect`.
- **SPIT +12 is not a chance either:** it is 0.5 on some diseases and 0 on others (Flames has 0 there too); its meaning was not settled and is not used.

## 2. Design

- **Infection.** `SkyrimCombat` tells a hook, once per landed, unblocked, non-bash blow that a non-player fighter lands on the player (and after which he lives), the spells of the attacker's race attacks (`FighterProfile::mAttackSpells`, filled from `RaceCombatRecord::mAttackSpells`, which `decodeRaceCombat` reads from ATKD +8) and its placement reference. `DiseaseService::onHit` looks for the first Disease spell among them; none: nothing happens. If the player has it, nothing happens (no roll). The player's resistance is the sum of the actor value 45 modifiers of his ability spells (`SkyrimCombat::playerSpells()`: his own, his race's, a stone's); 100 or more is immune (no roll spent). The chance is `kDefaultChance` 0.10 (ours) times (1 - resistance / 100). The roll is `splitmix64(seed + counter)` of a running counter (saved), so a run repeats exactly.
- **Effects (`modifiersOf`).** Signed (detrimental) sums of the diseases' effects, into `PlayerValueModifiers` (`value_modifiers.hpp`), which `SkyrimCombat::setPlayerValueModifiers` applies to the player's profile (`combat_disease.cpp`): stamina and magicka MAXIMUM (actor values 26, 25), stamina and magicka REGENERATION rates (29, 28: the profile's percent of the maximum a second; the same units are RECALLED-VERIFY), and a percent change of the weapon damage of One-handed, Two-handed and Archery (actor values 96 to 98: the damage of the weapon swung is multiplied by 1 + percent / 100, never below 0). A profile value is kept as base + the amount really added (never below 0), so the amount comes off when the modifiers change; the progression sync (`syncProgression`) rewrites the maxima, so it forgets what was applied and asks again.
  **Applied:** Bone Break Fever (stamina maximum -25), Brain Rot (magicka maximum -25), Rattles (stamina regeneration -2.5), Witbane (magicka regeneration -0.5), Rockjoint and Droops (one-handed and two-handed damage -25 and -15 percent). **Carried and described in the log, nothing applied:** Sanguinare Vampiris (health maximum 24: the engine owns the player's health), Black-Heart Blight (carry weight 32), Ataxia (lockpicking and pickpocket modifiers 104 and 103: no consumer scales those).
- **Notification and listing.** `DiseaseService` keeps a ring of 16 notifications ("You have contracted <name>", "You have been cured of <name>") and the list; the namespace `skyrimse.diseases` 1.0 (Read: `count`, `name(n)`, `revision`, `notification_count`, `notification(n)`) feeds `scripts/openeden/skyrimse/diseases_player.lua` (builtin line with `REQUIRES=core.skyrimse.diseases>=1.0`), which shows the notifications with `ui.showMessage` and, with `[GUI] rmlui`, a status line "Diseased: ..." at the bottom left. **No Skyrim window lists active effects here** (the skills window lists skills and perks, the inventory window items), so the status line is the listing.
- **Cures.** (1) A Cure Disease potion: `InventoryService::use` asks the new hooks `mIsCureEffect` (the potion's effect is archetype 3) and `mCureDisease`; a potion that cures is drunk although it restores no health (before: "no instant health effect", kept); a potion that also heals applies the health first and cures only if the heal went through. (2) A shrine: `StonesService::cast` calls the new hook `mCure` for a spell cast on the player that has a Cure Disease effect (all temple blessings). (3) Race resistances reduce the chance (above). The werewolf and vampire cures are not built.
- **Saves.** The chunk `diseases` v1: count (at most 16), the spells, the roll counter; strict (exact length, no duplicates, no zero). After a load the modifiers are sent again by `poll()`.
- **Names.** SPEL FULL is not read (the pack decodes no spell names); the name is the editor id without `Trap`, `DLC2` and `Disease`, split at capitals ("Bone Break Fever", "Black Heart Blight"). The notification text is ours; `sContractedDisease` is a string-table id the pack does not read.
- **Settings** (declared in `extension.json`): `diseases` (bool), `diseases test chance` (float, -1 = the default 0.10; 1.0 infects with every carrier blow), `diseases test infect` (a hex form id: infects once the records are read), `diseases seed` (int, 0 = default).

## 6. Not run, limits and decisions for toddhow

Not run: the Linux gate and GCC (`-Werror` traps checked by hand), clang-format 14 (formatted by hand), clang-tidy, the Lua integration tests, `openmw-tests`, a fresh review, mutation runs of the new code, a potion drunk in game, a re-infection after a cure with the same roll counter, the other carriers in game (skeever, bear, chaurus), armour disease resistance (constant enchantments are not applied), the Dragonborn shrines, the Lua status line without `[GUI] rmlui`.

Limits: the exit frame of a run shows the hex name "Disease 000b877e" for a frame (the library is gone while the pack shuts down); the infection comes only through the melee path (no creature spells or arrows, and `combat test attacker`-style NPC fights never infect, only the player does); a creature's disease is the first disease of its race attack list (the data lists one per race); trap diseases are not delivered (the trap scripts do not run in this runtime).

Decisions: (1) the chance, 10 percent per landed blow times what resistance leaves, is OURS (the data has none; an estimate of the original is RECALLED-VERIFY: please say if you remember a figure); (2) the name is the editor id split into words and the message text is ours (reading SPEL FULL and `sContractedDisease` needs string-table plumbing for spells; worth it if exact names matter); (3) Ataxia, Sanguinare Vampiris and Black-Heart Blight are carried and not applied (nothing scales lockpicking or pickpocket chances, the engine owns the player's health, and no encumbrance consumer exists); (4) the regeneration rates are applied as a subtraction in percent-per-second units (a guess that the rate values and the race fields are one quantity); (5) the status line is the listing, since no Skyrim UI lists active effects; (6) a Cure Disease potion with no health effect is now drunk instead of refused when `diseases` is on (with it off the hook is empty and nothing changed).
