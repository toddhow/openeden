No virtual was added to `CombatRequestWriter`.

## Survey
- **TrueHUD's layout** (from the screenshot toddhow showed; nothing of its art is used): the player's three bars stacked at the bottom left beside an emblem, framed bars with end caps over a dark backing; when a bar drops a pale segment marks the lost part and drains after a delay; a wide boss bar with the name at the top centre; small bars over the heads of engaged actors.
- **What marks a boss in the data.** Searched `Skyrim.esm` for `Boss`: 124 strings, none is a flag. NPC_ has no boss flag, ENCZ has none; bosses are marked only by NAME (`DraugrBoss`, `BanditBoss`, `LCharWarlock*Boss*`, `Dragon_Boss`, `MUSCombatBoss` for the music) and the keyword `ActorTypeDragon` exists once. So the rule is OURS: an actor whose base editor id contains `boss` or starts with `dragon` (case-insensitive) is a boss (`hudBossRule`). The top bar shows the engaged boss with the greatest health maximum in range; with none it falls back to the enemy the combat HUD already tracks (hit last or hitting last), drawn with another class (`style-boss-enemy`).
- **Where an actor's screen position comes from.** Options: (a) a small engine binding that projects; (b) the Lua API's `camera.worldToViewportVector`; (c) the pack gives world positions and Lua projects. **Chosen (c)**: the pack already reads every actor's position through `CombatRequestWriter::subjectState` inside `combatUpdate`, and the script owns the camera (`camera.getViewTransform`, `getFieldOfView`, `ui.screenSize`), so no engine code is needed. `worldToViewportVector` was rejected because a point behind the camera comes out mirrored and it reads video-resolution pixels instead of layout units. Neutral actors are not Lua objects (`isActor()` is false), so `nearby.actors` could not be used anyway.
- **Which actors.** The pack's `SkyrimCombat::forEachFighter` (new, read only) visits every ready actor fighter with `FighterView` (new fields `mLevel`, `mEngaged`: a blow given or taken lately, an engaged AI actor or a test hostile). An actor keeps its bar `kBarLingerSeconds` (4 s) after it stopped being engaged, is dropped when dead, must be within `kBarRange` (3500 units, about 50 m) of the player, nearest first, at most `kMaxActorBars` (8). Allies are followers (`isFollower`) and are drawn green. Magicka and stamina bars of an actor show `kSecondarySeconds` (3 s) after the value last changed by more than `kSecondaryChange` (0.5).
- **Morrowind's pieces.** Task 0188 already hides the MyGUI stat bars, weapon and spell icons, enemy bar and crosshair in a Skyrim combat session. New with `hud style`: Morrowind's sneak icon when the pack's sneak eye is on (`sneak`), and the minimap when the compass is on (`map`). The engine reads the three pack settings with `Settings::Manager::getOrDefault<bool>(key, "extension.skyrimse", false)`. The effects row and the cell name stay (the new HUD does not cover them).
- **Art.** Our RmlUi renderer has no clip mask or filter (decision 0015), so a frame is nested boxes: a dark backing, a pale trail box, the fill box, a 2 px edge above and below and a 4 px cap at each end, all in RCSS-equivalent inline style; the emblem is a bordered square with the level. **No image file was made or checked in**: nothing needed one.

## Design
1. `hudstyle::StyleHud` (pack, `hud_style.*`) gathers once per `combatUpdate` (after the plain HUD's update) the candidates, positions (+150 units above the origin), names (from `CombatHud`), allies, boss flags, secondary-bar timers (per-actor memos, at most 64), and the top bar. `selectBars` and `chooseBoss` are pure.
2. The script package `core.skyrimse.hudstyle` 1.0 (task 0097 bindings; declared only with the setting): `active`, `player` (7 numbers), `boss` (6), `boss_name`, `bars` (13 numbers per bar), `bar_names`.
3. Lua module `files/data/openeden/hud/skyrimstyle.lua` (`create(ui, style, env)`), player script `scripts/openeden/skyrim_hud_style.lua` (listed in `builtin.omwscripts` with `REQUIRES=core.skyrimse.hudstyle>=1.0`). Documents by the rule of note 0045 13.5: the player cluster, the top bar, the crosshair and eight small documents (one per actor slot, because a position change re-lays out a document). A slot keeps its actor while it is wanted. Damage trail: `stepTrail` (pure): a drop holds the pale part `TRAIL_DELAY` 0.5 s, then it drains at `TRAIL_RATE` 0.4 of the bar a second; healing takes it up with the fill. All widths are whole pixels so a still frame makes no protocol call.
4. `replacedHudParts(neutral, combat, rmlui, hudStyle, sneakEye, compass)` (defaults false: every old call is unchanged) gains `mSneak` and `mMinimap`.

## Out of scope
Texture art, per-actor icons (boss crown, ally marker), crosshair target info, a configurable layout, damage numbers, the effects row, the cell name.
